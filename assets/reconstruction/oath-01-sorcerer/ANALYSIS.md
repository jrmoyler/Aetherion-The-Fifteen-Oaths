# Reference analysis and reconstruction checkpoint

## Observed target

Reference-091 is a realistic elderly female sorcerer, labelled 158 cm tall. It contains front, back, side, three-quarter and backpack-removed views, plus face and material closeups. The body is short and broad; the chimney extends above the head and must not be counted as body height. The source is technically readable (1536 × 1024). Suitability is conditional for maximum likeness: the available views support reconstruction, but do not guarantee exact geometry or skin/hair microstructure.

The head has broad cheeks, heavy lower facial folds, an irregular grey-on-dark hairline, loose curled locks and a rear bun. The original runtime render instead has a smooth generic face and a pointed pale hair mass. These are critical identity failures.

The body is layered: folded neck cloth; short outer sleeves over longer under-sleeves; shaped chest bib; harness; waist belt; flared apron; longer black coat with edged side panels; gathered trousers; strapped boots. The apron overlaps the coat; harness straps contact the shoulders and connect to the backpack. Buckles sit on straps, not in empty space. Bracer shells overlap glove cuffs.

The rigid backpack is a rectangular kiln with a framed slotted door, hinges, latch, corner fasteners, lower/upper reinforcing rims and a stepped rectangular chimney. Front, back and side observations agree on this construction. Any proposed round boiler interpretation is rejected for reference-091.

Materials are separate: matte woven black fabric, mottled fired terracotta ceramic, dark leather and low-sheen oxidized iron with exposed worn edges. Exact roughness and metalness cannot be read from RGB art; material parameters remain inferences until relit review. Fine cloth relief must not replace actual folds and seam geometry.

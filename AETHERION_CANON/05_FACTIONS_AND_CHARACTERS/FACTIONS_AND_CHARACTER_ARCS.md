## Character construction

[PROPOSED] Each realm pairs an institutional leader with a civic professional whose problem tests the institution's useful purpose and its limits. The leader has a reason to preserve continuity; the civilian has a material stake in reform and may reject a reform that ignores dependents, jobs or consent. Neither role automatically represents the correct ending. CHARACTER_REGISTRY.json carries motivations, contradictions, stakes, visual/equipment direction, combat role, voice, relationships and branch outcomes.

[PROPOSED] The leaders are deliberately not all concealed villains. Sera Pell faces a valid refusal and uncertain release; Elen Voss openly defends relief funding; Lio Senn disputes emergency command under real danger; Iria Deyn risks paralysis through honest uncertainty; Adra Kest voluntarily bears costs others may leave behind; Jalen Rusk faces incompatible urgent transport duties. Other leaders conceal harm or overreach, but their institutions contain people capable of challenging them.

## Varied climaxes

[PROPOSED] Regional bosses are encounter anchors, not a universal species category or a demand to kill. The Unthawing Custodian is a living archivist; the Debt Leviathan is an ecological guardian; the Perfect Chorus is a willing ensemble; the Unending Orchard is productive invasive symbiosis; the Unmoored Atlas is a migrating living colony; the First Creditor is a pact champion; the Audience of One is an illusionist patron; the Final Schedule is a transport marshal. Other regions feature constructs or infrastructure conflicts where appropriate. Their IDs remain creature-oNN-boss for technical registry consistency, with ontology explicitly recorded by the asset system.

[PROPOSED] A negotiated resolution must still impose a real cost or commitment. Saving habitat may require rerouting food freight; respecting stasis consent may require an expensive transfer; stopping an invasive root network may reduce harvest; protecting local travelers may delay distant relief. Combat can buy time or protect a negotiation, but it cannot prove a political proposition by itself.

## Relationship graph use

[PROPOSED] RELATIONSHIP_GRAPH.json records issue-specific partnerships and disputes. A pair may trade successfully while opposing one another on privacy or land use. Quest scripts must check the relevant agreement and recent actions, not a single global friendship color. No Oath pair is inherently friendly or hostile because their damage elements combine in a conventional fantasy chart.

[PROPOSED] Personal relationships change through disclosed evidence, kept commitments and transition plans. Betrayal requires a documented motive, an intelligible warning and a triggering conflict; it is not selected randomly to create drama. A leader losing office does not make all residents enemies, and a companion refusing a plan does not delete their earlier testimony.

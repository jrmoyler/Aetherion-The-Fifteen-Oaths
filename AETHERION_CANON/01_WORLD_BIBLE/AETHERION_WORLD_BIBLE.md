# Aetherion: The Fifteen Oaths — world bible

Status: mixed authority, explicitly labeled below. Source IDs: SRC-USER-DIRECTIVE, SRC-RECOVERED-LORE, SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL. This is a preproduction canon proposal, not a claim that its world systems or assets exist in the audited castable application.

## The playable promise

[LOCKED; SRC-USER-DIRECTIVE] Every force has a covenant. Every covenant has a price. Aetherion is one seamless interconnected continent with exactly fifteen foundational Oaths and fifteen major realms. Oaths stabilize the damaged Weave while demanding allegiance, organizing civilization and restricting freedom. The central question is whether stability justifies surrendering freedom and which covenants deserve to survive.

[RECOVERED; SRC-RECOVERED-LORE] The original user-authored world establishes freely traversable interlocking realms, reputation-sensitive housing and certain mounts, a hybrid combat direction, multi-Oath practice, dynamic events, life skills and large-scale conflicts. These are recovered conceptual requirements. Specific algorithms, progression values, network architecture and production implementations remain PROPOSED unless separately audited.

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] The player's everyday experience alternates between receiving useful services and discovering the conditions attached to them. A frost library preserves a family's testimony but restricts its release. A foundry makes safer tools while concentrating control over labor. A water temple saves a drought-struck town and later calls in a debt. These are playable institutions with supporters, critics and people too busy surviving to fit either category.

## The world before the player

[LOCKED; SRC-USER-DIRECTIVE] The First Fracture damaged the Weave, the primordial structure holding reality together. The Oaths arose afterward as living covenants between mortals and forces. The world is already functioning when the player arrives.

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] The continent is neither uniformly ruined nor waiting helplessly for one hero. Most residents work, care for others, trade and maintain systems they inherited. Every realm has functioning protections and costly exclusions. The player's exceptional fragment changes negotiation leverage, not the fact that ordinary people understand their own lives and possess expertise the player lacks.

[UNRESOLVED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] The Fracture's ultimate cause is not established by approved source. The story supplies incompatible partial records and evidence of subsequent political choices, but does not invent a secret sixteenth element, sole evil mastermind or definitive theology. Any later cause requires a separate canon decision and review of all ending implications.

## What an Oath is

[LOCKED; SRC-USER-DIRECTIVE] An Oath is at once magical power, political institution, cultural identity, environmental force, progression system, economic and military power, and protection/control mechanism. None can be reduced to a combat damage color.

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Distinguish four layers in every scene: the force itself; the living covenant that shapes access; the institution interpreting its terms; and the people living under that institution. A corrupt leader does not prove the element is evil. A useful covenant does not make its current government legitimate. A player may respect a culture, oppose its rulers and temporarily equip its techniques without those becoming the same decision.

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Civilian access ranges from ordinary shared services to licensed professional practices and deliberately undertaken personal pacts. A farmer can live in a Fire realm without being a pyromancer; a healer can work in a Blood realm without accepting hereditary ownership. Migration, mixed households and cross-realm apprenticeships are ordinary enough to prevent elemental essentialism.

## The inciting event

[LOCKED; SRC-USER-DIRECTIVE] The Archivist of the Rime Archive, the last true Unbound, dies on the steps of Daybreak's great plaza. They name the player successor and entrust a fragment of the original Weave. The fragment permits one soul to hold multiple Oaths and threatens the continent's established order. Major powers seek to control, protect or destroy it.

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] The proposed capital name Aureate Steps and plaza layout are not recovered facts. The player reaches the plaza through an ordinary chosen background, encounters a public emergency, and receives the fragment before any Oath initiation. Sorin Kes, a proposed medic, witnesses the scene without becoming an infallible narrator. Early choices concern immediate protection, evidence and whom to trust; they do not retroactively change whether the locked death happened.

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Resolve the apparent Unbound contradiction by distinguishing an unclaimed beginner from a demonstrated true Unbound. The player begins with no chosen personal Oath and no proven means to sustain multiple covenants. The Archivist is the last known person verified to navigate covenant interfaces without being claimed. Receiving the fragment creates the player's exceptional capacity and succession claim. This is an explicit proposed explanation, not a hidden redefinition of the user's words. Alternate readings remain in CANON_CLAIMS.json until approved.

## The fragment and its limits

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] The original fragment is an interface that can hold distinct covenant signatures in one soul without immediately forcing exclusive allegiance. It does not create unlimited energy, force others to consent, guarantee that every pact is compatible or entitle the player to public office. Custody is politically important because it permits a credible experiment in changing how covenants relate.

[RECOVERED; SRC-RECOVERED-LORE] The earlier user text also describes multi-classing as holding multiple Oath fragments. [UNRESOLVED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] It does not settle whether these are physical shards, ritual tokens or metaphysical signatures. The proposed harmonization uses one original Weave fragment plus derivative covenant tokens earned through personal pacts. Systems must not require additional primordial fragments until that interpretation is approved.

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Knowledge, binding, narrative allegiance and active equipment are separate states. Possessing several agreements does not mean equipping all fifteen powers simultaneously. Narrative scenes check the player's commitments and behavior; combat systems check validated loadout and source-backed castable rules. No ability is mechanically changed merely to make a metaphor neat.

## Covenant costs and civilian agency

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Costs arise from an intelligible benefit: maintenance for shared furnaces, care for preserved records, safe routing for a relay, downstream duty for water, truthful surveys for foundations, accessible warnings for sound, stewardship and consent for growth, disclosure for hazardous research, appeal rights for protection, uncertainty reporting for experiments, bounded confidence for secrecy, renewable consent for sacrifice, honest framing for illusion, maintenance and rest for motion, and safe human override for machinery.

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Institutions may distort those duties into extraction or coercion. The story makes the distortion specific and remediable, while preserving the underlying work someone must still do. Ending a monopoly requires another way to maintain water quality; refusing inherited sacrifice requires a care plan; shutting down surveillance requires a workable warning network. Freedom and stability are both lived needs, never abstract meters with one obviously correct maximum.

## Continent and dependencies

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] A northern highland spine and central-west divide produce wet western forests, glacial headwaters, a temperate interior basin and drier eastern valleys. Southern roads converge on coastal industry and the southwest estuary. No realm is a disconnected elemental island. Floating platforms, reef districts and phasing halls retain stable mainland anchors and ordinary service routes.

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Trade is mutually constitutive: ore needs tools, tools need food and cooling, farms need water and repair, messages need maintained towers, medicine needs testing, and transport needs rest. Each realm has substitutes and local competence but no realm is autarkic. Route disruption changes prices and contracts within recoverable bounds; it does not erase a culture or permanently lock a player out of its story.

## People, belonging and power

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] The proposed core cast includes one institutional leader and one civic counterpoint for every realm, plus the player and Archivist. A small supporting cast adds Dessa Rowan, a Graft creature handler, and Tarin Esh, a Rime-bound travelling courier-historian. Existing civic characters Oda Kesh and Una Brin carry distinct recurring-rival and mentor arcs. These are functional narrative roles, not a claim that only these people matter. Leaders have defensible aims and consequential contradictions. Civic characters can disagree with reform, defend tradition or make mistakes; they are not uniform mouthpieces for the author's preferred politics.

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Realm affiliation is not ancestry, biological destiny, species or moral alignment. Mixed clothing, travel tools, intermarriage, adoption, migration and professional exchange appear in civilian scenes. No elemental culture receives a monopoly on intelligence, kindness, cruelty, courage or technological sophistication.

## Story structure and player agency

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] The opening is fixed; the first initiation is freely chosen after a short common orientation. Regional arcs may be completed in any order. Each offers three institutional settlement directions with distinct costs, and each can be investigated without binding its Oath. The main story advances through evidence categories and cross-realm commitments rather than a required element collection order.

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Restoration, Dominion and Liberation are working ending directions. Restoration preserves coordinated covenants under a negotiated revision, accepting ongoing governance and maintenance burdens. Dominion selects one dominant covenant with emergency coherence and concentrated power, retaining essential minority services but restricting plural authority. Liberation dismantles compulsory existing covenant claims through a staged replacement plan, increasing local freedom and transition risk. None is automatically morally pure or mechanically equivalent.

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Irreversible choices are confined to the player's narrative timeline or a clearly declared party instance. Shared-world services use recoverable event states. The game warns before final commitments, previews consequences in accessible language and preserves a reviewable record; multiplayer persistence never silently converts one player's ending into another player's lost content.

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Recovered artwork is preserved as evidence and reviewed for identity; new civilian spaces, clothing and creatures remain proposals. A contact sheet is not a texture atlas, an illustration is not a model, and a code file is not a measured functioning game system.

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Every new name, date, capital, local institution and detailed mechanic in this bible requires approval. ENTITY_CONTRACT.json is the shared naming source. Dossiers supply rich design direction; registries supply referentially consistent data; validation supplies only the evidence it actually measures. Open decisions remain visible rather than being filled with invented certainty.

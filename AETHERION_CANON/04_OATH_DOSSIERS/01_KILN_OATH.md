## 01. Worldview

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Creation is a duty to leave something usable after the flame. Kiln citizens admire repairers as much as inventors and dispute whether destroying an obsolete institution counts as honest craft.

## 02. Origins

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] After the First Fracture, a proposed compact tied furnace rights to eruption-watch service. The present institution treats controlled expenditure of fuel and effort as proof that power serves a public purpose.

## 03. Political structure

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Workshop assemblies allocate furnace time; a basin council sets evacuation and river-temperature limits. Owners may appeal quotas, but no property title overrides a posted volcanic exclusion zone.

## 04. Leadership

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Mara Venn, proposed elected master of shared furnaces, protects apprentices while concealing how much armament revenue funds safety crews. Her political survival depends on publishing a conversion plan rather than promising painless disarmament.

## 05. NPCs

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Ivo Serr is a cooling-channel engineer whose house would be demolished by the safest spillway. Apprentices, slag sorters, ceramic cooks and displaced quarry workers supply competing firsthand accounts.

## 06. Settlements

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Ashbridge sits on a cooled causeway between cropland and foundries. Its night market repairs imported tools, while farms beyond the bridge negotiate smoke days instead of existing as an industrial backdrop.

## 07. Capital

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Cindervault occupies terraces on an old caldera rim, never an active lava lake. Civic cisterns and outward evacuation roads make its volcanic grandeur physically legible and expose unequal access to safe elevation.

## 08. Geography

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] The western upland stands below the Rime headwaters and above Graft's wet forests. New lava can dam a tributary; survey stakes show why river diversion reaches neighbors far beyond a battle.

## 09. Architecture

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Heavy buttressed shells enclose ventilated work courts, with replaceable ceramic roofs and external cooling galleries. Public buildings display repair seams as civic records rather than hiding all wear behind black spikes.

## 10. Landscape materials

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Use vesicular basalt, rough obsidian, pale refractory brick, oxidized iron and chalk evacuation marks. Fresh glass edges contrast with worn handrails; not every surface glows or burns.

## 11. Flora and fauna

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Heat-tolerant scrub occupies cooled terraces; burrowers disperse mineral salts and nest beyond active vents. Slag-backed burrowers become hostile when dumping blocks nesting channels, creating a sanitation conflict rather than an evil species.

## 12. Climate and hazards

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Wet western winds meet volcanic heat, producing steam fog and ash rain. Eruption warnings combine ground vibration, flag patterns and audible bells; fire immunity never substitutes for oxygen or clear evacuation routes.

## 13. Civilian life

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Shared ovens reduce household fuel costs; shift meals and apprentice adoption networks organize neighborhoods. The covenant asks for maintenance service proportional to furnace access, with civic alternatives for people unable to work heat shifts.

## 14. Military doctrine

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Crews defend roads and quench lines before fortifications. Siege units require screened fuel stores, engineers and retreat lanes, making scorched-earth tactics a failure of logistics and legitimacy even when militarily effective.

## 15. Combat philosophy

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Pressure, commitment and recovery define the combat identity. Fire-based area control can open a route or deny one, but source castables keep their audited behavior and gameplay telegraphs must remain readable through smoke.

## 16. Weapons

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Hooked furnace spears pull dangerous loads; broad heat-shielded hammers express forging weight. Weapons need cold grips, visible replaceable heads and believable carrying methods before ornamental flame channels are added.

## 17. Armor

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Layered ceramics hang over ventilated leather and textile, with light rescue and heavy siege variants. Armor joints must preserve crouching and tool work; metal plates do not sit directly on exposed skin.

## 18. Sorcerers

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Sorcerers serve as thermal accountants and eruption watchers, using ceramic measure staffs and heat-lens visors. Their civic oath forbids falsifying danger readings, creating a real liability when military orders demand secrecy.

## 19. Ninjas and stealth

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Stealth crews travel ash-service tunnels and conceal heat signatures with insulated wraps. They sabotage feed systems rather than silently walking through magma; soot marks and missing maintenance seals give defenders investigative counterplay.

## 20. Creatures and enemies

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Enemies include coerced furnace guards, opportunistic salvage crews and territorial burrowers. Negotiation can separate paid workers from violent commanders; recovered fire VFX are references, not evidence these creatures already exist.

## 21. Bosses

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] The Furnace Witness is a proposed safety automaton repurposed to prioritize military output over evacuations. Its encounter alternates pressure-release and rescue windows; victory preserves its civilian core instead of demanding destruction of public infrastructure.

## 22. Mounts and morphs

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] A kiln-strider chassis crosses stable hot rubble using replaceable foot shoes and crew-carried cooling reserves. A heat-shroud morph is a separate high-risk proposal with readable duration and no permanent terrain immunity implied.

## 23. Professions

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Metallurgy, ceramics, glass repair, survey work, ash filtration and emergency cooking create civilian progression. Quality depends on cooling and material stewardship as well as rare ore, connecting combat salvage to ordinary repair demand.

## 24. Economy

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Kiln exports durable tools and refractory components, importing Fault ore, Tidal cooling expertise and Graft food. Wartime demand can raise workshop income while worsening civilian tool access, so prosperity and public welfare diverge visibly.

## 25. Housing

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Housing clusters around shared cool courts outside hazard corridors. Players can customize kilns and ceramics, but a protected workshop lease cannot block evacuation; relocation after an event preserves ownership and stored belongings.

## 26. Reputation

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Craft standing records fulfilled orders; public trust records safety disclosure and rescue conduct. A master smith who hides a cracked conduit may retain commercial customers while losing civic office and restricted furnace access.

## 27. Alliances

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Fault provides surveyed ore bodies; Tidal engineers keep cooling loops safe; Graft rehabilitates ash beds. These partners can demand better contracts without being disloyal, and none is reducible to an ingredient supplier.

## 28. Rivalries

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Impulse wants reproducible furnace standards that small makers fear will eliminate autonomy. Rime disputes controlled thaw for emergency reservoirs, while downstream Graft villages challenge ash dumping through open testimony.

## 29. Public events

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] A caldera-pressure event asks players to clear vents, move residents and choose a temporary spillway. Combat, engineering and civilian transport contribute separately; failure triggers recoverable road damage and relief work, not erased homes.

## 30. Puzzles

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Cooling puzzles route heat through visibly rated ceramic gates. Players must preserve a working return loop and can reset locally; accessibility uses shapes and pressure labels rather than red-versus-blue discrimination alone.

## 31. Dungeons

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] The Quench Below is a maintenance complex beneath the rim, with flooded galleries, inactive kilns and contested evidence of diverted safety funds. Its exit remains reachable without equipping a water or fire Oath.

## 32. World bosses

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] A migrating ash-fed guardian may obstruct multiple evacuation roads during an eruption. The public encounter rewards redirecting it toward an unoccupied mineral field; lethal resolution is possible but loses its soil-renewal service.

## 33. Environment interactions

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Heat can soften designated seals, power workshops or create temporary steam screens with water. Persistent basalt shaping belongs to authored rebuild states; arbitrary volcano creation and unbounded ignition are forbidden implementation assumptions.

## 34. Core quests

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] The regional quest asks whether surplus furnaces fund weapons, civilian reconstruction or a transparent mixed charter. Each branch changes local contracts and companion trust while leaving independent safety inspections and story travel available.

## 35. Side stories

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Ivo's spillway dispute weighs a family workshop against downstream risk. A second story follows a novice whose failed ceremonial blade is repurposed as a rescue tool, challenging prestige without punishing imperfect craft.

## 36. Multi-Oath conflict

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Fire and Frost patrons dispute when stored heat is waste or reserve; Fire and Nature dispute renewal burns. The fragment can mediate but cannot erase consent, fuel costs or ownership of the land affected.

## 37. Sound

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Piston breaths, hand-hammer call-and-response and changing furnace draught communicate state. Evacuation alarms use a distinct tempo and tactile caption so musical intensity cannot mask an actionable safety signal.

## 38. Visual motifs

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Broken circles repaired with visible staples, open-throat chimneys and outward-spreading buttresses express controlled release. Symbols show an opened crucible rather than a generic flame crest shared with every fire faction.

## 01. Worldview

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Movement matters because consequences arrive somewhere. Road culture honors progress and second chances while confronting the temptation to treat people as schedule errors and to borrow indefinitely from a future expected to repair the present.

## 02. Origins

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Proposed couriers stabilized emergency routes after the Fracture by coordinating motion and local temporal anomalies. Their road covenant became an institution that mistakes faster circulation for universal improvement, even when communities need rest.

## 03. Political structure

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Route communes manage local roads, while a timetable assembly coordinates long-distance service. Rest guarantees and public detours limit central scheduling authority; emergency accelerations require a stated end condition and review.

## 04. Leadership

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Jalen Rusk has reopened dangerous routes and sees delay as preventable harm. They conceal accumulated maintenance debt by compressing inspection windows, risking the very continuity their relentless scheduling is meant to protect.

## 05. NPCs

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Tessa Wren runs a rest station skipped by a new express route and records wear that official clocks overlook. Drivers, mechanics, cooks and disabled travelers show that mobility is more than maximum speed.

## 06. Settlements

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Restmile is a service town with sheltered stops, repair yards and accessible boarding. It argues that pauses are infrastructure rather than inefficiency, particularly for families, animals and crews who cannot live at express tempo.

## 07. Capital

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Wayclock occupies a broad junction of roads, workshops and public timekeeping halls. Fast lanes remain physically separated from homes and markets, with conventional walkable routes that never require a mount or temporal ability.

## 08. Geography

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Southern interior routes follow river terraces toward Impulse's coastal plain and connect Sanguine, Theatre and the Court. Road grades, flood crossings and rest distances matter beneath the region's clockwork spectacle.

## 09. Architecture

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Long low canopies, modular viaducts and exposed service galleries communicate continuous movement. Moving architecture has locked safe states, guarded pinch points and alternate passages for people unable to use kinetic shortcuts.

## 10. Landscape materials

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Recovered dark steel, brass and gears anchor the industrial language. Proposed worn stone, canvas and ceramic road markers distinguish travel infrastructure from Impulse's heavier factory massing and piston-driven surfaces.

## 11. Flora and fauna

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Steppe longrunners migrate along seasonal forage routes that highways can sever. Road crews maintain wildlife crossings, making faster transport answerable to ecological continuity rather than automatically superior to older paths.

## 12. Climate and hazards

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Seasonal drought, flash flooding and local time stutters constrain travel. Temporal anomalies have marked boundaries and consistent entry/exit behavior; ordinary weather and fatigue continue to matter outside them.

## 13. Civilian life

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Communal meals follow arrivals rather than one universal clock, and public rest is a respected contribution to safety. Covenant duty includes returning borrowed motion through maintenance, not submitting every hour of life to a timetable.

## 14. Military doctrine

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Forces defend route continuity, repair capacity and safe retreat. Rapid redeployment has supply and recovery needs; temporal tactics cannot retroactively erase civilian harm or make an unannounced attack impossible to anticipate.

## 15. Combat philosophy

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Time and motion combat uses legible acceleration, anticipation and recovery windows. Rewind fantasies are local authored mechanics only; no global rollback of other players, purchases, consent or completed shared-world decisions is implied.

## 16. Weapons

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Balanced sabers, flywheel staffs and road hooks express controlled momentum. Moving mechanisms need clear guards and safe carry states, with attack readability based on animation and telegraphing rather than invisible time manipulation.

## 17. Armor

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Streamlined dark plates over flexible travel textiles keep joints clear for riding and repair. Brass indicators show mechanical state, while couriers and road workers retain practical storage and weather protection.

## 18. Sorcerers

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Sorcerers calibrate local clocks and inspect temporal debt zones. Their civic duty is to record whose time was borrowed and which costs remain, making honest accounting essential to any promise of a second chance.

## 19. Ninjas and stealth

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Stealth couriers exploit schedule gaps, service routes and delayed signals. Their tools cannot delete timestamps from all authoritative records; defenders use independent clocks, witness accounts and physical wear to detect tampering.

## 20. Creatures and enemies

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Enemies include exploitative express operators, looping patrol constructs and stranded creatures displaced by roadworks. A person repeating a moment is not automatically an enemy, and rescue can be the correct response.

## 21. Bosses

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] The Final Schedule is a living transport marshal commanding a relief convoy through a failing temporal corridor. Stopping to rescue trapped travelers risks losing urgent supplies elsewhere; pressing on abandons people nearby. The encounter can become a nonlethal confrontation, but its resolution requires a costly redistribution of time, transport and care.

## 22. Mounts and morphs

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Flywheel road mounts travel inspected lanes and require braking, turning and rest constraints. A motion-blur morph is a proposed short traversal mode with clear endpoints and no universal wall, collision or combat immunity bypass.

## 23. Professions

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Vehicle craft, road maintenance, clockmaking, logistics, hospitality and animal rest care support civilian progression. Reliability and humane pacing are distinct from raw speed, giving slower routes an economic and social role.

## 24. Economy

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Escapement exports transport and precision motion components while importing fuel, food and structural materials. Express profits can starve local stops, so route subsidies and maintenance debt shape the regional economy visibly.

## 25. Housing

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Homes sit behind traffic buffers and offer workshops, stables and quiet courtyards. Time-themed decoration cannot accelerate another player's upkeep, alter real schedules or punish offline residents through fictional aging.

## 26. Reputation

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Route standing measures reliable delivery; traveler trust measures safe pacing and access. Beating a deadline by skipping rest may earn commercial praise while damaging trust and increasing visible repair costs.

## 27. Alliances

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Impulse builds drive components, Theatre designs training simulations and Tidal links road freight to ports. Shared schedules must respect each partner's physical constraints rather than treating all delay as irrational resistance.

## 28. Rivalries

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Rime disputes what should remain unchanged, Fault demands careful structural review and Graft protects migration corridors. Road leaders also confront communities harmed by bypasses, so progress has local as well as continental stakeholders.

## 29. Public events

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] A corridor-loop event traps travelers in a repeated route segment. Players establish stable exits, deliver supplies and repair timing anchors; failure prolongs a bounded event state but never traps a player indefinitely or rolls back their inventory.

## 30. Puzzles

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Timing puzzles provide adjustable pace, clear previews and non-reflex alternatives. Local resets preserve learned information and do not erase unrelated quest progress, making experimentation safe and consequences understandable.

## 31. Dungeons

## 32. World bosses

## 01. Worldview

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Some truths need shelter before they can be spoken. Court culture protects privacy and refuge while confronting the danger that secrecy lets powerful people erase obligations, victims and even the evidence that anyone has been harmed.

## 02. Origins

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Proposed courts hid threatened witnesses during the Fracture and preserved routes outside hostile jurisdictions. Emergency concealment became a permanent institution whose missing records now shield both the vulnerable and those who prey on them.

## 03. Political structure

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Veiled benches separate protective custody from adjudication, with independent witnesses holding partial records. No single office should control identity, testimony and enforcement, but fragmentation also makes accountability slow and incomplete.

## 04. Leadership

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Veya Noll has saved persecuted people by making them untraceable. They also concealed an ally's wrongdoing to protect the refuge network, forcing a choice between exposing vulnerable routes and allowing impunity.

## 05. NPCs

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Rell An ferries people across a ford whose legal existence is disputed and keeps practical records in ordinary objects. Refuge hosts, restorers and clerks show that privacy is sustained by work and trust, not merely stylish disappearance.

## 06. Settlements

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Witness Ford is a stable settlement outside the most severe phasing zones. It provides public services without demanding full disclosure of residents' histories, while maintaining bounded records needed for safety and mutual accountability.

## 07. Capital

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Absent Seat occupies a mainland valley with a persistent public footprint and intermittently concealed civic wings. The city does not vanish wholesale from shared navigation; safe roads and accessible service anchors remain consistently available.

## 08. Geography

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Eastern karst valleys receive headland runoff and connect the Theatre, Observatory, Escapement and Impulse. Caves and fog create concealment naturally, while void passages are bounded anomalies layered over credible drainage and terrain.

## 09. Architecture

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Partially screened courtyards, offset doorways and visibly repaired ruined halls control sightlines. Phasing chambers have stable thresholds and fallback exits, preventing impossible architecture from becoming arbitrary player entrapment.

## 10. Landscape materials

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Recovered black, grey, pale silver and deep-red tassels anchor surfaces. Smoked glass, worn paper and damp limestone are proposed civic additions; missing pieces and protected sightlines matter more than universal darkness.

## 11. Flora and fauna

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Dusk-window stalkers hunt along patchy light boundaries and avoid crowded refuges. Habitat corridors remain physical even when an Oath conceals them, allowing ecological study without claiming every shadow is another dimension.

## 12. Climate and hazards

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Sea fog, cave damp and intermittent void pockets reduce visibility. Hazard boundaries use contour cues and spatial audio alternatives; concealment cannot remove subtitles, exits or critical combat warnings.

## 13. Civilian life

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Residents exchange practical favors through trusted intermediaries and may use chosen public identities. Covenant duty is to protect entrusted information within agreed limits, never to grant officials unrestricted permission to alter memory.

## 14. Military doctrine

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Defenders escort witnesses, deny hostile observation and preserve escape routes. Sabotage targets coercive infrastructure, while erasing civilian records or attacking unknowing intermediaries carries serious internal and external consequences.

## 15. Combat philosophy

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Shadow combat favors positioning, concealment windows and deliberate exposure. Stealth has observable counterplay and bounded duration; memory lore never authorizes changing another player's actual chat history, controls or persistent identity.

## 16. Weapons

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Slim guarded blades, shuttered lanterns and compact line tools fit close passages. Weapon silhouettes remain visible against dark clothing, with readable sheathing and no assumption that black-on-black rendering is adequate stealth design.

## 17. Armor

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Layered matte cloth and small silver-edged plates reduce snagging while preserving joint protection. Red ties carry voluntary witness marks, distinguishing civic custodians from armed infiltrators without revealing protected identities.

## 18. Sorcerers

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Sorcerers maintain concealment boundaries and recover damaged recollections with explicit consent. Their code records what was changed, who authorized it and what uncertainty remains, so magical privacy cannot excuse unreviewable power.

## 19. Ninjas and stealth

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Stealth specialists are central but not socially superior to clerks or refuge workers. They use known sightline breaks, credentials and protected routes; detection tools reveal actionable traces without globally defeating all Court abilities.

## 20. Creatures and enemies

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Enemies include memory extortionists, abandoned bailiff constructs and violent claimants hunting refugees. A concealed person is not automatically an enemy, and investigation must distinguish protection from deception used to evade responsibility.

## 21. Bosses

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] The Nameless Bailiff enforces judgments whose subjects and evidence were erased. Players reconstruct enough accountable procedure to stop its pursuit without publishing the identities of every person the refuge network protects.

## 22. Mounts and morphs

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Shadow-trace coursers use mapped twilight routes and retain physical anatomy and care needs. A void-step morph is a separate traversal proposal with verified destinations, visible reentry and no access through arbitrary locked private spaces.

## 23. Professions

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Restoration, discreet transport, privacy-preserving records, legal advocacy and low-light craftsmanship sustain civilian life. Valuable work often consists of maintaining trustworthy boundaries rather than collecting secrets for leverage.

## 24. Economy

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] The Court exports protected escrow, confidential mediation and refuge logistics while importing ordinary food and tools. Confidentiality fees risk pricing poor residents out of safety, making public funding and narrow eligibility rules consequential.

## 25. Housing

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Homes use layered thresholds, private courtyards and consent-based guest visibility settings. Concealment is cosmetic or instance-scoped for housing and cannot hide hazardous structures, evade moderation or expose other players' private information.

## 26. Reputation

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Custodial standing measures kept confidences; public trust measures accountable use of secrecy. Publishing a true fact can still harm protected people, while withholding it can shield abuse, so neither score substitutes for judgment.

## 27. Alliances

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Reed supports anonymous testimony, Tidal offers discreet transport and Rime preserves sealed evidence. Partners demand different safeguards, and the story lets them negotiate limited disclosure without demanding total trust or total exposure.

## 28. Rivalries

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Daybreak presses for public accountability, Storm retains message traces and Sanguine claims ancestral records. Each can protect real interests while overreaching; the Court's own secrecy remains equally open to critique.

## 29. Public events

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] A phasing-failure event exposes refuge routes while destabilizing public buildings. Players escort residents, restore stable thresholds and choose what emergency information to share, with no random deletion of NPCs or player services.

## 30. Puzzles

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Absence puzzles compare inventories, witness gaps and persistent physical traces. Solutions acknowledge uncertainty and provide recap tools; the game cannot require players to remember a fact it never actually presented.

## 31. Dungeons

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] The Unfiled Palace contains protected testimony, erased obligations and stable service tunnels beneath shifting ceremonial rooms. Players separate evidence needed for justice from identities unnecessary to publish, rather than solving everything through total revelation.

## 32. World bosses

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] A void-touched guardian repeats a pursuit order with no surviving target description. The public encounter anchors safe routes and reconstructs a bounded stand-down condition, preserving mystery without making mechanics arbitrary.

## 33. Environment interactions

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Shadow conceals tagged objects, reveals absence traces and enables authored passage shortcuts. It cannot permanently remove shared NPCs, cancel ownership records or erase critical quest evidence from other players' timelines.

## 34. Core quests

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] The regional quest chooses independent sealed oversight, distributed witness custody or public judgments with protected identities. All branches preserve refuge and pursue wrongdoing, but differ in speed, leakage risk and institutional trust.

## 35. Side stories

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Rell's disputed ford case asks how to prove a service exists without exposing its passengers. Another follows a person who voluntarily changed identity and does not want their former life restored for someone else's closure.

## 36. Multi-Oath conflict

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Shadow plus Light can protect testimony while exposing abuse; Shadow plus Time complicates records without making consequences disappear. Multi-Oath use requires purpose-limited disclosure and consent for personal memory interventions.

## 37. Sound

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Close cloth movement, interrupted echoes and distant ordinary city life create selective absence. Critical threats retain accessible cues; silence never means players lose information needed to act safely.

## 38. Visual motifs

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Offset frames, preserved gaps and witness knots distinguish protective absence from generic darkness. The emblem is an empty central seat with two external witness marks, not a skull or black sun.

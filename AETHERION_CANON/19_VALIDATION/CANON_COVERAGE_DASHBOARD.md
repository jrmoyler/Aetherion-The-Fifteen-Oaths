## Read the cells, not just the totals

[Machine-readable 390-cell dashboard](CANON_COVERAGE_DASHBOARD.json) supplies criterion-level document paths, reference IDs, family requirement counts and explicit unknowns. [Interactive-free accessible table](CANON_COVERAGE_DASHBOARD.html) exposes the same fields.

## Gates shared by every realm

- Founder review of new lore, geography, leaders, chronology, abilities-to-world extensions and consequences.
- Individual reference concept acceptance and resolution of conflicting variants.
- Approved derivative meshes, rigs, clips, audio, shaders and LOD/collision specifications.
- GPU/source-equivalence testing, real target interactions and target-device measurements.
- Production networking/persistence/streaming implementation; schemas and design contracts do not constitute these systems.
- Rights review of third-party assets and dependencies.

See INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md for semantic findings and their resolution; see CANON_VALIDATION_REPORT.json for automated PASS/FAIL/SKIPPED/HUMAN_REVIEW checks.

# World map art direction

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] The map should first read as one lived continent with mountain systems, rivers, roads and settlements, then reveal fifteen covenant administrations. Avoid fifteen equally sized colored wedges or disconnected floating biomes. Exact silhouettes and boundaries remain subject to terrain and narrative approval.

## Information hierarchy

[PROPOSED] At continent scale show coastline, elevation bands, the northern spine, central-west divide, four drainage networks and capital anchors. At regional scale add passes, flood refuges, civilian settlements, contested freight nodes and ecological corridors. At local scale show architecture, public services and authored hazard volumes. Faction fill must never hide rivers, roads or terrain accessibility.

[PROPOSED] Use muted terrain colors and differentiated line patterns for political borders. Each Oath may contribute a small emblem and material-inspired accent; color alone cannot identify it. A legend must explicitly distinguish land adjacency, road, river direction, optional fast transit, protected relief corridor and conceptual rather than surveyed location.

## Composition and landmarks

[PROPOSED] Place the high northern band across the upper map, volcanic west and wet forest below it, central civic basin in the middle, drier mirrored and karst valleys east, and southern transport terraces feeding the industrial coast. Tidal's estuary remains attached at the southwest. Coastline drawing must preserve enough land width around major routes that no realm accidentally reads as an island.

[PROPOSED] Landmarks use distinct structural silhouettes: open crucible chimneys; stacked archive brackets; branching relay spans; open channel terraces; load-bearing split arches; tensioned cliff stages; grafted canopy ribs; compartmented glasshouses; open radiant colonnades; balanced orbital instruments; screened absent courtyards; linked kin halls; reversible stage frames; interrupted road wheels; and braced pistons. These are proposals to reconcile with recovered art, not replacement approved emblems.

## Deliverables and review

[PROPOSED] Concept pass: coast and relief thumbnail, drainage overlay, road and service overlay, political overlay and transition callouts. Layout pass: readable capital anchors, consistent adjacency and no disconnected essential routes. Production pass: approved scale, collision and traversal plans, streaming regions and authored event-state overlays. A polished illustration alone passes none of the production gates.

[PROPOSED] WORLD_SCHEMATIC.svg is an original code-generated conceptual diagram with no recovered art content. WORLD_GRAPH.mmd and WATERSHEDS.mmd expose the topology independently of visual placement. Do not treat their node coordinates as terrain coordinates or use a schematic river crossing to infer a physical confluence not described in WORLD_GRAPH.json.

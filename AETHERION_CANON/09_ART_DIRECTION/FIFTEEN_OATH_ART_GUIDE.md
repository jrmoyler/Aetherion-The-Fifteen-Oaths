# Fifteen-Oath art direction

Status: observed reference traits are RECOVERED evidence, with individual final-art approval UNRESOLVED. Motion, sound, modular production rules and extensions are PROPOSED. Sources: SRC-USER-DIRECTIVE, SRC-ASSET-PROPOSAL and per-Oath REF IDs.

The existing reference designs are the starting point. A future explicitly approved artifact takes priority within its exact scope. This guide neither replaces an approved design nor treats a continuation message as approval. No same armor across fifteen recolors.

## Common language and variation

Aetherion shares a coherent world scale and authoring convention. Faction recognition comes from volume distribution, construction, materials, equipment purpose, motion, architecture and audio. Reuse technical joints, export tooling and suitable base rigs; retain distinct outer silhouettes and functions. Civilian equipment must be useful and repairable, not a combat costume on every resident. Body shape, skin tone, age and gender are not faction restrictions.

Keep palette recognition redundant with symbol/shape/gesture. Preserve neutral value contrast around dangerous footprints. Test under day/night, fog, wetness, dense VFX and color-vision alternatives. No blend of Oaths grants a universal color-coded elemental advantage.

## Material and lighting rules

Calibrate source materials under the same lighting before comparison. A painted swatch is a target, not a material export. Exposed glass, water, metal, ice and emissive effects have independent costs. Do not use bloom to conceal missing surface definition. Wear follows use, climate and repairs. Source model licenses and commercial provenance remain gate DEC-016.

## Differentiation review

CROSS_OATH_COLLISION_AUDIT.json contains all105 unordered Oath pairs, with high-priority risks and explicit discrimination tests. Every pair remains NOT_TESTED_IN_ENGINE. The most important pair is Tidal Covenant versus Tidal Observatory: flowing shell/crest/reservoir identity versus dense crescent/ballast/orbital identity. Their shared word does not merge their symbols, materials, gameplay or territorial identities.

Do not infer a final faction-wide palette from a single image. The Folded Theatre character turnarounds carry plum/mauve while its overview/construction carry ivory/navy/gold. Escapement and other character variants also differ from overview silhouettes. Preserve each source and decide the scope of variants before enforcing a production-wide standard.

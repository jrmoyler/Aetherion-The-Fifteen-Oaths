The authoritative machine manifest is [SOURCE_TRACEABILITY.json](SOURCE_TRACEABILITY.json). Image identity and recovery evidence are in [SOURCE_INVENTORY.json](SOURCE_INVENTORY.json); individual images use REF-000 through REF-113 as evidence aliases and reference-000 through reference-113 as stable artifact IDs.

| Evidence | Establishes | Does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| SRC-USER-DIRECTIVE | Locked fifteen Oaths, inciting incident, themes, required scope, destination write/source read-only authority | Approval of newly authored cities, leaders, numerical budgets, architecture or balance |
| SRC-RECOVERED-LORE | Prior user-authored foundational story | Arbitrary additions from assistant-generated suggestions |

| SRC-AETHERION-EMPTY | Destination was empty at discovery | Destination remains empty after bootstrap or migration |
| REF-000…REF-113 | Specific recovered original file, byte hash, visual inspection and origin | All printed values correct or identical to executable source |
| SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL | Authorship of new world/geography/narrative proposals | Founder approval |

## Field-level precedence

A mixed record identifies locked and proposed fields explicitly. The record's PROPOSED envelope must not be read as renaming its referenced locked Oath; conversely, a locked Oath reference does not approve every proposed field in that record. Source catalog/preset records are kept verbatim in the castable registry, separately from proposed Aetherion mappings. Display names may change only through approval; IDs remain stable.

When a source conflicts, preserve both, identify exact versions, record the discrepancy and request the narrow decision needed. A corrected assistant-created sheet is a supersession candidate until user approval is established. History records may accurately quote an older castable count without overriding the current source audit.

## Team access and publication scope

The published original-reference paths are the team's reproducible visual inputs. ChatGPT conversation and Library-origin links may require the owner account and are retained for provenance. Private raw search responses and unrelated Library files are excluded. No claim of broader access follows from a recovered metadata record.

## Evidence that remains to be collected

- Individual final-art approval and resolution of competing construction/silhouette variants.
- Full GPU visual parity and source-equivalent hit/animation/summon control evidence.
- Nominated target-device measurements and production network/save implementation tests.
- Commercial redistribution clearance for every relevant third-party binary/animation/texture.
- Founder acceptance of proposed chronology, geography, characters, ending rules and systems.

Each missing item is an approval, test or rights gate, not a license to invent a passed result. The validation report separates PASS, FAIL, SKIPPED and HUMAN_REVIEW.

# Aetherion: The Fifteen Oaths — foundation overview

**Every force has a covenant. Every covenant has a price.**

This is a production foundation with explicit evidence and approval gates. It is not a declaration that a continent-scale game, 3D asset library or multiplayer simulation has been completed. Founder-locked premises are authoritative now; recovered source behavior is authoritative about the existing sandbox; proposed world and systems specifications are candidates for approval.

## The playable promise

The continent survives through fifteen living covenants created after the First Fracture damaged the Weave. Those institutions feed cities, keep waterways and roads usable, preserve knowledge and protect citizens. Their protection also limits freedom. The player begins Unbound; the Archivist of the Rime Archive dies on Daybreak's plaza steps and entrusts the player with a fragment of the original Weave. One soul can now hold multiple Oaths. The exceptional power is valuable because it challenges who may control stabilizing infrastructure, not simply because it permits more spells.

These statements are **LOCKED**, traced to SRC-USER-DIRECTIVE and corroborated by SRC-RECOVERED-LORE. The meaning of “last true Unbound” alongside an initially Unbound player needs an approved interpretive distinction (DEC-001). No new explanation silently overrides either phrase.

## Creative decisions the foundation must protect

- Exactly fifteen named Oaths, all on one continuous continent. The Water-based Tidal Covenant and Cosmic/Gravity-based Tidal Observatory have separate materials, institutions, symbols and mechanics. Their shared word receives a proposed linguistic explanation, not a merger.
- Each realm is a civilization: civilians, professions, maintenance obligations, institutions and shortages precede the player's arrival. No faction is uniformly good or evil.
- Narrative allegiance, known Oaths, bound Oaths, active affinities and equipped abilities are separate concepts. Proposed slot limits are testable defaults, not founder canon.
- Restoration, Dominion and Liberation have benefits and irreversible costs. None is a morality score or an automatically superior ending.
- Existing reference designs take precedence over new visual extrapolation. Sharing those images publicly does not certify final concept approval, correct model construction, animation timing or production readiness.

## From theme to systems — PROPOSED

A useful covenant choice offers a service, asks an obligation, and makes the tradeoff legible before commitment. A flood-control ward might protect a settlement while restricting upstream use. An archive preserves endangered memory while limiting access. A transport monopoly moves supplies quickly while determining whose village receives a relay. These are design patterns to implement with explicit authored states, permissions and recoveries, not unbounded environmental simulation.

Interaction rules operate on authorized material/context states. They do not grant a universal elemental damage hierarchy. Server order, bounded chains, encounter crowd-control budgets, protected volumes and recovery prevent a spectacular effect from permanently destroying another player's content. Tab-targeting expresses selection intent; source ability execution, range, aim and encounter counterplay remain meaningful.

The recommended first game milestone is one negotiated civic consequence spanning a small connected region pair, with combat and noncombat solutions, save/reload, accessibility and counterplay. It should prove why multiple covenants matter before expanding world scale. Actual region selection and production scope require the roadmap approval gate.

## Release-blocking distinctions

Imported is not fully validated. CPU lifecycle evidence does not prove GPU rendering, target damage, animation playback or source-equivalent visual quality. A three-dimensional illustration is not a model, rig, atlas, exported animation or shader. A JSON schema pass is not narrative, art or gameplay approval. A proposed performance budget is not a measured hardware benchmark. The full-game engine and production network stack remain undecided.

The foundation should be reviewed as one coherent proposed design package and a reproducible technical import. Commercial asset rights, full runtime fidelity, target devices, final art and new canon approvals remain explicit gates. Consult the generated validation report and root migration report for current measured counts rather than deriving completion from document headings.

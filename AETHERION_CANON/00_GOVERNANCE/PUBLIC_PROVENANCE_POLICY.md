# Public provenance policy

This is a minimized public projection. Private-source locators and retrieval identifiers are withheld; complete owner-side originals remain private. Local SRC/REF/reference/asset IDs resolve the public evidence chain through source titles, dates, scoped verification status, repository-relative paths and hashes. Withheld locators are not missing approval evidence and do not broaden the permission granted.

Permission to share the 114 recovered images does not authorize publishing private ChatGPT/Library locators or unrelated retrieval metadata. Source runtime provenance points to the public pinned repository and is preserved. No original Ability-Matrix file or original image byte is changed by this projection. Individual creative approval, production validation and rights review remain separate gates.

# Narrative state contract

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Engine-agnostic design contract. These are proposed data semantics, not an existing quest API in the audited Ability Matrix runtime.

## State domains

- Personal: witness access, reviewed initiations, voluntary bindings, evidence provenance, regional settlement, promises, final ending and personal aftermath.
- Party instance: explicit shared decision receipt, instance encounter state and consented branch presentation. A member may leave before commitment without losing personal progression.
- Shared region/server: recoverable event stage, public service availability, bounded market modifiers and territory state under the systems authority contract.
- World canon: approved authored facts and asset versions. A player action never silently mutates this repository's canon.

## Evidence and commitments

[PROPOSED] Evidence records identify proposition, source, independence group, reliability limits, acquisition quest and disclosure permission. Two copies of one account share an independence group. A protected source can support a sealed review without exposing identity publicly. Quest completion and evidence truth are separate: completing a search does not make every found statement correct.

[PROPOSED] Promise records identify beneficiary, exact undertaking, scope, expiry or review condition, foreseeable conflicts and a warning state. A betrayal branch requires a relevant unmet promise plus an intelligible actor motive and a prior warning. No random loyalty roll may replace those conditions.

## Initiation and order independence

[PROPOSED] Every quest-initiation-NN and quest-region-NN depends only on quest-main-02. Optional powers may create shortcuts, but an ordinary tool, public service or companion-assisted path preserves progression for every first Oath and for deferred binding. Faction standing may change dialogue, prices or restricted optional privileges; it cannot block the only path to story-critical evidence.

[PROPOSED] Regional settlement updates are keyed by region and commute when no explicit cross-realm promise is being resolved. Cross-realm conflicts are additional authored scenes with visible prerequisites, not hidden order-sensitive overwrites. Replaying evidence cannot duplicate rewards or erase a previous confirmed settlement.

## Ending predicates

[PROPOSED] Common predicates are corroboration_complete, custody_resolved, service_plan_ready and final_plan_reviewed. Every service-attestation-NN must carry a plan, affected civic reviewer and continuity fallback, earned through a regional arc or independent civic review. Dominion additionally requires a selected valid oath-01..15 and explicit acceptance of its authority terms. Restoration requires charter and review choices. Liberation requires staged release and replacement ownership choices.

[PROPOSED] The commitment transaction is atomic within its narrative scope: preview → explicit confirmation → validate unchanged prerequisites → record decision receipt → enact aftermath. If prerequisites changed, return to review with the changed facts. Network failure must not charge costs twice or create two different endings; retry by decision identifier and authoritative receipt.

## Recovery and accessibility

[PROPOSED] Before irreversible commitment, preserve a return path and readable summary. After commitment, preserve the chosen consequence while allowing repair of practical damage and continued access to optional content. Critical dialogue has captions, a transcript, speaker identification and reviewable choice wording. Time limits, color-only cues, hidden morality scores and mandatory auditory puzzles cannot decide irreversible political outcomes.

[PROPOSED] Save migrations preserve stable IDs, explicit branch receipts and evidence provenance. Deprecated quest IDs map through a reviewed migration table; unresolved records are quarantined for recovery rather than silently interpreted as a different choice. No memory-themed quest can erase real user data or authoritative consent history.

## Explicit alternate-route producers

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] quest-civic-review-01 through quest-civic-review-15 each depend only on orientation and produce their matching service-attestation flag plus observed interface and civilian-cost evidence. They inspect a public service, compare independent testimony and record a viable continuity plan with review provenance. They do not set region-settled state, defeat a boss or claim that the reviewer endorses the final ending.

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] quest-evidence-independent-record provides the independent-record flag through public Rime, witnessed Daybreak or protected Court access. Main investigation now requires the three evidence flags; final planning requires all fifteen service attestations and resolved custody. Final planning explicitly records the chosen ending plan's required decisions before the final commitment predicate is evaluated.

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] WORLD_NARRATIVE_VALIDATION.json includes actual declared-graph traversal from an empty flag state. Its proof paths reach each ending and aftermath using civic reviews while skipping every regional climax. These tests establish logical reachability in the proposed data contract, not that objectives are implemented or playable.

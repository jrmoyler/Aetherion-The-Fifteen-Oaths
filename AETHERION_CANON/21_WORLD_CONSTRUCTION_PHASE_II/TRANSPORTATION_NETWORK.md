# Continental transportation network

**All additions PROPOSED.** Sources: P2-SRC-FOUNDATION, P2-SRC-DIRECTIVE, P2-SRC-PROPOSAL. Thirty foundation routes remain the ordinary backbone. TRANSPORT_ROUTE_REGISTRY refines each route without changing its endpoint capitals or baseline ID. Every region must be explorable before binding any Oath.

## Operating layers

1. **Essential ordinary network:** public walking paths, mountain roads, pack trails and staffed relief transport. All thirty adjacency edges participate. No fare, faction reputation, PvP flag or magical ability gates the civic walking lane.
2. **Commodity network:** high-capacity trade highways, river barges, canals, carts and freight yards. Their capacity and cargo permissions can change with politics or events; ordinary access persists.
3. **Optional express network:** gliders, relay carriers, Escapement vehicles, gravity platforms, void thresholds and teleport anchors. These are useful conveniences with resource, safety and endpoint constraints, never substitutes for layer one.
4. **Local expedition network:** climbing, swimming, diving, tunnels and consensual traversal morphs. These reveal optional routes or shorten exploration but retain ordinary alternatives for essential services and main narrative progression.

## Costs, capacity and clocks

The registry's abstract credits are test tokens, not a newly approved currency. Walking fare is zero. Optional fare bands are design starting points, waived by relief vouchers when needed for essential access. They must be balanced against the economy worker's final income model before adoption. Creature mounts still require food, water, shelter and rest; machines require maintenance and energy. A free walking fare does not erase those logistics.

Route walk-minute intervals are **greybox pacing targets**, not measured travel, fictional calendar hours or a conversion from SVG dimensions. The proposed base bins are 14–21 minutes for ordinary terraces, 18–27 for wet/channel approaches and 24–36 for steep passes. Each edge-removal detour sums the relevant targets, exposing how much time an interruption can add. Do not use these values as marketing claims or terrain dimensions.

Capacity is a target service throughput in persons/hour or pack loads/hour, not simultaneous MMO concurrency or rendered actors. Foot targets are 60 on steep routes and 120 elsewhere; pack targets are 20 or 40. Optional mode capacities appear in the catalog, from four passengers/hour on constrained void thresholds to 300 on a trade highway. Scheduling, demand, boarding time and route-direction allocation remain prototype work. Opposing traffic shares a narrow corridor; do not multiply capacity by two without passing-space evidence.

## Express-service lore and restrictions

**Teleportation:** a licensed shared-anchor service maintained by surveyors and civic operators. Both anchors must be visited and certified, so travel cannot erase first discovery or territorial stakes. Fare supports inspection and finite charge reserve. A charge begins only after destination readiness and capacity reservation. Failure before departure refunds the fare; failure after transition returns to a certified recovery pad. No arbitrary coordinates, private interior destinations, battlefield extraction, contraband freight or Oath-exclusive civic access.

**Storm Relay:** charge drives a restrained grounded carrier on a verified span. Lightning spectacle communicates energy and lockout state; it does not justify instantaneous travel through every storm cloud. Grounding failure closes the carrier, not the public road. A route to a storm region remains possible without knowing or binding its Oath.

**Escapement Road:** winding stations and timed right-of-way make vehicles reliable. Timing anomalies are authored local volumes, not free global rewind or unlimited acceleration. Ticketing reflects maintenance and scheduled capacity. A stalled timetable never traps a player indefinitely or removes their belongings.

**Observatory:** platforms are counterweighted within bounded gravity volumes and rated for passenger mass. Emergency docks exist before launch. No platform extends a gravity anomaly across an entire neighboring realm; its named corridor can contain several separate local rides with ground transfers.

**The Missing Court:** paired charter thresholds alter a specific passage, with visible entry/reentry and endpoint verification. They cannot cross arbitrary walls, invalidate locked homes, conceal essential warnings or make ordinary citizens disappear. A failed endpoint aborts to origin. The user's “Hidden Court” travel phrase denotes this mode, not a new Oath or realm.

## Regional failure, detours and service protection

Each of the thirty records names a concrete closure trigger, preserved endpoint capitals, a computed alternate region sequence and its actual baseline route IDs. The detour is computed with that original edge removed. This proves the ordinary regional graph survives **any single-edge failure**. It does not prove the local bypass has already been built, a flood leaves the replacement bridge safe, or every combination of closures is survivable.

A closure controller must:

1. Forecast danger, stop new boarding, finish safe arrivals or evacuate to a certified pad.
2. Check the separate local service trace against the same hazard footprint; do not call two lanes on one bridge redundant.
3. If necessary select the listed regional detour, then recompute against all concurrent closures.
4. Verify capital care, storage retrieval, housing return and first-Oath access; keep protected routes staffed.
5. Reserve at least one quarter of scheduled civilian capacity for relief, allowing queues and supply escorts instead of unlimited instantaneous throughput.
6. Reopen only after structural, water, visibility and operator checks; event victory alone is not an engineering inspection.

At Shared Saddle, freight control changes taxes and staged cargo, not the civil right to cross. At Fault–Daybreak, an ore dispute can delay bulk loads without conscripting civilians. At Bitterglass–Tidal, contaminated water closes cargo channels and redirects people to dry routes. At Escapement–Impulse, a freight bridge outage moves relief to another geography; a foot lane on the broken bridge is not an alternative.

## Production tests

Run all 30 single-edge closures, representative correlated storm/surge/landslide closure sets, and full optional-mode shutdown. Test routes using a new unbound character without premium currency or faction reputation. Inspect slope, collision, rest spacing, turning radii, riverbank exits, navigation cues, rescue pad placement, local streaming and cargo queues. Record actual time distributions and throughput before replacing registry targets. Human review must check whether detour penalties are fun, fair and understandable; topology alone cannot decide that.

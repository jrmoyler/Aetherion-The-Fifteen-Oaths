# Region/capital evidence and acceptance

All specifications are PROPOSED. The foundation commit and file anchors are recorded in every registry and linked from every blueprint. The 63 original POI records are preserved value-for-value; added records supply spatial roles without renaming existing capitals or claiming a new realm. Observations come from each baseline dossier and art guide, not unseen images. Every added village/outpost has a provisioning or safety job; districts are not counted as independent settlements.

Automated evidence is in REGION_CAPITAL_ACCEPTANCE.json: strict schemas, unknown-field rejection, IDs, 28 required region fields, all document paths and capital graph resilience. Graph resilience is not proof of safe physical escape. All metre dimensions, height targets and practice envelopes require blockout and engineering/accessibility review. No real demographics, terrain coordinates, engine assets, production budgets or approval are implied.

Public event catchments retain poi-NN-event and world-event-NN; new boss, habitat, resource, secret and PvP IDs are coordinated with simulation. Architecture kit IDs are coordinated with the asset owner. Cross-file checks in the complete package remain the package QA owner's responsibility.

# FOLDED THEATRE major realm — Phase II spatial blueprint

Status: PROPOSED. Sources: P2-SRC-FOUNDATION, P2-SRC-DIRECTIVE, P2-SRC-PROPOSAL. All new dimensions, names and spatial arrangements need review. This is a refinement, not a replacement dossier or functioning level.

Baseline: [13 FOLDED THEATRE](https://github.com/jrmoyler/Aetherion-The-Fifteen-Oaths/tree/5dce9edae0bb3587eb7c72433395e7d5a19857ea/AETHERION_CANON/04_OATH_DOSSIERS/13_FOLDED_THEATRE.md); IDs region-13, oath-13, poi-13-capital.

## Terrain

east-central sheltered valleys; dry basins and low passes between escarpment and karst. Climate: dry summers, cool nights and episodic valley fog. Drainage remains basin-east. Place the capital as fixed backstage grid beneath reversible performance fronts, with plain service grid crosses spectacle-facing streets at marked anchor courts. Transition through cliff gardens become dry orchards and stable valley towns; do not paint a hard elemental border. Continent adjacency stays region-06, region-09, region-10, region-11, region-12, region-14; see WORLD_REGION_GRAPH.json. No new river may reverse a baseline basin or cross a divide without review.

## Capital

Interior performance spaces can change within authored bounds, while hospitals, exits and public services retain recognizable anchors. The detailed layout is [capital blueprint](../CAPITAL_CITY_BLUEPRINTS/13_MANYDOOR.md); its civic, working and household paths are independently legible. ## Settlements

## Villages

## Outposts

Place a visible refuge marker before the exposed approach, with shelter, a manual warning board, a maintenance locker and a return path. Its job is warning and continuity, not a toll wall that seals the realm.

## Infrastructure

Detailed capital service and exit graphs are in CAPITAL_LAYOUT_REGISTRY.json.

## Civilian

Baseline social purpose: Residents celebrate rehearsal, costume exchange and public disclosure of staged experiences. Covenant duty is to mark consequential illusions and preserve informed exit, while political patrons seek exceptions for supposedly beneficial civic myths. Spatial refinement gives everyday work a visible home: Backstage Homes contains kitchens, rest courts, accessible housing entries and a public route to Anchor Square. People disagree through hearings and bargaining, not a monolithic elemental personality. Visitors and dissenters retain food, care and routes.

## Commercial

The Theatre exports entertainment, training simulations and architectural illusion while importing food and precision materials. Patronage can fund civic imagination or monopolize the public story; transparent commissioning changes whose futures get rehearsed. Imports food, precision materials, timber land at Plain Gate; exports performance, training simulations, illusion craft pass through Scene Workshops. Shared repair counters and the exchange settlement absorb downtime. Display inventory shortages as delayed contracts with substitute ordinary services, never as blanket disappearance of supplies.

## Military

Forces use decoys, misdirection and rehearsed repositioning to reduce casualties. False surrender, hidden civilian hazards and impersonation of protected services create consequences, keeping deception from becoming an unlimited military excuse. Spatial delta: gate watch/refuge p2-poi-13-watch-refuge has separate civilian and equipment entrances, protected stores and a public-facing complaint board. Defenses face the approach; no artillery or discharge arc crosses housing or the service loop. Rotating guards walk gate → relief store → watch point → rest, and are not permanent combat crowds.

## Institution

District troupes share a civic stage charter, while plain-surface inspectors certify essential routes and contracts. The central conflict is whether those inspectors protect consent or merely enforce the tastes of established patrons. Put accountable hearings in Anchor Square and specialist instruction in Perspective School. Enterable p2-poi-13-public-record-room contains a public record desk, private consultation niche and service exit. Access disputes produce alternate evidence routes; Oath binding never becomes a library card or care requirement.

## Sacred

Unmasked Interval in Anchor Square is a voluntary sacred/commemorative Oath space: a disclosed, illusion-free remembrance pause between performances where choosing not to act is honored. It occupies an accessible alcove or court inside the existing civic district, so it adds a distinct purpose without inventing another settlement. The ordinary public entrance remains open to unbound visitors, observers and dissenters; no prayer, ritual, ancestry, faction standing or Oath binding is required for civic services. A quiet side exit and nonparticipatory route preserve choice. Objects left here are authored story props, not automatic transfers of player possessions.

## Habitats

Protect Mirrorless Ravine (p2-poi-13-habitat): dry-valley nesting and low-stimulation guardian diversion ground. Event recovery restores passage rather than permanently exterminating a biome.

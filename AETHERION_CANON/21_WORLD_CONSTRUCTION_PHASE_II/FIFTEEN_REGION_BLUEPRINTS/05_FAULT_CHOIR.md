# FAULT CHOIR major realm — Phase II spatial blueprint

Status: PROPOSED. Sources: P2-SRC-FOUNDATION, P2-SRC-DIRECTIVE, P2-SRC-PROPOSAL. All new dimensions, names and spatial arrangements need review. This is a refinement, not a replacement dossier or functioning level.

Baseline: [05 FAULT CHOIR](https://github.com/jrmoyler/Aetherion-The-Fifteen-Oaths/tree/5dce9edae0bb3587eb7c72433395e7d5a19857ea/AETHERION_CANON/04_OATH_DOSSIERS/05_FAULT_CHOIR.md); IDs region-05, oath-05, poi-05-capital.

## Terrain

central-west watershed divide; crystal-veined ridges, canyon shoulders and stepped valley floors. Climate: wet western faces, dry eastern lee and flash-flooding canyon bottoms. Drainage remains basin-choir. Place the capital as canyon shoulder with stepped buttress courts and two daylight adits, with load-bearing shoulder road across contour; deep chambers under surveyed rock only. Transition through exposed stone gives way to cultivated terrace soils and basin woodland; do not paint a hard elemental border. Continent adjacency stays region-01, region-02, region-03, region-07, region-08, region-09; see WORLD_REGION_GRAPH.json. No new river may reverse a baseline basin or cross a divide without review.

## Capital

Keystone Hall (poi-05-capital) keeps its baseline identity: Keystone Hall occupies a broad canyon shoulder anchored to surveyed bedrock. Deep chambers are civic workspaces, not an excuse to remove ventilation, drainage, emergency exits or accessible vertical transport. The detailed layout is [capital blueprint](../CAPITAL_CITY_BLUEPRINTS/05_KEYSTONE_HALL.md); its civic, working and household paths are independently legible. City envelope target 1300 × 900 m with 45 m local relief is a first blockout hypothesis, not approved geography.

## Settlements

Talus Steps (poi-05-settlement) remains the existing exchange settlement: Talus Steps is a movable terrace settlement outside old canyon walls. Its modular houses embarrass traditional elites but offer a practical model for living with unstable geology instead of pretending all movement is failure. Its role is retained rather than renamed to fill a checklist. SETTLEMENT_REGISTRY.json distinguishes independent settlements from capital districts. The added food/craft village and safety outpost address provisioning and last-mile relief; additional towns require demand, terrain and route evidence before proposal.

## Villages

Site on a viable ordinary spur near the exchange settlement, downstream of no contaminated intake and outside the boss footprint. A shared store, wash point, household cluster and cart turning court support its livelihood; field-to-store-to-market is its daily loop. Interior and household counts remain unresolved.

## Outposts

Splitmark Watch — slope monitoring and freight diversion at a watershed pass (p2-settlement-05-splitmark-watch). Watch staff rotate from the capital; visitors can rest without military allegiance. Place a visible refuge marker before the exposed approach, with shelter, a manual warning board, a maintenance locker and a return path. Its job is warning and continuity, not a toll wall that seals the realm.

## Infrastructure

Provide physically separate potable water, process/dirty return and storm overflow paths, with inspectable crossings; source and outfall must be checked against CONTINENT_GEOGRAPHY.md and the baseline watershed. Solid waste moves to a bounded service site, never a magical void. Manual signs and emergency stores remain when special infrastructure shuts down. Detailed capital service and exit graphs are in CAPITAL_LAYOUT_REGISTRY.json.

## Civilian

Baseline social purpose: Public maintenance days repair paths and exchange grievances around common load maps. Civic obligation is contributing to shared structural safety; caste-like inherited assignments are a contested abuse of that principle. Spatial refinement gives everyday work a visible home: Jointed Steps contains kitchens, rest courts, accessible housing entries and a public route to Weight Court. People disagree through hearings and bargaining, not a monolithic elemental personality. Visitors and dissenters retain food, care and routes.

## Commercial

Fault exports minerals and structural expertise, importing food diversity, tool steels and communication parts. Royalties can fund common foundations or entrench claim holders; the regional quest determines oversight rather than creating free resources. Imports tool steels, food diversity, message hardware land at Talus Gate; exports ore, crystal instruments, structural surveying pass through Braceworks. Shared repair counters and the exchange settlement absorb downtime. Display inventory shortages as delayed contracts with substitute ordinary services, never as blanket disappearance of supplies.

## Military

Military crews fortify supply corridors and create fallback terraces rather than walling every border. Defenders must leave relief access and keep geological interventions within surveyed zones, preserving civilian escape during siege. Spatial delta: gate watch/refuge p2-poi-05-watch-refuge has separate civilian and equipment entrances, protected stores and a public-facing complaint board. Defenses face the approach; no artillery or discharge arc crosses housing or the service loop. Rotating guards walk gate → relief store → watch point → rest, and are not permanent combat crowds.

## Institution

Terrace councils govern locally, while a load assembly certifies changes affecting shared foundations. Appeals require independent surveys, creating conflict when the institution that defines safe evidence also controls survey training. Put accountable hearings in Weight Court and specialist instruction in Listening Hall. Enterable p2-poi-05-public-record-room contains a public record desk, private consultation niche and service exit. Access disputes produce alternate evidence routes; Oath binding never becomes a library card or care requirement.

## Sacred

Unburdened Stone Recess in Weight Court is a voluntary sacred/commemorative Oath space: a voluntary laying-down of a small load to remember workers and renegotiate burdens; no structural counterweight is moved. It occupies an accessible alcove or court inside the existing civic district, so it adds a distinct purpose without inventing another settlement. The ordinary public entrance remains open to unbound visitors, observers and dissenters; no prayer, ritual, ancestry, faction standing or Oath binding is required for civic services. A quiet side exit and nonparticipatory route preserve choice. Objects left here are authored story props, not automatic transfers of player possessions.

## Underground

drained bedrock galleries → daylight rescue adit → shoulder civic road → stepped dwellings → open buttress crown; every occupied chamber has two independent egress directions. Separate civic maintenance from dungeon occupancy at signed vestibules. The underground plan is survey gate → worker shelter → counterweight chamber → load allocation archive → second daylight adit; dry/ordinary access and independent exit are part of the blockout. Flood, smoke, collapse and anomaly envelopes cannot span every exit at once. Where deep geology is unsuitable, use a shallow covered service route rather than inventing a vast cavern.

## Habitats

Resonance moles aerate thin soils and react to machinery vibrations. Cave fungi support food production but depend on ventilation and waste management, preventing the underground realm from becoming a consequence-free pantry. Protect Resonant Scree (p2-poi-05-habitat): burrow and crystal-organism margins outside occupied retaining walls. Map shelter, feeding edge, breeding refuge and migration pinch separately in the terrain blockout. Wildlife routine shifts between shelter and forage; warnings precede defensive behavior. Event recovery restores passage rather than permanently exterminating a biome.

## Hazards

Rain-shadow slopes are dry, canyon bottoms flash-flood, and fault movements disrupt foundations. Seismic warnings combine sound, gauge movement and visual notices so deaf players can participate in rescue and puzzles. Hazard ownership stays local to authored work/encounter volumes. Use observable warning → preparation → closure → recovery; failing staff or player attendance triggers a safe fallback, not permanent damage. Hazard resistance cannot substitute for air, footing, clear access or consent.

## Weather

Baseline weather: wet western faces, dry eastern lee and flash-flooding canyon bottoms. Heavy rain stops exposed cutting and raises seepage watches; contour bypass opens before lower adits close. Border blending follows elevation, exposure and water; magic modifies local authored conditions only. Night and weather combinations must be checked at all marked crossings; no mandatory color-only, audio-only or glare-based cue is acceptable.

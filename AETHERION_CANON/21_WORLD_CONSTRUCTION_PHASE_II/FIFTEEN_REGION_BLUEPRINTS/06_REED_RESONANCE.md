Status: PROPOSED. Sources: P2-SRC-FOUNDATION, P2-SRC-DIRECTIVE, P2-SRC-PROPOSAL. All new dimensions, names and spatial arrangements need review. This is a refinement, not a replacement dossier or functioning level.

Baseline: [06 REED RESONANCE](https://github.com/jrmoyler/Aetherion-The-Fifteen-Oaths/tree/5dce9edae0bb3587eb7c72433395e7d5a19857ea/AETHERION_CANON/04_OATH_DOSSIERS/06_REED_RESONANCE.md); IDs region-06, oath-06, poi-06-capital.

## Terrain

northeastern inland escarpment; windward ridge shoulders and leeward cliff terraces. Climate: gusty upland with sheltered garden microclimates. Drainage remains basin-dawn. Place the capital as escarpment crescent organized around sheltered windbreak courts, with grounded lee-side promenade parallels exposed performance edge. Transition through cliff scrub grades into central grassland and eastern dry valleys; do not paint a hard elemental border. Continent adjacency stays region-03, region-09, region-10, region-13; see WORLD_REGION_GRAPH.json. No new river may reverse a baseline basin or cross a divide without review.

## Outposts

Its job is warning and continuity, not a toll wall that seals the realm.

Provide physically separate potable water, process/dirty return and storm overflow paths, with inspectable crossings; source and outfall must be checked against CONTINENT_GEOGRAPHY.md and the baseline watershed. ## Civilian

Baseline social purpose: Communal kitchens use wind-driven machinery while storytellers, signers and scribes share cultural authority. Civic obligation is to help carry essential warnings in an accessible form, never to sing or accept one approved voice. Spatial refinement gives everyday work a visible home: Lee Hearths contains kitchens, rest courts, accessible housing entries and a public route to Quiet Commons. People disagree through hearings and bargaining, not a monolithic elemental personality. Visitors and dissenters retain food, care and routes.

## Commercial

Reed exports textiles, forecasting and sound engineering while importing structural fasteners and grain. Cultural commissions can fund shared stages or reward only favored voices; procurement reforms affect who can afford to speak. Imports grain, fasteners, repair materials land at Notch Gate; exports textiles, instruments, wind engineering pass through Reedworks. Shared repair counters and the exchange settlement absorb downtime. Display inventory shortages as delayed contracts with substitute ordinary services, never as blanket disappearance of supplies.

## Military

Scouts secure ridge approaches and maintain evacuation corridors before dueling on exposed stages. Acoustic suppression can protect a rescue but becomes abusive when used against peaceful assembly, a distinction reflected in regional law. Spatial delta: gate watch/refuge p2-poi-06-watch-refuge has separate civilian and equipment entrances, protected stores and a public-facing complaint board. Defenses face the approach; no artillery or discharge arc crosses housing or the service loop. Rotating guards walk gate → relief store → watch point → rest, and are not permanent combat crowds.

## Institution

Conductors coordinate emergencies but must surrender that authority afterward; prolonged emergencies are the central route by which temporary discipline becomes permanent control. Put accountable hearings in Quiet Commons and specialist instruction in Echo Stages. Enterable p2-poi-06-public-record-room contains a public record desk, private consultation niche and service exit. Access disputes produce alternate evidence routes; Oath binding never becomes a library card or care requirement.

## Sacred

The ordinary public entrance remains open to unbound visitors, observers and dissenters; no prayer, ritual, ancestry, faction standing or Oath binding is required for civic services. A quiet side exit and nonparticipatory route preserve choice. Objects left here are authored story props, not automatic transfers of player possessions.

## Hazards

Hazard resistance cannot substitute for air, footing, clear access or consent.

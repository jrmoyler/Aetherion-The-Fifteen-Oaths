# TIDAL COVENANT major realm — Phase II spatial blueprint

Status: PROPOSED. Sources: P2-SRC-FOUNDATION, P2-SRC-DIRECTIVE, P2-SRC-PROPOSAL. All new dimensions, names and spatial arrangements need review. This is a refinement, not a replacement dossier or functioning level.

Baseline: [04 TIDAL COVENANT](https://github.com/jrmoyler/Aetherion-The-Fifteen-Oaths/tree/5dce9edae0bb3587eb7c72433395e7d5a19857ea/AETHERION_CANON/04_OATH_DOSSIERS/04_TIDAL_COVENANT.md); IDs region-04, oath-04, poi-04-capital.

## Terrain

southwestern mainland estuary; low coastal floodplain, causeway-linked reef districts and raised refuge terraces. Climate: warm maritime, seasonal rain and storm surge. Drainage remains basin-west, basin-choir. Place the capital as mainland canal fan with attached reef fingers, with freshwater-to-estuary gradient; elevated land causeway stays continuous. Transition through fresh river channels become brackish marsh then coastal water; do not paint a hard elemental border. Continent adjacency stays region-07, region-08; see WORLD_REGION_GRAPH.json. No new river may reverse a baseline basin or cross a divide without review.

## Capital

Confluence Quay (poi-04-capital) keeps its baseline identity: Confluence Quay is a mainland estuary city with shore-attached reef districts and navigable canals. Reef-city imagery never creates disconnected realm islands; occupied reefs connect by maintained causeways and accessible ferries. The detailed layout is [capital blueprint](../CAPITAL_CITY_BLUEPRINTS/04_CONFLUENCE_QUAY.md); its civic, working and household paths are independently legible. City envelope target 1700 × 1300 m with 15 m local relief is a first blockout hypothesis, not approved geography.

## Settlements

Its residents know how upstream choices alter fishing, crops and foundation stability. Its role is retained rather than renamed to fill a checklist. SETTLEMENT_REGISTRY.json distinguishes independent settlements from capital districts. ## Villages

Site on a viable ordinary spur near the exchange settlement, downstream of no contaminated intake and outside the boss footprint. A shared store, wash point, household cluster and cart turning court support its livelihood; field-to-store-to-market is its daily loop. Interior and household counts remain unresolved.

## Outposts

Place a visible refuge marker before the exposed approach, with shelter, a manual warning board, a maintenance locker and a return path. Its job is warning and continuity, not a toll wall that seals the realm.

Provide physically separate potable water, process/dirty return and storm overflow paths, with inspectable crossings; source and outfall must be checked against CONTINENT_GEOGRAPHY.md and the baseline watershed. Solid waste moves to a bounded service site, never a magical void. Manual signs and emergency stores remain when special infrastructure shuts down. Detailed capital service and exit graphs are in CAPITAL_LAYOUT_REGISTRY.json.

## Civilian

Baseline social purpose: Public washing courts, shared cooking water and boat repair shape everyday encounters. Obligation is a measurable maintenance share with hardship relief; hereditary debt is a contested institution, never a natural law of the element. Spatial refinement gives everyday work a visible home: Dry Hearths contains kitchens, rest courts, accessible housing entries and a public route to Confluence Forum. People disagree through hearings and bargaining, not a monolithic elemental personality. Visitors and dissenters retain food, care and routes.

## Commercial

Tidal exports transport and water-management expertise while importing grain, timber and tools. Water allocation is not a simple auction; drought policy weighs household need, ecological flow and economically vital irrigation. Imports grain, timber, durable tools land at Landward Gate; exports shipping, fisheries, hydraulics pass through Ropewater Quays. Shared repair counters and the exchange settlement absorb downtime. Display inventory shortages as delayed contracts with substitute ordinary services, never as blanket disappearance of supplies.

## Military

Defenders control channels, rescue routes and harbor approaches. Naval power can protect trade or enforce abusive tolls, while freshwater sabotage is a severe political violation with emergency mitigation built into event design. Spatial delta: gate watch/refuge p2-poi-04-watch-refuge has separate civilian and equipment entrances, protected stores and a public-facing complaint board. Defenses face the approach; no artillery or discharge arc crosses housing or the service loop. Rotating guards walk gate → relief store → watch point → rest, and are not permanent combat crowds.

## Institution

Watershed assemblies give upstream and downstream settlements a voice; harbor houses manage navigation separately. Drought arbitration requires public flow records so maritime wealth cannot silently overrule drinking-water needs. Put accountable hearings in Confluence Forum and specialist instruction in Sluice College. Enterable p2-poi-04-public-record-room contains a public record desk, private consultation niche and service exit. Access disputes produce alternate evidence routes; Oath binding never becomes a library card or care requirement.

## Sacred

Returning Cup Court in Confluence Forum is a voluntary sacred/commemorative Oath space: a freshwater gratitude rite beside a symbolic sealed basin; it never consumes the emergency potable reserve. It occupies an accessible alcove or court inside the existing civic district, so it adds a distinct purpose without inventing another settlement. The ordinary public entrance remains open to unbound visitors, observers and dissenters; no prayer, ritual, ancestry, faction standing or Oath binding is required for civic services. A quiet side exit and nonparticipatory route preserve choice. Objects left here are authored story props, not automatic transfers of player possessions.

## Underground

Separate civic maintenance from dungeon occupancy at signed vestibules. Flood, smoke, collapse and anomaly envelopes cannot span every exit at once. Where deep geology is unsuitable, use a shallow covered service route rather than inventing a vast cavern.

## Habitats

Reedback grazers keep channels open and shelter fish nurseries. Overgrazing or exclusion damages habitat differently, giving husbandry and fishing meaningful seasonal responsibilities beyond harvesting a decorative coastal biome. Protect Nursery Shoals (p2-poi-04-habitat): seasonal estuary young protected from cargo wake and dredging. Map shelter, feeding edge, breeding refuge and migration pinch separately in the terrain blockout. Wildlife routine shifts between shelter and forage; warnings precede defensive behavior. Event recovery restores passage rather than permanently exterminating a biome.

## Hazards

Hazard ownership stays local to authored work/encounter volumes. Use observable warning → preparation → closure → recovery; failing staff or player attendance triggers a safe fallback, not permanent damage. Hazard resistance cannot substitute for air, footing, clear access or consent.

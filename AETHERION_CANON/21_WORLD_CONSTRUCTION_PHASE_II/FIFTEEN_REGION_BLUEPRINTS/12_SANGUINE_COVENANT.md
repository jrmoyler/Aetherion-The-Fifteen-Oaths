# SANGUINE COVENANT major realm — Phase II spatial blueprint

Status: PROPOSED. Sources: P2-SRC-FOUNDATION, P2-SRC-DIRECTIVE, P2-SRC-PROPOSAL. All new dimensions, names and spatial arrangements need review. This is a refinement, not a replacement dossier or functioning level.

Baseline: [12 SANGUINE COVENANT](https://github.com/jrmoyler/Aetherion-The-Fifteen-Oaths/tree/5dce9edae0bb3587eb7c72433395e7d5a19857ea/AETHERION_CANON/04_OATH_DOSSIERS/12_SANGUINE_COVENANT.md); IDs region-12, oath-12, poi-12-capital.

## Terrain

southern interior redlands; low iron-rich hills and river-crossing terraces. Climate: seasonal dry heat, cool uplands and flood pulses. Drainage remains basin-dawn. Place the capital as river-crossing terraces clustered around shared kitchens, with bridge-to-care stair with a parallel supply contour ramp. Transition through central farms grade into scrub pasture and southeastern steppe; do not paint a hard elemental border. Continent adjacency stays region-08, region-09, region-13, region-14; see WORLD_REGION_GRAPH.json. No new river may reverse a baseline basin or cross a divide without review.

## Capital

Kinhold (poi-12-capital) keeps its baseline identity: Kinhold rises on red-stone terraces above an inland river crossing. Ritual halls sit beside kitchens and recovery wards, making the material work of sustaining sacrifice visible instead of presenting blood symbolism without civilian life. The detailed layout is [capital blueprint](../CAPITAL_CITY_BLUEPRINTS/12_KINHOLD.md); its civic, working and household paths are independently legible. City envelope target 1300 × 1000 m with 40 m local relief is a first blockout hypothesis, not approved geography.

## Settlements

Open Hearth (poi-12-settlement) remains the existing exchange settlement: Open Hearth is a mixed household settlement that admits people without ancestral credentials. Its success challenges fortress elites while creating practical questions about how newcomers contribute to shared care and defense. Its role is retained rather than renamed to fill a checklist. SETTLEMENT_REGISTRY.json distinguishes independent settlements from capital districts. The added food/craft village and safety outpost address provisioning and last-mile relief; additional towns require demand, terrain and route evidence before proposal.

## Villages

Ironloam — grazing, pulses and soil management on mineral-red slopes (p2-settlement-12-ironloam). Site on a viable ordinary spur near the exchange settlement, downstream of no contaminated intake and outside the boss footprint. A shared store, wash point, household cluster and cart turning court support its livelihood; field-to-store-to-market is its daily loop. Interior and household counts remain unresolved.

## Outposts

Restshield Lodge — bridge relief and rotating guard rest above flood level (p2-settlement-12-restshield-lodge). Watch staff rotate from the capital; visitors can rest without military allegiance. Place a visible refuge marker before the exposed approach, with shelter, a manual warning board, a maintenance locker and a return path. Its job is warning and continuity, not a toll wall that seals the realm.

## Architecture

Use p2-arch-12-sanguine-cloisters, grounded in REF-102 and art-guide-12. Foundation motif: Crimson fortresses, ritual pillars and bound ceremonial platforms. Refinement: Thick communal halls, shaded recovery courts and interlinked household thresholds express mutual dependence. Private rooms and withdrawal spaces are equally important, giving architectural form to the right to refuse participation. Structural section: river-cut foundation shelf → bridge service apron → residential terraces → kitchen courts → quiet recovery cloister; no blood-fed hydrology. Ornament follows load, use and wear; new engineering dimensions remain review targets, and recovered images do not supply measurements.

## Infrastructure

food carts climb a contour ramp directly to Care Kitchens; linen and waste routes separate; guard rotations cannot consume the whole care staff. Provide physically separate potable water, process/dirty return and storm overflow paths, with inspectable crossings; source and outfall must be checked against CONTINENT_GEOGRAPHY.md and the baseline watershed. Solid waste moves to a bounded service site, never a magical void. Manual signs and emergency stores remain when special infrastructure shuts down. Detailed capital service and exit graphs are in CAPITAL_LAYOUT_REGISTRY.json.

## Civilian

Baseline social purpose: Communal meals, chosen-kin ceremonies and recorded care commitments organize daily life. Covenant duty must specify scope, duration and withdrawal terms; coercive inheritance is an institution to interrogate, not an unavoidable biological destiny. Spatial refinement gives everyday work a visible home: Open Hearths contains kitchens, rest courts, accessible housing entries and a public route to Pact Court. People disagree through hearings and bargaining, not a monolithic elemental personality. Visitors and dissenters retain food, care and routes.

## Commercial

Sanguine exports trained relief crews, textiles and caravan support while importing medicines and tools. Mutual-aid pools provide real security but can become coercive debt; reform must replace their useful functions rather than simply erase records. Imports medicines, tools, grain reserves land at Red Ford Gate; exports textiles, care networks, caravan support pass through Care Kitchens. Shared repair counters and the exchange settlement absorb downtime. Display inventory shortages as delayed contracts with substitute ordinary services, never as blanket disappearance of supplies.

## Military

Forces organize around mutual rescue and disciplined rotation, refusing to treat endurance as an infinite resource. Commanders who demand repeated sacrifice without recovery lose trust even if they win battles. Spatial delta: gate watch/refuge p2-poi-12-watch-refuge has separate civilian and equipment entrances, protected stores and a public-facing complaint board. Defenses face the approach; no artillery or discharge arc crosses housing or the service loop. Rotating guards walk gate → relief store → watch point → rest, and are not permanent combat crowds.

## Institution

Kin assemblies oversee communal care while consent tribunals review new and inherited pacts. Leaders disagree whether a descendant may end an obligation whose benefits still support others, making transition funding central to reform. Put accountable hearings in Pact Court and specialist instruction in Recovery Cloister. Enterable p2-poi-12-public-record-room contains a public record desk, private consultation niche and service exit. Access disputes produce alternate evidence routes; Oath binding never becomes a library card or care requirement.

## Sacred

Unbound Table in Pact Court is a voluntary sacred/commemorative Oath space: a shared memorial meal and voluntary renewal space; attendance never creates a pact, blood act or inherited obligation. It occupies an accessible alcove or court inside the existing civic district, so it adds a distinct purpose without inventing another settlement. The ordinary public entrance remains open to unbound visitors, observers and dissenters; no prayer, ritual, ancestry, faction standing or Oath binding is required for civic services. A quiet side exit and nonparticipatory route preserve choice. Objects left here are authored story props, not automatic transfers of player possessions.

## Underground

river-cut foundation shelf → bridge service apron → residential terraces → kitchen courts → quiet recovery cloister; no blood-fed hydrology. Separate civic maintenance from dungeon occupancy at signed vestibules. The underground plan is public care receiving room → ritual interface gallery → disputed records → replacement care junction → recovery-side exit; dry/ordinary access and independent exit are part of the blockout. Flood, smoke, collapse and anomaly envelopes cannot span every exit at once. Where deep geology is unsuitable, use a shallow covered service route rather than inventing a vast cavern.

## Landmarks

Primary skyline cue: Crimson fortresses, ritual pillars and bound ceremonial platforms. Secondary local anchors are Pact Court, Recovery Cloister and the clearly marked outpost refuge. Place these at decision points so silhouette, sign shapes and street logic work without saturated VFX. Great monuments never hide essential navigation behind mandatory camera rotation.

## Routes

Retain baseline ordinary pack routes to region-08, region-09, region-13, region-14 and the route IDs listed in TRANSPORT_ROUTE_REGISTRY.json / baseline TRADE_ROUTES.json. Local structure: neighbor approach → p2-settlement-12-restshield-lodge → poi-12-settlement → poi-12-capital; p2-settlement-12-ironloam uses a service spur. This ordering is functional, not surveyed distance. Capital public loop and housing-side relief exit remain outside events. Optional Oath traversal can shorten a route only after its ordinary counterpart works.

## Habitats

Ironbrush herdbeasts graze scrub and support transport, fiber and food economies. Redland packhorns require careful load management and communal pasture agreements, creating husbandry beyond the covenant's combat identity. Protect Redgrass Commons (p2-poi-12-habitat): grazers and ancestral guardian passage outside household terraces. Map shelter, feeding edge, breeding refuge and migration pinch separately in the terrain blockout. Wildlife routine shifts between shelter and forage; warnings precede defensive behavior. Event recovery restores passage rather than permanently exterminating a biome.

## Resources

Linenwater Fields (p2-poi-12-resource): fiber, food and repair wood reserved around communal care demand. Care reserves and recovery labor constrain gathering contracts; sacrifice is never an infinite resource mechanic. Place a safe observation approach, working cells and an independent supply exit. Resource availability has healthy, stressed, closed and recovering presentation; local stock and yield numbers remain unresolved and must agree with the simulation plan.

## Dungeon

The Inheritance Engine (poi-12-dungeon) remains: The Inheritance Engine combines ritual chambers with public care infrastructure and disputed records. Its challenge is dismantling coercive collection without abandoning people whose survival has become dependent on the same system. New spatial sequence: public care receiving room → ritual interface gallery → disputed records → replacement care junction → recovery-side exit. Foundation encounter context: The First Creditor is a living pact champion who voluntarily bears a large share of communal care and challenges unilateral withdrawal from agreements. Some obligations are coercive, others were freely renewed and support dependents. A nonlethal contest or mediated hearing establishes standing, but the final settlement must distinguish them and replace lost care.

Encounter identity: The First Creditor is a situational title for Adra Kest (character-12-leader), not an unregistered second person or a requirement to kill the leader. See DUNGEON_AND_EXPLORATION_PLAN.md for admission, reset, co-op and exit rules; do not turn living title-holders into mandatory kill targets.

## Worldboss

p2-poi-12-worldboss: An ancestral guardian mistakes changing kinship for betrayal and threatens a mixed settlement. The public encounter gathers living consent, protects residents and revises its mandate rather than proving one bloodline superior in combat. Refinement locates the footprint next to the habitat rather than inside the capital. Admission signs mark the active volume; refuges, distant viewing and relief logistics remain outside. Combat, redirection and repair roles share the encounter, with readable stand-down and recovery. If the event overlaps the public catchment, authority resolves to one bounded state, never additive hazards on all roads.

## Secret

Unrenewed Chamber (p2-poi-12-secret): a retired agreement room distinguishes expired coercion from living consent. Sort terms by renewal and beneficiary using readable records; no blood act or ancestry credential is needed. Its reward is optional testimony, a cosmetic/story variant or local repair knowledge; it cannot hide a mandatory civic exit, main-story prerequisite without hints, or a protected resident identity. A safe return path is visible from the discovery chamber.

## Housing

p2-poi-12-housing is the stable entry at Open Hearths. Housing includes multi-household courts, private withdrawal rooms and guest rights for chosen kin. Players can join communal homes without granting others control of their character, inventory or ability to leave. Proposed sample interior: 12 × 10 m clear room envelope plus 4 × 3 m entrance/storage vestibule, sized for blockout tests rather than a promised entitlement. Housing type uses local kit p2-arch-12-sanguine-cloisters; a street-facing recovery point preserves entry if event overlays close the exterior. No placement may obstruct public circulation, private storage permissions or the second exit.

## Events

Preserve Kinhold service catchment (poi-12-event) and world-event-12: A relief-muster event responds to flood or siege displacement. Players organize food, rest and voluntary service rotations; success rewards sustainable care, while exhaustion creates recoverable staffing gaps instead of glorified endless sacrifice. Warning boards at arrival and the outpost direct civilians to p2-district-12-red-ford-gate. Event bounds exclude the ordinary service loop; service staff relocate before an affected counter closes. Recovery works are optional contributions with automatic restoration when players leave. See WORLD_ECOLOGY_SYSTEM.md and NPC_CIVILIZATION_SYSTEM.md for shared-state coordination.

## Pvp

Mutual Stand Yard (p2-poi-12-pvp): nonlethal opt-in contests with staffed recovery and separate spectator exit. Entry is explicit and match-scoped at a separate gate; Oath choice, housing or event participation never flags visitors. Target an 80 × 55 m initial practice envelope only, with two withdrawn/refuge exits and spectator circulation outside combat. A future siege may use authored fortification sockets after a distinct review; it cannot absorb the public road or confiscate houses.

## Hazards

Hot dry spells alternate with seasonal floods and cool upland nights. Lifeforce magic does not replace water, nutrition or rest; recovery logistics remain an ordinary civic concern across both prosperity and conflict. Hazard ownership stays local to authored work/encounter volumes. Use observable warning → preparation → closure → recovery; failing staff or player attendance triggers a safe fallback, not permanent damage. Hazard resistance cannot substitute for air, footing, clear access or consent.

## Weather

Baseline weather: seasonal dry heat, cool uplands and flood pulses. Seasonal river rise closes the lower ford; high terrace bridge and care ramp remain the relief spine. Border blending follows elevation, exposure and water; magic modifies local authored conditions only. Night and weather combinations must be checked at all marked crossings; no mandatory color-only, audio-only or glare-based cue is acceptable.

## Daynight

Dawn shared cooking and care handover; noon consent hearings include respite breaks; night guard relief protects household sleep. Trading and household routines differ from patrols; essential food, care, stable housing and return services persist through rotating staff or an accessible fallback at Red Ford Gate. Do not use a whole-city curfew as an unannounced progress lock.

## Ambient

Measured heartlike percussion, communal singing and quiet kitchen activity humanize the region. Combat cost cues are clear and optional in intensity, avoiding constant invasive heartbeat audio or gratuitous injury emphasis. Blockout sound zones: working bed at Care Kitchens, conversational bed at Pact Court, quieter domestic bed at Open Hearths. Use occlusion boundaries before adding loops. Alert captions, readable devices and separate intensity controls survive weather and combat mixes.

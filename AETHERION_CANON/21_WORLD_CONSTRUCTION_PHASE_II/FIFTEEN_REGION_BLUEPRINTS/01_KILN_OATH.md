# KILN OATH major realm — Phase II spatial blueprint

Status: PROPOSED. Sources: P2-SRC-FOUNDATION, P2-SRC-DIRECTIVE, P2-SRC-PROPOSAL. All new dimensions, names and spatial arrangements need review. This is a refinement, not a replacement dossier or functioning level.

Baseline: [01 KILN OATH](https://github.com/jrmoyler/Aetherion-The-Fifteen-Oaths/tree/5dce9edae0bb3587eb7c72433395e7d5a19857ea/AETHERION_CANON/04_OATH_DOSSIERS/01_KILN_OATH.md); IDs region-01, oath-01, poi-01-capital.

## Terrain

western volcanic upland; upland caldera rims above the western river valleys. Climate: wet windward slopes with rain shadows inside calderas. Drainage remains basin-west. Place the capital as broken crescent on an extinct caldera rim, with outer-rim road east–west; work terraces step inward. Transition through basalt passes, managed ash beds and lower temperate forest; do not paint a hard elemental border. Continent adjacency stays region-02, region-05, region-07; see WORLD_REGION_GRAPH.json. No new river may reverse a baseline basin or cross a divide without review.

## Capital

Cindervault (poi-01-capital) keeps its baseline identity: Cindervault occupies terraces on an old caldera rim, never an active lava lake. Civic cisterns and outward evacuation roads make its volcanic grandeur physically legible and expose unequal access to safe elevation. The detailed layout is [capital blueprint](../CAPITAL_CITY_BLUEPRINTS/01_CINDERVAULT.md); its civic, working and household paths are independently legible. City envelope target 1400 × 800 m with 65 m local relief is a first blockout hypothesis, not approved geography.

## Settlements

Ashbridge (poi-01-settlement) remains the existing exchange settlement: Ashbridge sits on a cooled causeway between cropland and foundries. Its night market repairs imported tools, while farms beyond the bridge negotiate smoke days instead of existing as an industrial backdrop. Its role is retained rather than renamed to fill a checklist. SETTLEMENT_REGISTRY.json distinguishes independent settlements from capital districts. The added food/craft village and safety outpost address provisioning and last-mile relief; additional towns require demand, terrain and route evidence before proposal.

## Villages

Pumice Fold — ash-bed horticulture and seed washing on a cooled slope (p2-settlement-01-pumice-fold). Site on a viable ordinary spur near the exchange settlement, downstream of no contaminated intake and outside the boss footprint. A shared store, wash point, household cluster and cart turning court support its livelihood; field-to-store-to-market is its daily loop. Interior and household counts remain unresolved.

## Outposts

Ventward Lodge — pressure observation and marked pass closure at the outer rim (p2-settlement-01-ventward-lodge). Watch staff rotate from the capital; visitors can rest without military allegiance. Place a visible refuge marker before the exposed approach, with shelter, a manual warning board, a maintenance locker and a return path. Its job is warning and continuity, not a toll wall that seals the realm.

## Architecture

Use p2-arch-01-kiln-courts, grounded in REF-113 and art-guide-01. Foundation motif: Obsidian basalt terraces, vented foundry towers and lava-lit channels. Refinement: Heavy buttressed shells enclose ventilated work courts, with replaceable ceramic roofs and external cooling galleries. Public buildings display repair seams as civic records rather than hiding all wear behind black spikes. Structural section: stable basalt foundation → drained service crawl → inhabited rim ledge → vented kiln roofs; active vents remain outside foundations. Ornament follows load, use and wear; new engineering dimensions remain review targets, and recovered images do not supply measurements.

## Infrastructure

ore and fuel enter at Cinder Gate; food crosses the outer-rim road to Cool Courts; spent quench water stays in a closed return; Quench Gallery lies below the kiln aprons, and inspected mechanical pumps return cooled process water uphill through a separate closed pipe. Provide physically separate potable water, process/dirty return and storm overflow paths, with inspectable crossings; source and outfall must be checked against CONTINENT_GEOGRAPHY.md and the baseline watershed. Solid waste moves to a bounded service site, never a magical void. Manual signs and emergency stores remain when special infrastructure shuts down. Detailed capital service and exit graphs are in CAPITAL_LAYOUT_REGISTRY.json.

## Civilian

Baseline social purpose: Shared ovens reduce household fuel costs; shift meals and apprentice adoption networks organize neighborhoods. The covenant asks for maintenance service proportional to furnace access, with civic alternatives for people unable to work heat shifts. Spatial refinement gives everyday work a visible home: Cool Courts contains kitchens, rest courts, accessible housing entries and a public route to Cistern Forum. People disagree through hearings and bargaining, not a monolithic elemental personality. Visitors and dissenters retain food, care and routes.

## Commercial

Kiln exports durable tools and refractory components, importing Fault ore, Tidal cooling expertise and Graft food. Wartime demand can raise workshop income while worsening civilian tool access, so prosperity and public welfare diverge visibly. Imports food, ore, cooling expertise land at Cinder Gate; exports tools, refractory ceramics, specialist heat work pass through Kiln Aprons. Shared repair counters and the exchange settlement absorb downtime. Display inventory shortages as delayed contracts with substitute ordinary services, never as blanket disappearance of supplies.

## Military

Crews defend roads and quench lines before fortifications. Siege units require screened fuel stores, engineers and retreat lanes, making scorched-earth tactics a failure of logistics and legitimacy even when militarily effective. Spatial delta: gate watch/refuge p2-poi-01-watch-refuge has separate civilian and equipment entrances, protected stores and a public-facing complaint board. Defenses face the approach; no artillery or discharge arc crosses housing or the service loop. Rotating guards walk gate → relief store → watch point → rest, and are not permanent combat crowds.

## Institution

Workshop assemblies allocate furnace time; a basin council sets evacuation and river-temperature limits. Owners may appeal quotas, but no property title overrides a posted volcanic exclusion zone. Put accountable hearings in Cistern Forum and specialist instruction in Survey Crown. Enterable p2-poi-01-public-record-room contains a public record desk, private consultation niche and service exit. Access disputes produce alternate evidence routes; Oath binding never becomes a library card or care requirement.

## Sacred

Open Crucible Alcove in Cistern Forum is a voluntary sacred/commemorative Oath space: voluntary remembrance of failed works and public repair promises; cooled broken ceramics are displayed with their makers’ consent. It occupies an accessible alcove or court inside the existing civic district, so it adds a distinct purpose without inventing another settlement. The ordinary public entrance remains open to unbound visitors, observers and dissenters; no prayer, ritual, ancestry, faction standing or Oath binding is required for civic services. A quiet side exit and nonparticipatory route preserve choice. Objects left here are authored story props, not automatic transfers of player possessions.

## Underground

stable basalt foundation → drained service crawl → inhabited rim ledge → vented kiln roofs; active vents remain outside foundations. Separate civic maintenance from dungeon occupancy at signed vestibules. The underground plan is dry inspection stair → flooded return-gallery overlook → inactive kiln hall → evidence office → outward rescue adit; dry/ordinary access and independent exit are part of the blockout. Flood, smoke, collapse and anomaly envelopes cannot span every exit at once. Where deep geology is unsuitable, use a shallow covered service route rather than inventing a vast cavern.

## Landmarks

Place these at decision points so silhouette, sign shapes and street logic work without saturated VFX. Great monuments never hide essential navigation behind mandatory camera rotation.

## Habitats

Heat-tolerant scrub occupies cooled terraces; burrowers disperse mineral salts and nest beyond active vents. Slag-backed burrowers become hostile when dumping blocks nesting channels, creating a sanitation conflict rather than an evil species. Protect Saltburrow Terraces (p2-poi-01-habitat): cooled scrub and mineral-dispersing burrowers outside vent plumes. Map shelter, feeding edge, breeding refuge and migration pinch separately in the terrain blockout. Wildlife routine shifts between shelter and forage; warnings precede defensive behavior. Event recovery restores passage rather than permanently exterminating a biome.

## Resources

Paleclay Bench (p2-poi-01-resource): refractory clay pits with erosion bunds above the clean-water intake. Strip narrow clay cells, stabilize the previous cell, then reopen; extraction never enters live vents. Place a safe observation approach, working cells and an independent supply exit. Resource availability has healthy, stressed, closed and recovering presentation; local stock and yield numbers remain unresolved and must agree with the simulation plan.

## Worldboss

p2-poi-01-worldboss: A migrating ash-fed guardian may obstruct multiple evacuation roads during an eruption. The public encounter rewards redirecting it toward an unoccupied mineral field; lethal resolution is possible but loses its soil-renewal service. Refinement locates the footprint next to the habitat rather than inside the capital. Admission signs mark the active volume; refuges, distant viewing and relief logistics remain outside. Combat, redirection and repair roles share the encounter, with readable stand-down and recovery. If the event overlaps the public catchment, authority resolves to one bounded state, never additive hazards on all roads.

## Housing

p2-poi-01-housing is the stable entry at Cool Courts. Housing clusters around shared cool courts outside hazard corridors. Players can customize kilns and ceramics, but a protected workshop lease cannot block evacuation; relocation after an event preserves ownership and stored belongings. Proposed sample interior: 12 × 10 m clear room envelope plus 4 × 3 m entrance/storage vestibule, sized for blockout tests rather than a promised entitlement. Housing type uses local kit p2-arch-01-kiln-courts; a street-facing recovery point preserves entry if event overlays close the exterior. No placement may obstruct public circulation, private storage permissions or the second exit.

## Events

Preserve Cindervault service catchment (poi-01-event) and world-event-01: A caldera-pressure event asks players to clear vents, move residents and choose a temporary spillway. Combat, engineering and civilian transport contribute separately; failure triggers recoverable road damage and relief work, not erased homes. Warning boards at arrival and the outpost direct civilians to p2-district-01-cinder-gate. Event bounds exclude the ordinary service loop; service staff relocate before an affected counter closes. Recovery works are optional contributions with automatic restoration when players leave. See WORLD_ECOLOGY_SYSTEM.md and NPC_CIVILIZATION_SYSTEM.md for shared-state coordination.

## Hazards

Wet western winds meet volcanic heat, producing steam fog and ash rain. Eruption warnings combine ground vibration, flag patterns and audible bells; fire immunity never substitutes for oxygen or clear evacuation routes. Hazard ownership stays local to authored work/encounter volumes. Use observable warning → preparation → closure → recovery; failing staff or player attendance triggers a safe fallback, not permanent damage. Hazard resistance cannot substitute for air, footing, clear access or consent.

## Weather

Baseline weather: wet windward slopes with rain shadows inside calderas. Ash rain closes exposed kilns first; clean-water stores are covered before an outward evacuation. Border blending follows elevation, exposure and water; magic modifies local authored conditions only. Night and weather combinations must be checked at all marked crossings; no mandatory color-only, audio-only or glare-based cue is acceptable.

## Daynight

Dawn ash checks open work courts; midday heat pauses shift commerce into shaded galleries; night repair stalls operate at Ashbridge. Trading and household routines differ from patrols; essential food, care, stable housing and return services persist through rotating staff or an accessible fallback at Cinder Gate. Do not use a whole-city curfew as an unannounced progress lock.

## Ambient

Piston breaths, hand-hammer call-and-response and changing furnace draught communicate state. Evacuation alarms use a distinct tempo and tactile caption so musical intensity cannot mask an actionable safety signal. Blockout sound zones: working bed at Kiln Aprons, conversational bed at Cistern Forum, quieter domestic bed at Cool Courts. Use occlusion boundaries before adding loops. Alert captions, readable devices and separate intensity controls survive weather and combat mixes.

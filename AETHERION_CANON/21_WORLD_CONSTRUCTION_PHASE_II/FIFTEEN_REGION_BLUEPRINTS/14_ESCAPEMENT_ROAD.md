# ESCAPEMENT ROAD major realm — Phase II spatial blueprint

Status: PROPOSED. Sources: P2-SRC-FOUNDATION, P2-SRC-DIRECTIVE, P2-SRC-PROPOSAL. All new dimensions, names and spatial arrangements need review. This is a refinement, not a replacement dossier or functioning level.

Baseline: [14 ESCAPEMENT ROAD](https://github.com/jrmoyler/Aetherion-The-Fifteen-Oaths/tree/5dce9edae0bb3587eb7c72433395e7d5a19857ea/AETHERION_CANON/04_OATH_DOSSIERS/14_ESCAPEMENT_ROAD.md); IDs region-14, oath-14, poi-14-capital.

## Terrain

southeastern inland transport terraces; low rolling steppe and graded river corridors. Climate: seasonal drought with intense rain and flash floods. Drainage remains basin-dawn. Place the capital as linear junction city with grade-separated express and resting loops, with river-terrace road forks around a central walkable timekeeping hall. Transition through pasture and roadside settlements approach humid coastal industry; do not paint a hard elemental border. Continent adjacency stays region-11, region-12, region-13, region-15; see WORLD_REGION_GRAPH.json. No new river may reverse a baseline basin or cross a divide without review.

## Capital

Wayclock (poi-14-capital) keeps its baseline identity: Wayclock occupies a broad junction of roads, workshops and public timekeeping halls. Fast lanes remain physically separated from homes and markets, with conventional walkable routes that never require a mount or temporal ability. The detailed layout is [capital blueprint](../CAPITAL_CITY_BLUEPRINTS/14_WAYCLOCK.md); its civic, working and household paths are independently legible. City envelope target 2100 × 900 m with 20 m local relief is a first blockout hypothesis, not approved geography.

## Settlements

Restmile (poi-14-settlement) remains the existing exchange settlement: Restmile is a service town with sheltered stops, repair yards and accessible boarding. It argues that pauses are infrastructure rather than inefficiency, particularly for families, animals and crews who cannot live at express tempo. Its role is retained rather than renamed to fill a checklist. SETTLEMENT_REGISTRY.json distinguishes independent settlements from capital districts. The added food/craft village and safety outpost address provisioning and last-mile relief; additional towns require demand, terrain and route evidence before proposal.

## Villages

Brakefield — fodder, wheel wood and rest pasture between long road stages (p2-settlement-14-brakefield). Site on a viable ordinary spur near the exchange settlement, downstream of no contaminated intake and outside the boss footprint. A shared store, wash point, household cluster and cart turning court support its livelihood; field-to-store-to-market is its daily loop. Interior and household counts remain unresolved.

## Outposts

Long Pause — bridge watch and conventional-route refuge beside a temporal corridor (p2-settlement-14-long-pause). Watch staff rotate from the capital; visitors can rest without military allegiance. Place a visible refuge marker before the exposed approach, with shelter, a manual warning board, a maintenance locker and a return path. Its job is warning and continuity, not a toll wall that seals the realm.

## Architecture

Use p2-arch-14-escapement-stations, grounded in REF-099 and art-guide-14. Foundation motif: Elevated clockwork highways, kinetic arch gates and temporal workshop bays. Refinement: Long low canopies, modular viaducts and exposed service galleries communicate continuous movement. Moving architecture has locked safe states, guarded pinch points and alternate passages for people unable to use kinetic shortcuts. Structural section: drained river terrace → slow public street → enclosed express cutting and bridge crossings → ordinary clock halls; local temporal envelopes do not alter the public global clock. Ornament follows load, use and wear; new engineering dimensions remain review targets, and recovered images do not supply measurements.

## Infrastructure

caravans unload at Rest Gate; express transfers use an isolated cut; relief cargo crosses through Relief Siding at grade-separated bridges. Provide physically separate potable water, process/dirty return and storm overflow paths, with inspectable crossings; source and outfall must be checked against CONTINENT_GEOGRAPHY.md and the baseline watershed. Solid waste moves to a bounded service site, never a magical void. Manual signs and emergency stores remain when special infrastructure shuts down. Detailed capital service and exit graphs are in CAPITAL_LAYOUT_REGISTRY.json.

## Civilian

Baseline social purpose: Communal meals follow arrivals rather than one universal clock, and public rest is a respected contribution to safety. Covenant duty includes returning borrowed motion through maintenance, not submitting every hour of life to a timetable. Spatial refinement gives everyday work a visible home: Pause Gardens contains kitchens, rest courts, accessible housing entries and a public route to Public Timehall. People disagree through hearings and bargaining, not a monolithic elemental personality. Visitors and dissenters retain food, care and routes.

## Commercial

Escapement exports transport and precision motion components while importing fuel, food and structural materials. Express profits can starve local stops, so route subsidies and maintenance debt shape the regional economy visibly. Imports fuel, food, structural materials land at Rest Gate; exports transport, vehicles, precision motion parts pass through Repair Mile. Shared repair counters and the exchange settlement absorb downtime. Display inventory shortages as delayed contracts with substitute ordinary services, never as blanket disappearance of supplies.

## Military

Forces defend route continuity, repair capacity and safe retreat. Rapid redeployment has supply and recovery needs; temporal tactics cannot retroactively erase civilian harm or make an unannounced attack impossible to anticipate. Spatial delta: gate watch/refuge p2-poi-14-watch-refuge has separate civilian and equipment entrances, protected stores and a public-facing complaint board. Defenses face the approach; no artillery or discharge arc crosses housing or the service loop. Rotating guards walk gate → relief store → watch point → rest, and are not permanent combat crowds.

## Institution

Route communes manage local roads, while a timetable assembly coordinates long-distance service. Rest guarantees and public detours limit central scheduling authority; emergency accelerations require a stated end condition and review. Put accountable hearings in Public Timehall and specialist instruction in Route College. Enterable p2-poi-14-public-record-room contains a public record desk, private consultation niche and service exit. Access disputes produce alternate evidence routes; Oath binding never becomes a library card or care requirement.

## Sacred

Still Minute Shelter in Public Timehall is a voluntary sacred/commemorative Oath space: a voluntary pause for absent travelers and rest labor; its ordinary clock continues and no temporal trap is created. It occupies an accessible alcove or court inside the existing civic district, so it adds a distinct purpose without inventing another settlement. The ordinary public entrance remains open to unbound visitors, observers and dissenters; no prayer, ritual, ancestry, faction standing or Oath binding is required for civic services. A quiet side exit and nonparticipatory route preserve choice. Objects left here are authored story props, not automatic transfers of player possessions.

## Underground

drained river terrace → slow public street → enclosed express cutting and bridge crossings → ordinary clock halls; local temporal envelopes do not alter the public global clock. Separate civic maintenance from dungeon occupancy at signed vestibules. The underground plan is present-day siding → repeating repair bay → trapped traveler shelter → accident record room → conventional terrace exit; dry/ordinary access and independent exit are part of the blockout. Flood, smoke, collapse and anomaly envelopes cannot span every exit at once. Where deep geology is unsuitable, use a shallow covered service route rather than inventing a vast cavern.

## Landmarks

Primary skyline cue: Elevated clockwork highways, kinetic arch gates and temporal workshop bays. Secondary local anchors are Public Timehall, Route College and the clearly marked outpost refuge. Place these at decision points so silhouette, sign shapes and street logic work without saturated VFX. Great monuments never hide essential navigation behind mandatory camera rotation.

## Habitats

Steppe longrunners migrate along seasonal forage routes that highways can sever. Road crews maintain wildlife crossings, making faster transport answerable to ecological continuity rather than automatically superior to older paths. Protect River Restlands (p2-poi-14-habitat): grazing and migratory crossing under protected slow bridges. Map shelter, feeding edge, breeding refuge and migration pinch separately in the terrain blockout. Wildlife routine shifts between shelter and forage; warnings precede defensive behavior. Event recovery restores passage rather than permanently exterminating a biome.

## Resources

Wheelgrove (p2-poi-14-resource): managed timber and repair metal from retired route segments. Recovery loops cannot duplicate salvage; repair inventories are keyed to persistent present-day state. Place a safe observation approach, working cells and an independent supply exit. Resource availability has healthy, stressed, closed and recovering presentation; local stock and yield numbers remain unresolved and must agree with the simulation plan.

## Dungeon

The Returned Mile (poi-14-dungeon) remains: The Returned Mile is an abandoned transport segment where unfinished maintenance repeats. Players distinguish echoes from present residents, repair actual infrastructure and choose which recorded account of the accident enters public history. New spatial sequence: present-day siding → repeating repair bay → trapped traveler shelter → accident record room → conventional terrace exit. Foundation encounter context: The Final Schedule is a living transport marshal commanding a relief convoy through a failing temporal corridor. Stopping to rescue trapped travelers risks losing urgent supplies elsewhere; pressing on abandons people nearby. The encounter can become a nonlethal confrontation, but its resolution requires a costly redistribution of time, transport and care.

Encounter identity: The Final Schedule is a situational title for Jalen Rusk (character-14-leader), not an unregistered second person or a requirement to kill the leader. ## Worldboss

Refinement locates the footprint next to the habitat rather than inside the capital. Admission signs mark the active volume; refuges, distant viewing and relief logistics remain outside. ## Events

Recovery works are optional contributions with automatic restoration when players leave. See WORLD_ECOLOGY_SYSTEM.md and NPC_CIVILIZATION_SYSTEM.md for shared-state coordination.

## Hazards

Seasonal drought, flash flooding and local time stutters constrain travel. Temporal anomalies have marked boundaries and consistent entry/exit behavior; ordinary weather and fatigue continue to matter outside them. Hazard ownership stays local to authored work/encounter volumes. Use observable warning → preparation → closure → recovery; failing staff or player attendance triggers a safe fallback, not permanent damage. Hazard resistance cannot substitute for air, footing, clear access or consent.

## Weather

Baseline weather: seasonal drought with intense rain and flash floods. River rise closes low transfer decks; manual terrace detour bypasses temporal corridors and preserves boarding. Border blending follows elevation, exposure and water; magic modifies local authored conditions only. Night and weather combinations must be checked at all marked crossings; no mandatory color-only, audio-only or glare-based cue is acceptable.

## Daynight

Dawn convoy inspection; midday staggered rest and repairs; night quiet routing uses reduced express slots with manual fallback. Trading and household routines differ from patrols; essential food, care, stable housing and return services persist through rotating staff or an accessible fallback at Rest Gate. Do not use a whole-city curfew as an unannounced progress lock.

## Ambient

Road rhythms, distinct clock rates and long mechanical breaths create movement without constant frantic ticking. Temporal hazards have visual phase indicators, and audio can be reduced without losing essential timing information. Blockout sound zones: working bed at Repair Mile, conversational bed at Public Timehall, quieter domestic bed at Pause Gardens. Use occlusion boundaries before adding loops. Alert captions, readable devices and separate intensity controls survive weather and combat mixes.

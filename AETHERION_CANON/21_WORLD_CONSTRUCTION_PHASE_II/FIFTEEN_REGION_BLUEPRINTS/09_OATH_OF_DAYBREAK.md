# OATH OF DAYBREAK major realm — Phase II spatial blueprint

Status: PROPOSED. Sources: P2-SRC-FOUNDATION, P2-SRC-DIRECTIVE, P2-SRC-PROPOSAL. All new dimensions, names and spatial arrangements need review. This is a refinement, not a replacement dossier or functioning level.

Baseline: [09 OATH OF DAYBREAK](https://github.com/jrmoyler/Aetherion-The-Fifteen-Oaths/tree/5dce9edae0bb3587eb7c72433395e7d5a19857ea/AETHERION_CANON/04_OATH_DOSSIERS/09_OATH_OF_DAYBREAK.md); IDs region-09, oath-09, poi-09-capital.

## Terrain

central temperate basin; broad river terraces between highland approaches. Climate: moderate continental basin with seasonal fog and summer heat. Drainage remains basin-dawn. Place the capital as civic plaza and radial arcades crossed by an independent refuge belt, with monumental stair axis intersects an accessible contour arcade. Transition through meadows and farms connect upland roads to southern river terraces; do not paint a hard elemental border. Continent adjacency stays region-03, region-05, region-06, region-08, region-12, region-13; see WORLD_REGION_GRAPH.json. No new river may reverse a baseline basin or cross a divide without review.

## Settlements

## Villages

Site on a viable ordinary spur near the exchange settlement, downstream of no contaminated intake and outside the boss footprint. A shared store, wash point, household cluster and cart turning court support its livelihood; field-to-store-to-market is its daily loop. Interior and household counts remain unresolved.

## Outposts

Watch staff rotate from the capital; visitors can rest without military allegiance. Place a visible refuge marker before the exposed approach, with shelter, a manual warning board, a maintenance locker and a return path. Its job is warning and continuity, not a toll wall that seals the realm.

## Architecture

## Civilian

Baseline social purpose: Public meals, open petitions and care shifts define civic participation. Covenant duty is to offer protection without demanding confession, with institutional disputes over whether that ideal can survive security threats. Spatial refinement gives everyday work a visible home: Lantern Homes contains kitchens, rest courts, accessible housing entries and a public route to Great Plaza. People disagree through hearings and bargaining, not a monolithic elemental personality. Visitors and dissenters retain food, care and routes.

## Commercial

Daybreak exports sanctuary services, arbitration and precision light work while importing food and construction materials. Donor dependence can distort protection priorities, so transparent budgets matter as much as luminous ideals. Imports food, stone, medical materials land at Basin Gate; exports sanctuary, arbitration, precision light work pass through Makers Arcade. Shared repair counters and the exchange settlement absorb downtime. Display inventory shortages as delayed contracts with substitute ordinary services, never as blanket disappearance of supplies.

## Military

Wardens defend evacuation corridors and civilian gathering points, using proportional force and documented custody. Military leaders may demand emergency exceptions, but the narrative preserves consequences for turning sanctuary into coercive occupation. Spatial delta: gate watch/refuge p2-poi-09-watch-refuge has separate civilian and equipment entrances, protected stores and a public-facing complaint board. Defenses face the approach; no artillery or discharge arc crosses housing or the service loop. Rotating guards walk gate → relief store → watch point → rest, and are not permanent combat crowds.

## Institution

Sanctuary councils administer care while civic benches adjudicate disputes with documented appeals. Wardens answer to both, making delay possible but also preventing a single charismatic protector from legally owning every decision. Put accountable hearings in Great Plaza and specialist instruction in Appeal Court. Enterable p2-poi-09-public-record-room contains a public record desk, private consultation niche and service exit. Access disputes produce alternate evidence routes; Oath binding never becomes a library card or care requirement.

## Sacred

Dawn Witness Alcove in Great Plaza is a voluntary sacred/commemorative Oath space: an opt-in commemorative place acknowledging the plaza’s locked narrative loss without inventing a new version of the Archivist’s death. It occupies an accessible alcove or court inside the existing civic district, so it adds a distinct purpose without inventing another settlement. The ordinary public entrance remains open to unbound visitors, observers and dissenters; no prayer, ritual, ancestry, faction standing or Oath binding is required for civic services. A quiet side exit and nonparticipatory route preserve choice. Objects left here are authored story props, not automatic transfers of player possessions.

## Underground

Flood, smoke, collapse and anomaly envelopes cannot span every exit at once. Where deep geology is unsuitable, use a shallow covered service route rather than inventing a vast cavern.

## Habitats

Urban birds and pollinator gardens support ordinary life, while excessive ward brightness can disrupt nocturnal wildlife and neighboring residents. Protect Basin Meadow (p2-poi-09-habitat): pollinator strips and ordinary grazing outside ward perimeter. Map shelter, feeding edge, breeding refuge and migration pinch separately in the terrain blockout. Wildlife routine shifts between shelter and forage; warnings precede defensive behavior. Event recovery restores passage rather than permanently exterminating a biome.

## Hazards

A moderate basin climate brings seasonal fog, summer heat and flood risk. Magical radiance does not abolish night or weather; ward failures reveal dependence on maintenance rather than proving that residents were morally unworthy. Hazard ownership stays local to authored work/encounter volumes. Use observable warning → preparation → closure → recovery; failing staff or player attendance triggers a safe fallback, not permanent damage. Hazard resistance cannot substitute for air, footing, clear access or consent.

## Weather

Baseline weather: moderate continental basin with seasonal fog and summer heat. Flooded lower approaches divert to the contour arcade; ward glare and fog never erase ground signage. Border blending follows elevation, exposure and water; magic modifies local authored conditions only. Night and weather combinations must be checked at all marked crossings; no mandatory color-only, audio-only or glare-based cue is acceptable.

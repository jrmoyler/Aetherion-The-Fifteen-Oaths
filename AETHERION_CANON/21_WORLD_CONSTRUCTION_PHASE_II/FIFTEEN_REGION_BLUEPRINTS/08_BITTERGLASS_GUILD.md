# BITTERGLASS GUILD major realm — Phase II spatial blueprint

Status: PROPOSED. Sources: P2-SRC-FOUNDATION, P2-SRC-DIRECTIVE, P2-SRC-PROPOSAL. All new dimensions, names and spatial arrangements need review. This is a refinement, not a replacement dossier or functioning level.

Baseline: [08 BITTERGLASS GUILD](https://github.com/jrmoyler/Aetherion-The-Fifteen-Oaths/tree/5dce9edae0bb3587eb7c72433395e7d5a19857ea/AETHERION_CANON/04_OATH_DOSSIERS/08_BITTERGLASS_GUILD.md); IDs region-08, oath-08, poi-08-capital.

## Terrain

southwestern treatment wetlands; low-gradient inland channels above tidal influence. Climate: humid seasonal floodplain with stagnant-air episodes. Drainage remains basin-choir. Place the capital as compartmented wetland pavilions on a clean-air boardwalk grid, with clean upstream intake to monitored downstream treatment; dry cross-grid separates flows. Transition through terraced drainage becomes managed marsh, then brackish estuary; do not paint a hard elemental border. Continent adjacency stays region-04, region-05, region-07, region-09, region-12; see WORLD_REGION_GRAPH.json. No new river may reverse a baseline basin or cross a divide without review.

## Capital

Vitrial (poi-08-capital) keeps its baseline identity: Vitrial combines sealed greenhouse districts with open civic air corridors. Vent stacks, wash stations and independent sampling kiosks are visible civic infrastructure, preventing the poison realm from becoming an implausibly uninhabitable toxic city. The detailed layout is [capital blueprint](../CAPITAL_CITY_BLUEPRINTS/08_VITRIAL.md); its civic, working and household paths are independently legible. City envelope target 1400 × 1000 m with 10 m local relief is a first blockout hypothesis, not approved geography.

## Settlements

Clearfen (poi-08-settlement) remains the existing exchange settlement: Clearfen is a treatment town where clean channels and contaminated channels are physically separated and labeled. Its workers want economic security without concealing the fact that their health and land bear regional costs. Its role is retained rather than renamed to fill a checklist. SETTLEMENT_REGISTRY.json distinguishes independent settlements from capital districts. The added food/craft village and safety outpost address provisioning and last-mile relief; additional towns require demand, terrain and route evidence before proposal.

## Villages

Sedgehold — basketry, clean-water gardens and monitored reed filtration work (p2-settlement-08-sedgehold). Site on a viable ordinary spur near the exchange settlement, downstream of no contaminated intake and outside the boss footprint. A shared store, wash point, household cluster and cart turning court support its livelihood; field-to-store-to-market is its daily loop. Interior and household counts remain unresolved.

## Outposts

Whitepost Station — upstream sample watch and emergency clean-water transfer (p2-settlement-08-whitepost-station). Watch staff rotate from the capital; visitors can rest without military allegiance. Place a visible refuge marker before the exposed approach, with shelter, a manual warning board, a maintenance locker and a return path. Its job is warning and continuity, not a toll wall that seals the realm.

## Infrastructure

provisions stay on dry clean boardwalk; waste enters sealed service lanes; sampled output waits in labeled holding tanks; treatment cannot magically erase unlimited load. Provide physically separate potable water, process/dirty return and storm overflow paths, with inspectable crossings; source and outfall must be checked against CONTINENT_GEOGRAPHY.md and the baseline watershed. Solid waste moves to a bounded service site, never a magical void. Manual signs and emergency stores remain when special infrastructure shuts down. Detailed capital service and exit graphs are in CAPITAL_LAYOUT_REGISTRY.json.

## Civilian

Baseline social purpose: Public assay days, shared washhouses and transparent labeling are civic rituals. Covenant duty is disclosure of known risks and participation in remediation, with protection for whistleblowers rather than compulsory self-experimentation. Spatial refinement gives everyday work a visible home: Dry Fen Homes contains kitchens, rest courts, accessible housing entries and a public route to Open Air Court. People disagree through hearings and bargaining, not a monolithic elemental personality. Visitors and dissenters retain food, care and routes.

## Commercial

Bitterglass exports medicines, reagents and testing services while importing fuel and food. Open formulas reduce monopoly rents but require replacement funding for quality assurance, creating a concrete cost for an apparently easy reform. Imports fuel, food, precision vessels land at Clean Gate; exports medicines, testing, glass, reagents pass through Still Rows. Shared repair counters and the exchange settlement absorb downtime. Display inventory shortages as delayed contracts with substitute ordinary services, never as blanket disappearance of supplies.

## Military

Guild forces isolate contaminated areas and disrupt supply lines with controlled tools. Chemical warfare creates lasting political consequences and relief obligations; civilian poisoning is not normalized as a clever low-cost tactic. Spatial delta: gate watch/refuge p2-poi-08-watch-refuge has separate civilian and equipment entrances, protected stores and a public-facing complaint board. Defenses face the approach; no artillery or discharge arc crosses housing or the service loop. Rotating guards walk gate → relief store → watch point → rest, and are not permanent combat crowds.

## Institution

Laboratory houses hold a guild charter, but resident councils and independent testers can challenge permits. The dramatic conflict is whether affected communities can fund evidence when the accused laboratories own most instruments. Put accountable hearings in Open Air Court and specialist instruction in Witness Laboratory. Enterable p2-poi-08-public-record-room contains a public record desk, private consultation niche and service exit. Access disputes produce alternate evidence routes; Oath binding never becomes a library card or care requirement.

## Sacred

Clear Vessel Niche in Open Air Court is a voluntary sacred/commemorative Oath space: a symbolic rinsed-glass remembrance display for care labor and preventable harms; no ingestion or chemical rite. It occupies an accessible alcove or court inside the existing civic district, so it adds a distinct purpose without inventing another settlement. The ordinary public entrance remains open to unbound visitors, observers and dissenters; no prayer, ritual, ancestry, faction standing or Oath binding is required for civic services. A quiet side exit and nonparticipatory route preserve choice. Objects left here are authored story props, not automatic transfers of player possessions.

## Underground

wetland soil and piles → isolated drainage trays → dry public deck → occupied pavilions → outward vent stacks; air intakes face away from process exhaust. Separate civic maintenance from dungeon occupancy at signed vestibules. The underground plan is wash vestibule → protected worker refuge → isolated still chamber → dependency archive → clean-side exit; dry/ordinary access and independent exit are part of the blockout. Flood, smoke, collapse and anomaly envelopes cannot span every exit at once. Where deep geology is unsuitable, use a shallow covered service route rather than inventing a vast cavern.

## Habitats

Filter-shelled crawlers concentrate minerals and require careful habitat management. Harvesting them for reagents can improve short-term treatment while reducing long-term filtration, giving gatherers ecological choices beyond rarity tiers. Protect Recovery Fen (p2-poi-08-habitat): wetland nesting cells buffered from contaminated service channels. Map shelter, feeding edge, breeding refuge and migration pinch separately in the terrain blockout. Wildlife routine shifts between shelter and forage; warnings precede defensive behavior. Event recovery restores passage rather than permanently exterminating a biome.

## Hazards

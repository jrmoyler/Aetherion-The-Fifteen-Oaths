# RIME ARCHIVE major realm — Phase II spatial blueprint

Status: PROPOSED. Sources: P2-SRC-FOUNDATION, P2-SRC-DIRECTIVE, P2-SRC-PROPOSAL. All new dimensions, names and spatial arrangements need review. This is a refinement, not a replacement dossier or functioning level.

Baseline: [02 RIME ARCHIVE](https://github.com/jrmoyler/Aetherion-The-Fifteen-Oaths/tree/5dce9edae0bb3587eb7c72433395e7d5a19857ea/AETHERION_CANON/04_OATH_DOSSIERS/02_RIME_ARCHIVE.md); IDs region-02, oath-02, poi-02-capital.

## Terrain

northwestern high headwaters; highest glacial catchments and sheltered rock-founded valleys. Climate: cold alpine with seasonal melt and whiteouts. Drainage remains basin-west. Place the capital as paired rock benches along a glacial valley, with valley-floor warm street with parallel rock-founded archive bench. Transition through snow line descends into mixed stone and conifer margins; do not paint a hard elemental border. Continent adjacency stays region-01, region-03, region-05; see WORLD_REGION_GRAPH.json. No new river may reverse a baseline basin or cross a divide without review.

## Capital

Stillfolio (poi-02-capital) keeps its baseline identity: Stillfolio terraces into a sheltered glacial valley on rock foundations. Libraries bridge around ice movement instead of assuming glaciers are permanent bedrock; evacuation stacks can be moved before a shelf becomes unsafe. The detailed layout is [capital blueprint](../CAPITAL_CITY_BLUEPRINTS/02_STILLFOLIO.md); its civic, working and household paths are independently legible. City envelope target 1200 × 700 m with 30 m local relief is a first blockout hypothesis, not approved geography.

## Settlements

Meltgate (poi-02-settlement) remains the existing exchange settlement: Meltgate is a seasonal exchange town where cold storage meets the warmer valley. It translates catalogues into common trade speech and gives non-archivists a place to challenge access decisions. Its role is retained rather than renamed to fill a checklist. SETTLEMENT_REGISTRY.json distinguishes independent settlements from capital districts. The added food/craft village and safety outpost address provisioning and last-mile relief; additional towns require demand, terrain and route evidence before proposal.

## Villages

Thawfield — short-season roots, fodder storage and runoff measurement below the snow line (p2-settlement-02-thawfield). Site on a viable ordinary spur near the exchange settlement, downstream of no contaminated intake and outside the boss footprint. A shared store, wash point, household cluster and cart turning court support its livelihood; field-to-store-to-market is its daily loop. Interior and household counts remain unresolved.

## Outposts

Blueflag Refuge — whiteout shelter and ice-dam observation on exposed approach (p2-settlement-02-blueflag-refuge). Watch staff rotate from the capital; visitors can rest without military allegiance. Place a visible refuge marker before the exposed approach, with shelter, a manual warning board, a maintenance locker and a return path. Its job is warning and continuity, not a toll wall that seals the realm.

## Architecture

Use p2-arch-02-rime-vaults, grounded in REF-112 and art-guide-02. Foundation motif: Glacial vault shelves, ice pillars and dark stone archive thresholds. Refinement: Insulated stone shells surround blue-white ice vaults, with narrow vestibules and warm public rooms. Outer ice is replaceable environmental architecture; manuscript rooms and occupied structures need credible humidity and thermal separation. Structural section: bedrock anchors → frost-drained undercroft → warm occupied rooms → ventilated cold store; no load-bearing glacial ice. Ornament follows load, use and wear; new engineering dimensions remain review targets, and recovered images do not supply measurements.

## Infrastructure

Melt Exchange decants food into insulated carts; Rock Stacks uses removable storage modules; fuel carts never cross patient rooms. Provide physically separate potable water, process/dirty return and storm overflow paths, with inspectable crossings; source and outfall must be checked against CONTINENT_GEOGRAPHY.md and the baseline watershed. Solid waste moves to a bounded service site, never a magical void. Manual signs and emergency stores remain when special infrastructure shuts down. Detailed capital service and exit graphs are in CAPITAL_LAYOUT_REGISTRY.json.

## Civilian

Baseline social purpose: Households share warm reading rooms, preserved food and copying work. Civic duty is care for an entrusted record or vulnerable person; people may serve through translation or cooking without possessing elite magical literacy. Spatial refinement gives everyday work a visible home: Hearth Shelves contains kitchens, rest courts, accessible housing entries and a public route to Warm Index. People disagree through hearings and bargaining, not a monolithic elemental personality. Visitors and dissenters retain food, care and routes.

## Commercial

Rime exports preservation and reliable historical copies while importing food, insulation and heating tools. Paid premium access funds conservation but risks making public memory a luxury, which civic contracts can address. Imports food, insulation, heating tools land at Melt Exchange; exports preservation, records, cold-chain services pass through Binding Row. Shared repair counters and the exchange settlement absorb downtime. Display inventory shortages as delayed contracts with substitute ordinary services, never as blanket disappearance of supplies.

## Military

Defense delays attackers and protects evacuation of people and irreplaceable records. Guards prioritize containment with reviewable release conditions; indefinite imprisonment remains a disputed abuse rather than the default reward for institutional loyalty. Spatial delta: gate watch/refuge p2-poi-02-watch-refuge has separate civilian and equipment entrances, protected stores and a public-facing complaint board. Defenses face the approach; no artillery or discharge arc crosses housing or the service loop. Rotating guards walk gate → relief store → watch point → rest, and are not permanent combat crowds.

## Institution

Collection councils separate custody, interpretation and public access decisions. Emergency seals expire unless independently renewed; the conflict is who can realistically challenge custodians when challengers lack the sealed evidence. Put accountable hearings in Warm Index and specialist instruction in Rock Stacks. Enterable p2-poi-02-public-record-room contains a public record desk, private consultation niche and service exit. Access disputes produce alternate evidence routes; Oath binding never becomes a library card or care requirement.

## Sacred

Vigil of the Unread Page in Warm Index is a voluntary sacred/commemorative Oath space: a warm remembrance room where families may leave an unrecorded page; silence and declining preservation are respected. It occupies an accessible alcove or court inside the existing civic district, so it adds a distinct purpose without inventing another settlement. The ordinary public entrance remains open to unbound visitors, observers and dissenters; no prayer, ritual, ancestry, faction standing or Oath binding is required for civic services. A quiet side exit and nonparticipatory route preserve choice. Objects left here are authored story props, not automatic transfers of player possessions.

## Underground

bedrock anchors → frost-drained undercroft → warm occupied rooms → ventilated cold store; no load-bearing glacial ice. Separate civic maintenance from dungeon occupancy at signed vestibules. The underground plan is public catalog room → displaced trolley siding → cold specimen hall → contested testimony chamber → rock-side transfer door; dry/ordinary access and independent exit are part of the blockout. Flood, smoke, collapse and anomaly envelopes cannot span every exit at once. Where deep geology is unsuitable, use a shallow covered service route rather than inventing a vast cavern.

## Landmarks

Primary skyline cue: Glacial vault shelves, ice pillars and dark stone archive thresholds. Secondary local anchors are Warm Index, Rock Stacks and the clearly marked outpost refuge. Place these at decision points so silhouette, sign shapes and street logic work without saturated VFX. Great monuments never hide essential navigation behind mandatory camera rotation.

## Routes

Retain baseline ordinary pack routes to region-01, region-03, region-05 and the route IDs listed in TRANSPORT_ROUTE_REGISTRY.json / baseline TRADE_ROUTES.json. Local structure: neighbor approach → p2-settlement-02-blueflag-refuge → poi-02-settlement → poi-02-capital; p2-settlement-02-thawfield uses a service spur. This ordering is functional, not surveyed distance. Capital public loop and housing-side relief exit remain outside events. Optional Oath traversal can shorten a route only after its ordinary counterpart works.

## Habitats

Lichen and sheltered fungi feed rime-antler grazers that browse thaw margins. Seasonal migration signals changing snowpack; predators approach towns when sealed roads interrupt that movement, not because frost creatures are inherently cruel. Protect Moraine Quiet (p2-poi-02-habitat): conifer margins and seasonal grazer passage kept clear of ice-dam outlets. Map shelter, feeding edge, breeding refuge and migration pinch separately in the terrain blockout. Wildlife routine shifts between shelter and forage; warnings precede defensive behavior. Event recovery restores passage rather than permanently exterminating a biome.

## Resources

Feltwater Meadows (p2-poi-02-resource): renewable insulation fibers and spring water gathered without disturbing frozen stores. Meadow harvest rotates around nesting and melt windows; cold-chain quality is a logistical state, not infinite preservation. Place a safe observation approach, working cells and an independent supply exit. Resource availability has healthy, stressed, closed and recovering presentation; local stock and yield numbers remain unresolved and must agree with the simulation plan.

## Dungeon

The Uncatalogued Vault (poi-02-dungeon) remains: The Uncatalogued Vault stores material excluded from the public index, including conflicting Fracture testimony. It is an investigative dungeon with transport and containment encounters, not a room containing a single unquestionable exposition tablet. New spatial sequence: public catalog room → displaced trolley siding → cold specimen hall → contested testimony chamber → rock-side transfer door. Foundation encounter context: The Unthawing Custodian is a living senior archivist defending a patient who validly refused awakening, while an evacuation crisis makes continued stasis dangerous. A duel can secure time and access, but the resolution must preserve consent and fund a difficult transfer rather than expose a conveniently invalid order.

Encounter identity: The Unthawing Custodian is a situational title for Sera Pell (character-02-leader), not an unregistered second person or a requirement to kill the leader. See DUNGEON_AND_EXPLORATION_PLAN.md for admission, reset, co-op and exit rules; do not turn living title-holders into mandatory kill targets.

## Worldboss

p2-poi-02-worldboss: A glacial guardian follows an old migratory corridor through settled land during thaw. The public objective opens a safe passage and stabilizes ice bridges; combat is one component of a larger relocation effort. Refinement locates the footprint next to the habitat rather than inside the capital. Admission signs mark the active volume; refuges, distant viewing and relief logistics remain outside. Combat, redirection and repair roles share the encounter, with readable stand-down and recovery. If the event overlaps the public catchment, authority resolves to one bounded state, never additive hazards on all roads.

## Secret

Its reward is optional testimony, a cosmetic/story variant or local repair knowledge; it cannot hide a mandatory civic exit, main-story prerequisite without hints, or a protected resident identity. A safe return path is visible from the discovery chamber.

## Housing

p2-poi-02-housing is the stable entry at Hearth Shelves. Homes have warm inhabited cores and optional cold storage annexes. Housing progression expands workshops and shared reading access; players are not asked to maintain a fictional medical cryostasis system through punitive daily login fees. Proposed sample interior: 12 × 10 m clear room envelope plus 4 × 3 m entrance/storage vestibule, sized for blockout tests rather than a promised entitlement. Housing type uses local kit p2-arch-02-rime-vaults; a street-facing recovery point preserves entry if event overlays close the exterior. No placement may obstruct public circulation, private storage permissions or the second exit.

## Events

Preserve Stillfolio service catchment (poi-02-event) and world-event-02: A thaw-front event rescues residents and moves collections before an ice shelf breaks. Choosing which copies travel first changes available research services temporarily; originals have recovery quests rather than undocumented permanent deletion. Warning boards at arrival and the outpost direct civilians to p2-district-02-melt-exchange. Event bounds exclude the ordinary service loop; service staff relocate before an affected counter closes. Recovery works are optional contributions with automatic restoration when players leave. See WORLD_ECOLOGY_SYSTEM.md and NPC_CIVILIZATION_SYSTEM.md for shared-state coordination.

## Pvp

## Hazards

Persistent cold gives way to brief melt pulses, whiteouts and crevasse shifts. Slowing zones are covenant anomalies with marked boundaries, separate from ordinary hypothermia or the Time Oath's causality questions. Hazard ownership stays local to authored work/encounter volumes. Use observable warning → preparation → closure → recovery; failing staff or player attendance triggers a safe fallback, not permanent damage. Hazard resistance cannot substitute for air, footing, clear access or consent.

## Weather

Baseline weather: cold alpine with seasonal melt and whiteouts. Whiteout shuts scenic bridges; sheltered rock walk and refuge beacons retain a physical route. Border blending follows elevation, exposure and water; magic modifies local authored conditions only. Night and weather combinations must be checked at all marked crossings; no mandatory color-only, audio-only or glare-based cue is acceptable.

## Daynight

Morning thaw inspection changes porter routes; midday visitors use Warm Index; night cataloguing continues behind quiet partition doors. Trading and household routines differ from patrols; essential food, care, stable housing and return services persist through rotating staff or an accessible fallback at Melt Exchange. Do not use a whole-city curfew as an unannounced progress lock.

## Ambient

Snow absorption, long breathing spaces and the creak of loaded ice form the sound bed. Warm reading rooms introduce close human voices, while hazard tones avoid being indistinguishable from decorative chimes. Blockout sound zones: working bed at Binding Row, conversational bed at Warm Index, quieter domestic bed at Hearth Shelves. Use occlusion boundaries before adding loops. Alert captions, readable devices and separate intensity controls survive weather and combat mixes.

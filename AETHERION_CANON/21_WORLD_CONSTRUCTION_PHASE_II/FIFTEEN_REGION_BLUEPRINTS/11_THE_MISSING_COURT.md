# THE MISSING COURT major realm — Phase II spatial blueprint

Status: PROPOSED. Sources: P2-SRC-FOUNDATION, P2-SRC-DIRECTIVE, P2-SRC-PROPOSAL. All new dimensions, names and spatial arrangements need review. This is a refinement, not a replacement dossier or functioning level.

Baseline: [11 THE MISSING COURT](https://github.com/jrmoyler/Aetherion-The-Fifteen-Oaths/tree/5dce9edae0bb3587eb7c72433395e7d5a19857ea/AETHERION_CANON/04_OATH_DOSSIERS/11_THE_MISSING_COURT.md); IDs region-11, oath-11, poi-11-capital.

## Terrain

eastern karst valleys; broken limestone valleys and cave systems above the coastal plain. Climate: cool fog pockets amid drier sheltered slopes. Drainage remains basin-east. Place the capital as persistent karst street loop enclosing bounded concealed wings, with stable valley road and daylight sinkhole margins; anomalies confined to marked rooms. Transition through dry valley surfaces meet damp cave-fed channels and lower coastal scrub; do not paint a hard elemental border. Continent adjacency stays region-10, region-13, region-14, region-15; see WORLD_REGION_GRAPH.json. No new river may reverse a baseline basin or cross a divide without review.

## Capital

Absent Seat (poi-11-capital) keeps its baseline identity: Absent Seat occupies a mainland valley with a persistent public footprint and intermittently concealed civic wings. The city does not vanish wholesale from shared navigation; safe roads and accessible service anchors remain consistently available. The detailed layout is [capital blueprint](../CAPITAL_CITY_BLUEPRINTS/11_ABSENT_SEAT.md); its civic, working and household paths are independently legible. City envelope target 1200 × 1000 m with 15 m local relief is a first blockout hypothesis, not approved geography.

## Settlements

The added food/craft village and safety outpost address provisioning and last-mile relief; additional towns require demand, terrain and route evidence before proposal.

## Villages

Site on a viable ordinary spur near the exchange settlement, downstream of no contaminated intake and outside the boss footprint. A shared store, wash point, household cluster and cart turning court support its livelihood; field-to-store-to-market is its daily loop. Interior and household counts remain unresolved.

## Outposts

Place a visible refuge marker before the exposed approach, with shelter, a manual warning board, a maintenance locker and a return path. Its job is warning and continuity, not a toll wall that seals the realm.

Detailed capital service and exit graphs are in CAPITAL_LAYOUT_REGISTRY.json.

## Civilian

Baseline social purpose: Residents exchange practical favors through trusted intermediaries and may use chosen public identities. Covenant duty is to protect entrusted information within agreed limits, never to grant officials unrestricted permission to alter memory. Spatial refinement gives everyday work a visible home: Known Hearths contains kitchens, rest courts, accessible housing entries and a public route to Public Footprint. People disagree through hearings and bargaining, not a monolithic elemental personality. Visitors and dissenters retain food, care and routes.

## Commercial

The Court exports protected escrow, confidential mediation and refuge logistics while importing ordinary food and tools. Confidentiality fees risk pricing poor residents out of safety, making public funding and narrow eligibility rules consequential. Imports food, tools, reliable public records land at Ford Gate; exports confidential mediation, refuge logistics, restoration pass through Veil Workshops. Shared repair counters and the exchange settlement absorb downtime. Display inventory shortages as delayed contracts with substitute ordinary services, never as blanket disappearance of supplies.

## Military

Defenders escort witnesses, deny hostile observation and preserve escape routes. Sabotage targets coercive infrastructure, while erasing civilian records or attacking unknowing intermediaries carries serious internal and external consequences. Spatial delta: gate watch/refuge p2-poi-11-watch-refuge has separate civilian and equipment entrances, protected stores and a public-facing complaint board. Defenses face the approach; no artillery or discharge arc crosses housing or the service loop. Rotating guards walk gate → relief store → watch point → rest, and are not permanent combat crowds.

## Institution

Veiled benches separate protective custody from adjudication, with independent witnesses holding partial records. No single office should control identity, testimony and enforcement, but fragmentation also makes accountability slow and incomplete. Put accountable hearings in Public Footprint and specialist instruction in Witness Cloister. Enterable p2-poi-11-public-record-room contains a public record desk, private consultation niche and service exit. Access disputes produce alternate evidence routes; Oath binding never becomes a library card or care requirement.

## Sacred

It occupies an accessible alcove or court inside the existing civic district, so it adds a distinct purpose without inventing another settlement. The ordinary public entrance remains open to unbound visitors, observers and dissenters; no prayer, ritual, ancestry, faction standing or Oath binding is required for civic services. A quiet side exit and nonparticipatory route preserve choice. Objects left here are authored story props, not automatic transfers of player possessions.

## Underground

## Habitats

Dusk-window stalkers hunt along patchy light boundaries and avoid crowded refuges. Habitat corridors remain physical even when an Oath conceals them, allowing ecological study without claiming every shadow is another dimension. Map shelter, feeding edge, breeding refuge and migration pinch separately in the terrain blockout. Wildlife routine shifts between shelter and forage; warnings precede defensive behavior. Event recovery restores passage rather than permanently exterminating a biome.

## Hazards

Sea fog, cave damp and intermittent void pockets reduce visibility. Hazard boundaries use contour cues and spatial audio alternatives; concealment cannot remove subtitles, exits or critical combat warnings. Hazard ownership stays local to authored work/encounter volumes. Use observable warning → preparation → closure → recovery; failing staff or player attendance triggers a safe fallback, not permanent damage. Hazard resistance cannot substitute for air, footing, clear access or consent.

## Weather

Border blending follows elevation, exposure and water; magic modifies local authored conditions only.

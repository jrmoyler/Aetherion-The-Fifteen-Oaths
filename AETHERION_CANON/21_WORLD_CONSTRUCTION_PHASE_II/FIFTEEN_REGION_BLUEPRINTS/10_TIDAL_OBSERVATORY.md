# TIDAL OBSERVATORY major realm — Phase II spatial blueprint

Status: PROPOSED. Sources: P2-SRC-FOUNDATION, P2-SRC-DIRECTIVE, P2-SRC-PROPOSAL. All new dimensions, names and spatial arrangements need review. This is a refinement, not a replacement dossier or functioning level.

Baseline: [10 TIDAL OBSERVATORY](https://github.com/jrmoyler/Aetherion-The-Fifteen-Oaths/tree/5dce9edae0bb3587eb7c72433395e7d5a19857ea/AETHERION_CANON/04_OATH_DOSSIERS/10_TIDAL_OBSERVATORY.md); IDs region-10, oath-10, poi-10-capital.

## Terrain

northeastern sea headland; high coastal cliffs, stable terraces and anchored research platforms. Climate: clear dry intervals, sea wind and coastal fog. Drainage remains basin-east. Place the capital as anchored headland terraces with isolated experimental platforms, with mainland stair-and-ramp spine climbs from maintenance base to sighting crown. Transition through headland scrub descends through dry gullies toward eastern valleys; do not paint a hard elemental border. Continent adjacency stays region-06, region-13, region-11; see WORLD_REGION_GRAPH.json. No new river may reverse a baseline basin or cross a divide without review.

## Capital

Parallax Crown (poi-10-capital) keeps its baseline identity: Parallax Crown occupies an eastern headland with anchored observatories and limited suspended platforms. The platforms do not form a separate island realm; redundant mainland stairs and lifts preserve the continent's connected geography. The detailed layout is [capital blueprint](../CAPITAL_CITY_BLUEPRINTS/10_PARALLAX_CROWN.md); its civic, working and household paths are independently legible. City envelope target 1500 × 800 m with 100 m local relief is a first blockout hypothesis, not approved geography.

## Settlements

Ballast Haven (poi-10-settlement) remains the existing exchange settlement: Ballast Haven sits on stable mainland rock beneath the research platforms. It houses maintenance families and visitors who cannot tolerate altered gravity, keeping accessibility and ordinary life central to the cosmic realm. Its role is retained rather than renamed to fill a checklist. SETTLEMENT_REGISTRY.json distinguishes independent settlements from capital districts. The added food/craft village and safety outpost address provisioning and last-mile relief; additional towns require demand, terrain and route evidence before proposal.

## Villages

Site on a viable ordinary spur near the exchange settlement, downstream of no contaminated intake and outside the boss footprint. A shared store, wash point, household cluster and cart turning court support its livelihood; field-to-store-to-market is its daily loop. Interior and household counts remain unresolved.

## Outposts

Fogmark Refuge — mainland visibility watch and platform evacuation receiving point (p2-settlement-10-fogmark-refuge). Watch staff rotate from the capital; visitors can rest without military allegiance. Place a visible refuge marker before the exposed approach, with shelter, a manual warning board, a maintenance locker and a return path. Its job is warning and continuity, not a toll wall that seals the realm.

## Architecture

Use p2-arch-10-observatory-terraces, grounded in REF-104 and art-guide-10. Foundation motif: Cliff observatories, orbital monuments and suspended platforms. Refinement: Balanced cantilevers, broad instrument bearings and visible ballast masses frame controlled imbalance. Floating structures require legible anchoring, safe shutdown and occupied-space clearance, not decorative impossibility everywhere. Structural section: mainland rock anchors → ordinary-gravity service base → inhabited terraces → sighting halls → bounded optional platform volume; no separate floating realm. Ornament follows load, use and wear; new engineering dimensions remain review targets, and recovered images do not supply measurements.

## Infrastructure

heavy ballast stays low until inspected; food never crosses experimental platforms; conventional lifts have a mainland stair-and-ramp substitute. Provide physically separate potable water, process/dirty return and storm overflow paths, with inspectable crossings; source and outfall must be checked against CONTINENT_GEOGRAPHY.md and the baseline watershed. Solid waste moves to a bounded service site, never a magical void. Manual signs and emergency stores remain when special infrastructure shuts down. Detailed capital service and exit graphs are in CAPITAL_LAYOUT_REGISTRY.json.

## Civilian

Baseline social purpose: Public sky evenings share discoveries in accessible language, while ballast crews host practical repair schools. Civic duty is to disclose uncertainty and compensate affected communities, not to accept every experiment for the sake of progress. Spatial refinement gives everyday work a visible home: Steady Hearths contains kitchens, rest courts, accessible housing entries and a public route to Parallax Court. People disagree through hearings and bargaining, not a monolithic elemental personality. Visitors and dissenters retain food, care and routes.

## Commercial

The Observatory exports navigation and measurement services while importing food, metals and maintenance labor. Funding tied to spectacular results can distort research priorities, giving replication and local safety visible political value. Imports food, metals, maintenance labor land at Ballast Gate; exports navigation, measurement, gravity engineering pass through Instrument Terraces. Shared repair counters and the exchange settlement absorb downtime. Display inventory shortages as delayed contracts with substitute ordinary services, never as blanket disappearance of supplies.

## Military

Defenders control approach vectors and stabilize critical platforms before using force. Strategic forecasts aid logistics, but celestial perspective cannot justify indiscriminate bombardment or treat civilian districts as expendable experimental terrain. Spatial delta: gate watch/refuge p2-poi-10-watch-refuge has separate civilian and equipment entrances, protected stores and a public-facing complaint board. Defenses face the approach; no artillery or discharge arc crosses housing or the service loop. Rotating guards walk gate → relief store → watch point → rest, and are not permanent combat crowds.

## Institution

Research circles publish models to a common review forum, while inhabited platform councils approve experiments affecting their homes. The institutional flaw is unequal authority between prestigious theorists and the people carrying practical risk. Put accountable hearings in Parallax Court and specialist instruction in Meridian College. Enterable p2-poi-10-public-record-room contains a public record desk, private consultation niche and service exit. Access disputes produce alternate evidence routes; Oath binding never becomes a library card or care requirement.

## Sacred

It occupies an accessible alcove or court inside the existing civic district, so it adds a distinct purpose without inventing another settlement. The ordinary public entrance remains open to unbound visitors, observers and dissenters; no prayer, ritual, ancestry, faction standing or Oath binding is required for civic services. A quiet side exit and nonparticipatory route preserve choice. Objects left here are authored story props, not automatic transfers of player possessions.

## Underground

mainland rock anchors → ordinary-gravity service base → inhabited terraces → sighting halls → bounded optional platform volume; no separate floating realm. Separate civic maintenance from dungeon occupancy at signed vestibules. The underground plan is mainland instrument hall → conventional lift siding → evacuated platform control → failed calibration archive → independent mainland ramp; dry/ordinary access and independent exit are part of the blockout. Flood, smoke, collapse and anomaly envelopes cannot span every exit at once. Where deep geology is unsuitable, use a shallow covered service route rather than inventing a vast cavern.

## Habitats

Ballast-wing roostlings nest around stable thermal updrafts and avoid shifting gravity fields. Their movements warn workers of local anomalies, giving ecology an observational role without claiming animals understand celestial mathematics. Protect Headland Transit (p2-poi-10-habitat): cliff nesting and gravity-adapted organism passage outside research fields. Map shelter, feeding edge, breeding refuge and migration pinch separately in the terrain blockout. Wildlife routine shifts between shelter and forage; warnings precede defensive behavior. Event recovery restores passage rather than permanently exterminating a biome.

# IMPULSE FOUNDRY major realm — Phase II spatial blueprint

Status: PROPOSED. Sources: P2-SRC-FOUNDATION, P2-SRC-DIRECTIVE, P2-SRC-PROPOSAL. All new dimensions, names and spatial arrangements need review. This is a refinement, not a replacement dossier or functioning level.

Baseline: [15 IMPULSE FOUNDRY](https://github.com/jrmoyler/Aetherion-The-Fifteen-Oaths/tree/5dce9edae0bb3587eb7c72433395e7d5a19857ea/AETHERION_CANON/04_OATH_DOSSIERS/15_IMPULSE_FOUNDRY.md); IDs region-15, oath-15, poi-15-capital.

## Terrain

southeastern coastal plain; low river outlets, raised freight yards and inland service terraces. Climate: humid maritime with storm surge and industrial heat islands. Drainage remains basin-dawn, basin-east. Place the capital as freight plain divided by industrial buffers and an independent residential loop, with river-to-port heavy freight corridor parallel to public transit and evacuation routes. Transition through river terraces widen into silt plains, ports and managed marsh buffers; do not paint a hard elemental border. Continent adjacency stays region-11, region-14; see WORLD_REGION_GRAPH.json. No new river may reverse a baseline basin or cross a divide without review.

## Capital

Counterpoise (poi-15-capital) keeps its baseline identity: Counterpoise occupies the southeast coastal plain around freight yards and kinetic foundries. Heavy industry is separated from homes by service buffers and public transit, with coastal evacuation routes independent of factory command systems. The detailed layout is [capital blueprint](../CAPITAL_CITY_BLUEPRINTS/15_COUNTERPOISE.md); its civic, working and household paths are independently legible. City envelope target 2000 × 1400 m with 15 m local relief is a first blockout hypothesis, not approved geography.

## Settlements

Handshift (poi-15-settlement) remains the existing exchange settlement: Handshift is a mixed workshop town that preserves repairable tools and shared technical education. It seeks access to modern equipment without surrendering control of production schedules or becoming a disposable subcontractor district. Its role is retained rather than renamed to fill a checklist. SETTLEMENT_REGISTRY.json distinguishes independent settlements from capital districts. The added food/craft village and safety outpost address provisioning and last-mile relief; additional towns require demand, terrain and route evidence before proposal.

## Villages

Siltgarden — food plots and sediment stewardship upstream of port works (p2-settlement-15-siltgarden). Site on a viable ordinary spur near the exchange settlement, downstream of no contaminated intake and outside the boss footprint. A shared store, wash point, household cluster and cart turning court support its livelihood; field-to-store-to-market is its daily loop. Interior and household counts remain unresolved.

## Outposts

Breakwater Watch — surge observation and independent mechanical evacuation controls (p2-settlement-15-breakwater-watch). Watch staff rotate from the capital; visitors can rest without military allegiance. Place a visible refuge marker before the exposed approach, with shelter, a manual warning board, a maintenance locker and a return path. Its job is warning and continuity, not a toll wall that seals the realm.

## Architecture

Use p2-arch-15-impulse-yards, grounded in REF-101 and art-guide-15. Foundation motif: Industrial rail bays, modular gantries and kinetic fortifications. Refinement: Large bolted frames, crane spans and modular service decks expose how forces travel. Human-scale entrances, repair access and sheltered civic courts prevent the entire city from reading as machinery with incidental people attached. Structural section: flood-managed plain → drained service slab → separated freight trench and public deck → manufacturing hall → inspected crane volume; residential floors sit beyond machinery reach. Ornament follows load, use and wear; new engineering dimensions remain review targets, and recovered images do not supply measurements.

## Infrastructure

heavy loads stay in Kinetic Yards; public tram and food delivery use the residential loop; Coast Relief controls do not depend on factory authority. Provide physically separate potable water, process/dirty return and storm overflow paths, with inspectable crossings; source and outfall must be checked against CONTINENT_GEOGRAPHY.md and the baseline watershed. Solid waste moves to a bounded service site, never a magical void. Manual signs and emergency stores remain when special infrastructure shuts down. Detailed capital service and exit graphs are in CAPITAL_LAYOUT_REGISTRY.json.

## Civilian

Baseline social purpose: Repair schools, communal workshops and shift kitchens support civic life. Covenant duty is to maintain and share the benefits of tools, with a protected right to challenge unsafe production orders and propose alternatives. Spatial refinement gives everyday work a visible home: Quiet Blocks contains kitchens, rest courts, accessible housing entries and a public route to Stopwork Forum. People disagree through hearings and bargaining, not a monolithic elemental personality. Visitors and dissenters retain food, care and routes.

## Commercial

Impulse exports machinery and standardized components while importing ore, food, water and specialized craft. Automation can lower prices and eliminate livelihoods simultaneously, so retraining and shared ownership matter to the regional resolution. Imports ore, food, water, specialist craft land at Freight Gate; exports machinery, standardized parts, repair systems pass through Kinetic Yards. Shared repair counters and the exchange settlement absorb downtime. Display inventory shortages as delayed contracts with substitute ordinary services, never as blanket disappearance of supplies.

## Military

Military forces emphasize logistics, repair, combined engineering and recoverable fortifications. Siege engines require supply and crews; industrial power cannot justify permanent destruction of player housing or denial of neutral civilian routes. Spatial delta: gate watch/refuge p2-poi-15-watch-refuge has separate civilian and equipment entrances, protected stores and a public-facing complaint board. Defenses face the approach; no artillery or discharge arc crosses housing or the service loop. Rotating guards walk gate → relief store → watch point → rest, and are not permanent combat crowds.

## Institution

Production councils coordinate shared machinery while worker assemblies and settlement boards negotiate service priorities. Emergency command can accelerate repairs, but permanent emergency status is the central threat to civic self-government. Put accountable hearings in Stopwork Forum and specialist instruction in Repair Institute. Enterable p2-poi-15-public-record-room contains a public record desk, private consultation niche and service exit. Access disputes produce alternate evidence routes; Oath binding never becomes a library card or care requirement.

## Sacred

Hands at Rest Alcove in Stopwork Forum is a voluntary sacred/commemorative Oath space: a quiet remembrance of makers and preventable injuries, with deactivated tools displayed and stop-work dignity affirmed. It occupies an accessible alcove or court inside the existing civic district, so it adds a distinct purpose without inventing another settlement. The ordinary public entrance remains open to unbound visitors, observers and dissenters; no prayer, ritual, ancestry, faction standing or Oath binding is required for civic services. A quiet side exit and nonparticipatory route preserve choice. Objects left here are authored story props, not automatic transfers of player possessions.

## Underground

flood-managed plain → drained service slab → separated freight trench and public deck → manufacturing hall → inspected crane volume; residential floors sit beyond machinery reach. Separate civic maintenance from dungeon occupancy at signed vestibules. The underground plan is worker reception → halted assembly lane → protected refuge → production-control chamber → independent fire-and-flood exit; dry/ordinary access and independent exit are part of the blockout. Flood, smoke, collapse and anomaly envelopes cannot span every exit at once. Where deep geology is unsuitable, use a shallow covered service route rather than inventing a vast cavern.

## Landmarks

Primary skyline cue: Industrial rail bays, modular gantries and kinetic fortifications. Secondary local anchors are Stopwork Forum, Repair Institute and the clearly marked outpost refuge. Place these at decision points so silhouette, sign shapes and street logic work without saturated VFX. Great monuments never hide essential navigation behind mandatory camera rotation.

## Routes

Retain baseline ordinary pack routes to region-11, region-14 and the route IDs listed in TRANSPORT_ROUTE_REGISTRY.json / baseline TRADE_ROUTES.json. Local structure: neighbor approach → p2-settlement-15-breakwater-watch → poi-15-settlement → poi-15-capital; p2-settlement-15-siltgarden uses a service spur. This ordering is functional, not surveyed distance. Capital public loop and housing-side relief exit remain outside events. Optional Oath traversal can shorten a route only after its ordinary counterpart works.

## Habitats

Scrap-nesting scavengers exploit warm service cavities and transport seeds between disturbed sites. Habitat design and waste management can reduce conflict, while indiscriminate extermination removes useful scavenging without fixing the underlying problem. Protect Siltbank Corridor (p2-poi-15-habitat): shore birds, river life and heavy guardian diversion margin. Map shelter, feeding edge, breeding refuge and migration pinch separately in the terrain blockout. Wildlife routine shifts between shelter and forage; warnings precede defensive behavior. Event recovery restores passage rather than permanently exterminating a biome.

## Resources

Return Steel Yard (p2-poi-15-resource): sorted recoverable scrap with lockout-safe lifting and runoff containment. Scrap can be recovered only after lockout and ownership review; no reward for causing industrial accidents. Place a safe observation approach, working cells and an independent supply exit. Resource availability has healthy, stressed, closed and recovering presentation; local stock and yield numbers remain unresolved and must agree with the simulation plan.

## Dungeon

The Idle Assembly (poi-15-dungeon) remains: The Idle Assembly is an occupied factory where workers have halted unsafe production and automated enforcement continues. The dungeon combines negotiation, rescue and mechanical encounters, with evidence determining responsibility rather than faction uniforms alone. New spatial sequence: worker reception → halted assembly lane → protected refuge → production-control chamber → independent fire-and-flood exit. Foundation encounter context: The Perfect Executor is a production controller that optimizes output by removing workers' ability to stop unsafe processes. Players restore human override and stabilize essential services before choosing its future governance. See DUNGEON_AND_EXPLORATION_PLAN.md for admission, reset, co-op and exit rules; do not turn living title-holders into mandatory kill targets.

## Worldboss

p2-poi-15-worldboss: A heavy civic construct follows a demolition plan made before a neighborhood existed. Public roles disable drives, shield residents and update maps, turning reconstruction and governance into part of the boss resolution. Refinement locates the footprint next to the habitat rather than inside the capital. Admission signs mark the active volume; refuges, distant viewing and relief logistics remain outside. Combat, redirection and repair roles share the encounter, with readable stand-down and recovery. If the event overlaps the public catchment, authority resolves to one bounded state, never additive hazards on all roads.

## Secret

Manual Override Cell (p2-poi-15-secret): a sealed inspection room proves removal of worker stop authority. Compare physical stop circuits with archived authorizations; a supplied manual tool provides safe bypass. Its reward is optional testimony, a cosmetic/story variant or local repair knowledge; it cannot hide a mandatory civic exit, main-story prerequisite without hints, or a protected resident identity. A safe return path is visible from the discovery chamber.

## Housing

p2-poi-15-housing is the stable entry at Quiet Blocks. Housing combines durable modular units with personal workshops and communal repair bays. Players customize cosmetic machinery without exporting noise, collision hazards or resource drains to neighbors, and event reconstruction preserves ownership and belongings. Proposed sample interior: 12 × 10 m clear room envelope plus 4 × 3 m entrance/storage vestibule, sized for blockout tests rather than a promised entitlement. Housing type uses local kit p2-arch-15-impulse-yards; a street-facing recovery point preserves entry if event overlays close the exterior. No placement may obstruct public circulation, private storage permissions or the second exit.

## Events

Preserve Counterpoise service catchment (poi-15-event) and world-event-15: An infrastructure surge event threatens freight, housing power and river pumps. Players isolate failing sectors, move supplies and keep essential services running; repair outcomes alter temporary production bonuses rather than permanently erasing a city. Warning boards at arrival and the outpost direct civilians to p2-district-15-freight-gate. Event bounds exclude the ordinary service loop; service staff relocate before an affected counter closes. Recovery works are optional contributions with automatic restoration when players leave. See WORLD_ECOLOGY_SYSTEM.md and NPC_CIVILIZATION_SYSTEM.md for shared-state coordination.

## Pvp

Idle Ram Yard (p2-poi-15-pvp): depowered industrial training apron outside freight and home routes. Entry is explicit and match-scoped at a separate gate; Oath choice, housing or event participation never flags visitors. Target an 80 × 55 m initial practice envelope only, with two withdrawn/refuge exits and spectator circulation outside combat. A future siege may use authored fortification sockets after a distinct review; it cannot absorb the public road or confiscate houses.

## Hazards

Humid coastal summers, storm surges and industrial heat islands define weather. Waste heat and exhaust have bounded environmental consequences, and safety warnings remain legible through rain, steam and dense machinery. Hazard ownership stays local to authored work/encounter volumes. Use observable warning → preparation → closure → recovery; failing staff or player attendance triggers a safe fallback, not permanent damage. Hazard resistance cannot substitute for air, footing, clear access or consent.

## Weather

Baseline weather: humid maritime with storm surge and industrial heat islands. Coastal surge interrupts port loading; elevated relief paths and inland gates remain independent of production systems. Border blending follows elevation, exposure and water; magic modifies local authored conditions only. Night and weather combinations must be checked at all marked crossings; no mandatory color-only, audio-only or glare-based cue is acceptable.

## Daynight

Dawn lockout checks and worker transport; midday shift handover slows freight; night residential buffer limits noise and emergency systems remain manual. Trading and household routines differ from patrols; essential food, care, stable housing and return services persist through rotating staff or an accessible fallback at Freight Gate. Do not use a whole-city curfew as an unannounced progress lock.

## Ambient

Heavy low-frequency impacts, pneumatic breath and human work calls establish scale. Safety alarms use distinct rhythms and captions, and quiet residential zones demonstrate that the city is more than an endless combat factory. Blockout sound zones: working bed at Kinetic Yards, conversational bed at Stopwork Forum, quieter domestic bed at Quiet Blocks. Use occlusion boundaries before adding loops. Alert captions, readable devices and separate intensity controls survive weather and combat mixes.

## Npc Routines

Anchor existing character-15-leader to scheduled hearings at Stopwork Forum; character-15-civilian moves between specialist work at Kinetic Yards, public testimony and home/rest without being respawned as another identity. New background roles are role slots, not invented named protagonists: village producer → exchange store → home; porter → gate → workshop → break; watch → outpost → relief → rest; custodian → institution → service lane. Dawn lockout checks and worker transport; midday shift handover slows freight; night residential buffer limits noise and emergency systems remain manual. During hazards, interrupt into warn/escort/refuge before resuming; bound simulation off-screen and never require a precise real-time appointment for story access.

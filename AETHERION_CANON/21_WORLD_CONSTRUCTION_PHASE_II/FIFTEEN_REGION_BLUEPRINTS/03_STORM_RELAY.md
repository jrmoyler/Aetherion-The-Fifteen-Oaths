# STORM RELAY major realm — Phase II spatial blueprint

Status: PROPOSED. Sources: P2-SRC-FOUNDATION, P2-SRC-DIRECTIVE, P2-SRC-PROPOSAL. All new dimensions, names and spatial arrangements need review. This is a refinement, not a replacement dossier or functioning level.

Baseline: [03 STORM RELAY](https://github.com/jrmoyler/Aetherion-The-Fifteen-Oaths/tree/5dce9edae0bb3587eb7c72433395e7d5a19857ea/AETHERION_CANON/04_OATH_DOSSIERS/03_STORM_RELAY.md); IDs region-03, oath-03, poi-03-capital.

## Terrain

north-central mountain saddles; high ridge passes below the permanent snow line. Climate: orographic storms and icing. Drainage remains basin-choir. Place the capital as three anchored saddle clusters joined by a sheltered ground loop, with ridge service spine below cableways. Transition through storm towers descend through rocky pasture to basin roads; do not paint a hard elemental border. Continent adjacency stays region-02, region-05, region-06, region-09; see WORLD_REGION_GRAPH.json. No new river may reverse a baseline basin or cross a divide without review.

## Capital

Crownspan (poi-03-capital) keeps its baseline identity: Crownspan straddles several mountain saddles connected by protected cableways. Public squares sit away from discharge paths; dramatic suspended relay galleries are serviced from stable anchor towers and sheltered evacuation stairs. The detailed layout is [capital blueprint](../CAPITAL_CITY_BLUEPRINTS/03_CROWNSPAN.md); its civic, working and household paths are independently legible. City envelope target 1700 × 600 m with 50 m local relief is a first blockout hypothesis, not approved geography.

## Settlements

Grounding Reach (poi-03-settlement) remains the existing exchange settlement: Grounding Reach is a pass town with low-tech message houses beside high-energy stations. Its paper couriers remain useful during storms, making fallback infrastructure a lived economy rather than an unexplained game menu. Its role is retained rather than renamed to fill a checklist. SETTLEMENT_REGISTRY.json distinguishes independent settlements from capital districts. The added food/craft village and safety outpost address provisioning and last-mile relief; additional towns require demand, terrain and route evidence before proposal.

## Villages

Site on a viable ordinary spur near the exchange settlement, downstream of no contaminated intake and outside the boss footprint. A shared store, wash point, household cluster and cart turning court support its livelihood; field-to-store-to-market is its daily loop. Interior and household counts remain unresolved.

## Outposts

Last Bell — manual semaphore and sheltered pass watch beyond powered relay reach (p2-settlement-03-last-bell). Watch staff rotate from the capital; visitors can rest without military allegiance. Place a visible refuge marker before the exposed approach, with shelter, a manual warning board, a maintenance locker and a return path. Its job is warning and continuity, not a toll wall that seals the realm.

## Architecture

## Infrastructure

pack trains reach Pass Anchor beneath the cableways; manual porters bridge outage gaps; charged equipment travels separately from food and paper. Provide physically separate potable water, process/dirty return and storm overflow paths, with inspectable crossings; source and outfall must be checked against CONTINENT_GEOGRAPHY.md and the baseline watershed. Solid waste moves to a bounded service site, never a magical void. Manual signs and emergency stores remain when special infrastructure shuts down. Detailed capital service and exit graphs are in CAPITAL_LAYOUT_REGISTRY.json.

## Civilian

Baseline social purpose: Neighbors share public listening hours and teach verification of relayed information. Civic duty means keeping an addressable response point or staffing a fallback route, with privacy-protecting options for people fleeing persecution. Spatial refinement gives everyday work a visible home: Lee Houses contains kitchens, rest courts, accessible housing entries and a public route to Relay Commons. People disagree through hearings and bargaining, not a monolithic elemental personality. Visitors and dissenters retain food, care and routes.

## Commercial

Storm sells transmission capacity and weather services, importing insulation and structural repairs. Emergency service is ring-fenced from commercial pricing; factions contest how to fund universal access without embedding covert political control. Imports insulators, structural parts, food land at Pass Anchor; exports communications, weather services, routing pass through Cable Court. Shared repair counters and the exchange settlement absorb downtime. Display inventory shortages as delayed contracts with substitute ordinary services, never as blanket disappearance of supplies.

## Military

Relay forces secure continuity, repair teams and message integrity. Their military strength is coordination and rapid repositioning; deliberate civilian blackout is a contested escalation with immediate humanitarian and reputational costs. Spatial delta: gate watch/refuge p2-poi-03-watch-refuge has separate civilian and equipment entrances, protected stores and a public-facing complaint board. Defenses face the approach; no artillery or discharge arc crosses housing or the service loop. Rotating guards walk gate → relief store → watch point → rest, and are not permanent combat crowds.

## Institution

Tower cooperatives elect delegates to a routing council; public emergency traffic has protected priority. Commercial operators seek exceptions, and local towers can resist central orders at the risk of breaking shared guarantees. Put accountable hearings in Relay Commons and specialist instruction in Signal School. Enterable p2-poi-03-public-record-room contains a public record desk, private consultation niche and service exit. Access disputes produce alternate evidence routes; Oath binding never becomes a library card or care requirement.

## Sacred

Unsent Bell Niche in Relay Commons is a voluntary sacred/commemorative Oath space: a hand-rung memorial for messages and travelers lost in storms, with a tactile dedication board and no mandatory broadcast. It occupies an accessible alcove or court inside the existing civic district, so it adds a distinct purpose without inventing another settlement. The ordinary public entrance remains open to unbound visitors, observers and dissenters; no prayer, ritual, ancestry, faction standing or Oath binding is required for civic services. A quiet side exit and nonparticipatory route preserve choice. Objects left here are authored story props, not automatic transfers of player possessions.

## Underground

rock anchor → covered foot gallery → inhabited saddle courts → cablework → discharge mast; no residential room shares a grounding shaft. Separate civic maintenance from dungeon occupancy at signed vestibules. The underground plan is manual dispatch room → abandoned routing decks → occupied repair refuge → loop-isolation chamber → sheltered cable-anchor stair; dry/ordinary access and independent exit are part of the blockout. Flood, smoke, collapse and anomaly envelopes cannot span every exit at once. Where deep geology is unsuitable, use a shallow covered service route rather than inventing a vast cavern.

## Habitats

Copper-wing kites exploit rising air and roost outside discharge zones. Protective nesting sleeves reduce outages; indiscriminate pest removal harms a species that also warns crews of unstable air currents. Protect Lee Nest Ridges (p2-poi-03-habitat): sheltered bird colonies separated from discharge islands. Map shelter, feeding edge, breeding refuge and migration pinch separately in the terrain blockout. Wildlife routine shifts between shelter and forage; warnings precede defensive behavior. Event recovery restores passage rather than permanently exterminating a biome.

## Hazards

Orographic storms, icing and sudden crosswinds constrain travel. Lightning is channeled along marked corridors, with accessible countdown warnings and grounded shelters; routine outdoor life remains possible between events. Hazard ownership stays local to authored work/encounter volumes. Use observable warning → preparation → closure → recovery; failing staff or player attendance triggers a safe fallback, not permanent damage. Hazard resistance cannot substitute for air, footing, clear access or consent.

## Weather

Baseline weather: orographic storms and icing. Lightning disconnects optional sky decks; hand winches and covered stairs preserve essential service. Border blending follows elevation, exposure and water; magic modifies local authored conditions only. Night and weather combinations must be checked at all marked crossings; no mandatory color-only, audio-only or glare-based cue is acceptable.

# GRAFT SANCTUARY major realm — Phase II spatial blueprint

Status: PROPOSED. Sources: P2-SRC-FOUNDATION, P2-SRC-DIRECTIVE, P2-SRC-PROPOSAL. All new dimensions, names and spatial arrangements need review. This is a refinement, not a replacement dossier or functioning level.

Baseline: [07 GRAFT SANCTUARY](https://github.com/jrmoyler/Aetherion-The-Fifteen-Oaths/tree/5dce9edae0bb3587eb7c72433395e7d5a19857ea/AETHERION_CANON/04_OATH_DOSSIERS/07_GRAFT_SANCTUARY.md); IDs region-07, oath-07, poi-07-capital.

## Terrain

western lowland forest belt; river-fed foothills and broad cultivated forest floors. Climate: wet temperate to warm southern forest. Drainage remains basin-west. Place the capital as ground-connected tree clusters around a permeable commons, with drainage swales through a ground ring; canopy branches form optional upper loops. Transition through ash-rich upper farms grade into riparian woods and managed wetlands; do not paint a hard elemental border. Continent adjacency stays region-01, region-04, region-05, region-08; see WORLD_REGION_GRAPH.json. No new river may reverse a baseline basin or cross a divide without review.

## Capital

Rootmeet (poi-07-capital) keeps its baseline identity: Rootmeet grows around several connected mature trees with inspectable structural supports and ground-level services. Living bridges have dormant-season backups; sap flow and canopy light place real limits on expansion. The detailed layout is [capital blueprint](../CAPITAL_CITY_BLUEPRINTS/07_ROOTMEET.md); its civic, working and household paths are independently legible. City envelope target 1600 × 1200 m with 10 m local relief is a first blockout hypothesis, not approved geography.

## Settlements

Gleaning Margin (poi-07-settlement) remains the existing exchange settlement: Gleaning Margin is a mixed farm town at the forest edge with open commons and private plots. It tests whether an independent household can receive emergency care without surrendering long-term control over its land. Its role is retained rather than renamed to fill a checklist. SETTLEMENT_REGISTRY.json distinguishes independent settlements from capital districts. The added food/craft village and safety outpost address provisioning and last-mile relief; additional towns require demand, terrain and route evidence before proposal.

## Villages

Cutting Patch — nursery cuttings and independent food plots outside the capital root network (p2-settlement-07-cutting-patch). Site on a viable ordinary spur near the exchange settlement, downstream of no contaminated intake and outside the boss footprint. A shared store, wash point, household cluster and cart turning court support its livelihood; field-to-store-to-market is its daily loop. Interior and household counts remain unresolved.

## Outposts

Floodroot Lodge — river gauge, footbridge repair and wildlife crossing watch (p2-settlement-07-floodroot-lodge). Watch staff rotate from the capital; visitors can rest without military allegiance. Place a visible refuge marker before the exposed approach, with shelter, a manual warning board, a maintenance locker and a return path. Its job is warning and continuity, not a toll wall that seals the realm.

## Architecture

Use p2-arch-07-graft-courts, grounded in REF-107 and art-guide-07. Foundation motif: Root arches, woven living platforms and cultivated tree chambers. Refinement: Living timber frames combine trained roots with replaceable woven walls and stone hearth islands. Occupied structures include rot inspections, firebreaks and accessible ground routes rather than assuming every resident climbs a giant tree. Structural section: drained root buffer → accessible ground court → inspectable support frame → optional tree dwelling → canopy walkway; no building assumes uncontrolled growth carries structural load. Ornament follows load, use and wear; new engineering dimensions remain review targets, and recovered images do not supply measurements.

## Infrastructure

food and clean tools enter Margin Gate; soil-bearing carts pass quarantine; heavy loads stay at ground level; dormant canopy bridges never interrupt supply. Provide physically separate potable water, process/dirty return and storm overflow paths, with inspectable crossings; source and outfall must be checked against CONTINENT_GEOGRAPHY.md and the baseline watershed. Solid waste moves to a bounded service site, never a magical void. Manual signs and emergency stores remain when special infrastructure shuts down. Detailed capital service and exit graphs are in CAPITAL_LAYOUT_REGISTRY.json.

## Civilian

Baseline social purpose: Shared nurseries, food preservation and animal care organize daily work. Covenant duty is stewardship proportionate to benefit, with nonphysical contributions accepted and the right to refuse personal biological alteration explicitly protected. Spatial refinement gives everyday work a visible home: Dapple Houses contains kitchens, rest courts, accessible housing entries and a public route to Root Commons. People disagree through hearings and bargaining, not a monolithic elemental personality. Visitors and dissenters retain food, care and routes.

## Commercial

Graft exports food, fibers and ecological restoration while importing tools, salt and mineral supplements. Crop diversity protects trade resilience; pushing every farm toward the highest-yield template risks a continent-wide shared vulnerability. Imports tools, salt, mineral supplements land at Margin Gate; exports food, fibers, ecological restoration pass through Pruning Courts. Shared repair counters and the exchange settlement absorb downtime. Display inventory shortages as delayed contracts with substitute ordinary services, never as blanket disappearance of supplies.

## Military

Defenders protect food stores, seed diversity and evacuation trails. Living barriers delay invaders but must leave wildlife and civilian corridors; weaponizing disease or forced assimilation is a faction controversy, not routine heroic practice. Spatial delta: gate watch/refuge p2-poi-07-watch-refuge has separate civilian and equipment entrances, protected stores and a public-facing complaint board. Defenses face the approach; no artillery or discharge arc crosses housing or the service loop. Rotating guards walk gate → relief store → watch point → rest, and are not permanent combat crowds.

## Institution

Garden commons elect seasonal stewards, while sanctuary councils regulate grafts affecting shared roots and waterways. Consent records must be renewed when a graft changes function, preventing old permission from becoming unlimited biological ownership. Put accountable hearings in Root Commons and specialist instruction in Seed Ward. Enterable p2-poi-07-public-record-room contains a public record desk, private consultation niche and service exit. Access disputes produce alternate evidence routes; Oath binding never becomes a library card or care requirement.

## Sacred

Ungrafted Bench in Root Commons is a voluntary sacred/commemorative Oath space: a circle for gratitude and leave-taking where a cutting may remain deliberately unjoined, recognizing the right to withdraw. It occupies an accessible alcove or court inside the existing civic district, so it adds a distinct purpose without inventing another settlement. The ordinary public entrance remains open to unbound visitors, observers and dissenters; no prayer, ritual, ancestry, faction standing or Oath binding is required for civic services. A quiet side exit and nonparticipatory route preserve choice. Objects left here are authored story props, not automatic transfers of player possessions.

## Underground

drained root buffer → accessible ground court → inspectable support frame → optional tree dwelling → canopy walkway; no building assumes uncontrolled growth carries structural load. Separate civic maintenance from dungeon occupancy at signed vestibules. The underground plan is ground care ward → seed vault service passage → disputed root junction → consent archive → independent daylight disconnect court; dry/ordinary access and independent exit are part of the blockout. Flood, smoke, collapse and anomaly envelopes cannot span every exit at once. Where deep geology is unsuitable, use a shallow covered service route rather than inventing a vast cavern.

## Landmarks

Primary skyline cue: Root arches, woven living platforms and cultivated tree chambers. Secondary local anchors are Root Commons, Seed Ward and the clearly marked outpost refuge. Place these at decision points so silhouette, sign shapes and street logic work without saturated VFX. Great monuments never hide essential navigation behind mandatory camera rotation.

## Routes

Retain baseline ordinary pack routes to region-01, region-04, region-05, region-08 and the route IDs listed in TRANSPORT_ROUTE_REGISTRY.json / baseline TRADE_ROUTES.json. Local structure: neighbor approach → p2-settlement-07-floodroot-lodge → poi-07-settlement → poi-07-capital; p2-settlement-07-cutting-patch uses a service spur. This ordering is functional, not surveyed distance. Capital public loop and housing-side relief exit remain outside events. Optional Oath traversal can shorten a route only after its ordinary counterpart works.

## Habitats

Seed-carrier browsers distribute fruit and maintain glades; predators prevent destructive overbrowsing. Rootpath harts are trained animals with welfare and forage needs, not summonable equipment that disappears when inconvenient. Protect Old Canopy Passage (p2-poi-07-habitat): guardian migration and pollinator continuity between mature stands. Map shelter, feeding edge, breeding refuge and migration pinch separately in the terrain blockout. Wildlife routine shifts between shelter and forage; warnings precede defensive behavior. Event recovery restores passage rather than permanently exterminating a biome.

## Resources

Coppice Commons (p2-poi-07-resource): rotational poles, fruits and seed collection with independently marked plots. Harvest depends on marked stewardship cells; food reserves and propagation stock cannot be converted wholesale to player loot. Place a safe observation approach, working cells and an independent supply exit. Resource availability has healthy, stressed, closed and recovering presentation; local stock and yield numbers remain unresolved and must agree with the simulation plan.

## Dungeon

The Common Root (poi-07-dungeon) remains: The Common Root is an occupied maintenance network beneath the capital, combining care wards, seed vaults and disputed control junctions. The central question is who may disconnect, not whether all shared infrastructure should die. New spatial sequence: ground care ward → seed vault service passage → disputed root junction → consent archive → independent daylight disconnect court. Foundation encounter context: The Unending Orchard is a productive living symbiosis whose roots invade independent farms while feeding households with no immediate substitute. Cutting it back reduces food, moving it consumes another habitat and leaving it intact restricts autonomy. Players can fight dangerous growth, but no hidden malicious component removes the need for a costly settlement. See DUNGEON_AND_EXPLORATION_PLAN.md for admission, reset, co-op and exit rules; do not turn living title-holders into mandatory kill targets.

## Worldboss

p2-poi-07-worldboss: An old canopy guardian moves through farmland after its habitat corridor closes. The event restores passage and protects harvests, allowing animal handling, engineering and defense to contribute without requiring a kill. Refinement locates the footprint next to the habitat rather than inside the capital. Admission signs mark the active volume; refuges, distant viewing and relief logistics remain outside. Combat, redirection and repair roles share the encounter, with readable stand-down and recovery. If the event overlaps the public catchment, authority resolves to one bounded state, never additive hazards on all roads.

## Secret

Trace labeled moisture and consent markers to a manual isolation collar; no plant Oath required. Its reward is optional testimony, a cosmetic/story variant or local repair knowledge; it cannot hide a mandatory civic exit, main-story prerequisite without hints, or a protected resident identity. A safe return path is visible from the discovery chamber.

## Housing

p2-poi-07-housing is the stable entry at Dapple Houses. Homes grow through authored modular stages with inspectable supports and optional garden plots. Players choose living or conventional interiors, and inactivity never lets unchecked growth destroy furniture or revoke ownership. Proposed sample interior: 12 × 10 m clear room envelope plus 4 × 3 m entrance/storage vestibule, sized for blockout tests rather than a promised entitlement. Housing type uses local kit p2-arch-07-graft-courts; a street-facing recovery point preserves entry if event overlays close the exterior. No placement may obstruct public circulation, private storage permissions or the second exit.

## Events

Preserve Rootmeet service catchment (poi-07-event) and world-event-07: An overgrowth event asks players to prune escape routes, relocate animals and preserve food-bearing sections. Success creates several viable recovery landscapes; burning everything may be fast but requires costly soil and habitat repair. Warning boards at arrival and the outpost direct civilians to p2-district-07-margin-gate. Event bounds exclude the ordinary service loop; service staff relocate before an affected counter closes. Recovery works are optional contributions with automatic restoration when players leave. See WORLD_ECOLOGY_SYSTEM.md and NPC_CIVILIZATION_SYSTEM.md for shared-state coordination.

## Pvp

## Hazards

Rain, fungal blooms and seasonal canopy shifts define weather. Drought and invasive overgrowth are different events requiring different responses, so healing the land never means maximizing biomass everywhere. Hazard ownership stays local to authored work/encounter volumes. Use observable warning → preparation → closure → recovery; failing staff or player attendance triggers a safe fallback, not permanent damage. Hazard resistance cannot substitute for air, footing, clear access or consent.

## Weather

Baseline weather: wet temperate to warm southern forest. Saturated ground closes canopy work; hard-surface ground detours remain outside flood swales. Border blending follows elevation, exposure and water; magic modifies local authored conditions only. Night and weather combinations must be checked at all marked crossings; no mandatory color-only, audio-only or glare-based cue is acceptable.

## Daynight

Dawn irrigation and pollinator quiet; noon open commons and care shifts; dusk pruning crews inspect pathways before nocturnal wildlife movement. Trading and household routines differ from patrols; essential food, care, stable housing and return services persist through rotating staff or an accessible fallback at Margin Gate. Do not use a whole-city curfew as an unannounced progress lock.

## Ambient

Insects, working water, pruning rhythms and close communal voices vary with season and canopy density. Overgrowth warnings interrupt the sound bed clearly, with readable plant-state animations and captions. Blockout sound zones: working bed at Pruning Courts, conversational bed at Root Commons, quieter domestic bed at Dapple Houses. Use occlusion boundaries before adding loops. Alert captions, readable devices and separate intensity controls survive weather and combat mixes.

## Geological framework

The northern spine is an old uplifted metamorphic belt. Rime holds glacial cirques on its highest western blocks; Storm occupies lower, fractured quartzite saddles. Fault is the southwest-striking folded sedimentary and mineralized continuation, not a separate mountain ring around an elemental tile. Resistant sandstone ribs alternate with weathering shale. Mineral exposure explains quarries and settlement terraces, while steep young fractures explain inspection and rockfall risks.

Kiln's volcanic apron is younger than the adjacent uplift. It overlaps the west side of the older basement and locally dams tributaries. The main Thawcourse occupies an established gorge outside the active caldera floor. Cindervault remains on a cooled rim, not a floating city in lava. Graft's lower fans receive weathered volcanic soils and transported sediment, not unlimited magical fertility.

Reed's caprock escarpment descends eastward toward the Theatre valleys. Its cliffs have back slopes and sheltered gardens; not every hectare is a sheer precipice. The Observatory's hard headland stands above these valleys, allowing coastal fog below and clear viewing intervals above. Court limestone overlies relatively impermeable shale, permitting both sink loss and lower springs. Broken thresholds are cultural and local-anomaly architecture over this physical terrain.

Sanguine's redness comes from iron-bearing sandstone and soils. Escapement follows old river terraces and rolling loess steppe, not clockwork terrain all the way to bedrock. Impulse sits on a depositional lower plain: foundation choices, dredging, flood storage and raised freight yards must be visible under its machinery. Neither Oath deletes sediment or creates unlimited load-bearing ground.

## Relief hierarchy and climate

WORLD_REGION_GRAPH supplies proposed regional min/max elevations and a distinct main-channel station altitude. A low channel through Fault does not imply its watershed crest is low. A dry capital bench can sit above a wet river corridor. These intervals deliberately overlap across borders.

- Northern high catchments: permanent or long-lasting snow only on the highest sheltered surfaces; seasonal melt travels downslope. Ice outside this band requires a bounded Rime installation or anomaly.
- Western windward belt: moist prevailing westerlies wet the Kiln uplands and Graft forests; caldera interiors have local shelter and ash-modified soils. Coastal warmth is a proposed marine influence, not yet an ocean-circulation simulation.
- Interior lee: Fault and Storm reduce moisture reaching Daybreak, Sanguine and Theatre. Seasonal rain still occurs; dry valleys are not rainless deserts. Irrigation depends on storage and withdrawal limits.
- Northeast coast: headland winds and sea fog alternate with dry observing windows. Observatory is a gravity/navigation culture, never an ocean-water duplicate of Tidal Covenant.
- Southeast plain: humid marine incursions and heat storage contrast with inland steppe. Industrial waste heat modifies local comfort and ventilation, not continental weather.

No approved latitude, calendar, axial tilt or annual rainfall exists. Therefore temperature/rainfall climatologies must remain qualitative until the planetary and seasonal frame is chosen. A weather implementation may use provisional runoff buckets; that does not validate this landform or rainfall budget.

| basin-east / Veilwater | Observatory 850 → Theatre 380 → Court 170 → Impulse 8 → ocean 0 | Headland back-slope runoff bends inland around resistant ridge, then returns east through karst springs |

These are ordered control stations, not a claim that the river bed takes a straight line between capitals. The Veilwater's inland bend is intentional: the seaward headland face may have minor short streams, but the registered trunk drains its back slope into Theatre before returning toward the Court. Every consecutive basin pair has a preserved land adjacency. No main river requires a canal over the Fault divide.

Thawcourse and Choirwater remain separate in region-04 until estuarine mixing. Dawnrun and Veilwater reach region-15 through separate lower channels; only a documented lower-plain mixing reach can join them. Their station graphs must not be unioned into one global DAG solely by region ID: a region is an area containing multiple river stations, not a single hydraulic junction.

Court groundwater receives an explicit sink-to-spring reach within its own basin. Water does not disappear permanently into the void. Terrain production must identify infiltration, subsurface gradient, reemergence and flood overflow. Until then the diagram uses a continuous logical river edge with no measured underground travel time.

### Lakes and retention, proposed placements

- Rime moraine lake: below the ice accumulation zone and upstream of the Kiln gorge. Natural moraine dam, visible spill sill and rock-side emergency overflow. An ice-dam failure increases downstream discharge; it never sends water uphill. Do not put occupied streets on the outburst fan.
- Fault shoulder reservoir: a small managed off-channel impoundment supplied by a diversion, with a return spillway to Choirwater. It is not a continent-spanning lake across the divide.
- Graft oxbows: seasonally connected cut-off bends on the lower Thawcourse; habitat and flood storage coexist with cultivated raised ground.
- Bitterglass treatment cells: engineered settling and reaction basins, hydraulically isolated from potable supply. An attractive green pool is not assumed swimmable, a bottomless filter or a natural regional lake.
- Theatre shallow seasonal lake: low basin depression with a sill draining to Veilwater in wet periods; seasonal drawdown exposes salt crusts. No permanent high-flow lake without a water budget.
- Impulse retention basins: off-channel flood storage above surge gates, separated from industrial process water and homes. Filling them reduces storage available for the next storm.

These lake types have no new canon POI identity; regional designers may attach named local sites after review. Lakes require inlet/outlet, sill height, storage range and downstream owner before terrain lock.

## Coasts, estuaries and land continuity

The west shore changes from rocky volcanic exposure to alluvial forest margin and sheltered southwest estuary. Tidal's reef districts use connected causeways or dry civic approaches; reefs are never the only way to reach its capital. Shore habitats grade freshwater → brackish → marine. Storm surge and salt intrusion are coastal boundary conditions, not a reason to salt the entire upstream basin.

The northeast headland is cliff-dominated with protected landing coves behind the main exposure. Court embayments follow drowned lower karst valleys. The southeast shore broadens into deltas, mudflats and raised gravel port terraces. A coastwise service from Confluence Quay to Counterpoise runs around the southern mainland; it supplements, and does not invent, a new region-04/15 land adjacency.

## Border grammar and disputed ground

Apply borders to terrain by local segment, not a single visual trope for an entire realm:

- 01–02 and 02–03: high survey saddles and catchment shoulders; the Thawcourse crossing is a specific gap in that rule.
- 01–05, 02–05, 03–05 and 05–09: old ridge surveys, mineral concessions and low passes. Borders can cross a river at a bridge; they cannot force the river to climb the border ridge.
- 03–06: Shared Saddle is a contested freight-control node; both jurisdictions retain public shelter obligations. 03–09 and 06–09 follow shoulder roads down to common basin approaches.
- 01–07, 05–07 and 07–08: ash farms, watershed woodland and grazing rights create mixed border settlements rather than abrupt color walls.
- 04–07, 04–08 and 05–08: wetland demarcation follows surveyed banks and cooperative flood cells. The channel migration envelope is shared management space inside the existing realms, not unclaimed new territory.
- 06–10, 06–13 and 10–13: ridge shoulders, escarpment benches and the headland back-slope catchment form visible transitions.
- 08–09, 08–12, 09–12, 09–13 and 12–13: agricultural terraces and low passes carry old cadastral lines; vegetation remains physically continuous.
- 10–11, 11–13 and 11–15: carbonate contact, mapped spring rights and flood-safe approaches govern jurisdiction. No border line is an automatic phasing gate.
- 11–14, 12–14, 13–14 and 14–15: road charter boundaries and tributary crossings define toll administration. Contested freight infrastructure cannot become compulsory PvP or compulsory Oath binding.

Neutral relief commons, grazing easements and watershed reserves remain administratively inside the listed territories. Ordinary travelers encounter civic law and weather, not fifteen unrelated elemental force fields.

## What must be validated on actual terrain

Sample a heightfield and hydrologically condition only documented culverts/spillways. Delineate catchments from that surface; inspect river longitudinal profiles, lake sills, saddle heights and outlet continuity. Mark bridges as water-permeable structures, not unbroken embankments. Check flood storage, salinity extent and correlated storm/closure states. Adjust channels and borders through a recorded review delta while preserving canonical IDs. Current validation proves only conceptual topology and strictly descending station targets; it cannot prove discharge, erosion, watershed area, groundwater physics, climate realism or passable road grades.

# Dungeon and exploration plan — Phase II

Status: PROPOSED. Sources: P2-SRC-FOUNDATION, P2-SRC-DIRECTIVE, P2-SRC-PROPOSAL. This is a spatial and content-production plan, not a runtime implementation or new set of fifteen replacement stories.

## Scope and admission

Preserve all fifteen baseline dungeon IDs, their civilian stakes, the Confluence Chamber and named encounter identities. A regional dungeon is an authored bounded space connected to the ordinary continent. The entry is legible, exposes intended commitment and party consent, and supplies an unbound tool route. A dungeon crossing cannot be the only way to reach normal housing, food, care or another realm. Main-story alternatives cannot be hidden behind PvP standing.

The initial prototype uses one traversable entrance, an independent rescue exit, a stable refuge and a return interaction at every commitment threshold. These are design obligations, not an arbitrary room count. Party size, duration, difficulty tiers, loot quantities and matchmaking remain unresolved pending the baseline gameplay-system review. Do not label fifteen full dungeons implementation-ready because their spatial sequence is described.

## Exploration grammar and fair discovery

Arrival landmark → safe observation → demonstrated local rule → optional branch → consequential encounter → evidence/recovery → public return. Observation must show the hazard before punishment. Ordinary tools, captioned signals, diagrams and recap interfaces preserve first-Oath independence. Optional Oath paths can exchange effort or provide a different view; they cannot remove the encounter's ethical question or become a mandatory one-build lock.

Secrets are physically motivated maintenance or records spaces. Every secret has a discoverable ordinary clue, an accessible reading alternative and a visible return. Their rewards support context, cosmetic expression or optional repair, not hidden mandatory civic functionality. Information about protected witnesses remains compartmented; more complete collection is not automatically a morally superior outcome.

## State, authority and recovery

Record instance revision, consented party membership, checkpoints, inspected evidence and authored encounter outcomes separately from regional simulation. A local valve reset cannot erase another party's decision or alter unrelated housing. Rejoining starts at the latest safe shared checkpoint; a disconnected player cannot be stranded in a hazard volume. On host/server or navigation failure, return to the stable capital refuge and preserve already accepted evidence; final save/ownership rules require systems validation.

Runtime ownership must resolve a dungeon/event/world-boss overlap before activation. Admit only one authority for a shared hazard footprint; independent routes remain open. Victory over living leaders is not a kill mandate. Negotiation, nonlethal encounter resolution, technical isolation and civilian care remain valid where the baseline establishes them. Rewards must not encourage creating the civic hazard repeatedly.

## Non-linear exploration and readiness bands

All bands below are PROPOSED encounter-authoring categories, not canonical region levels, approved combat ratings or mandatory character-level gates. Every realm contains a safe civic arrival, approachable local exploration and optional more demanding pockets. A player may begin with any Oath, return to earlier locations for different evidence, and travel the ordinary continent without clearing fifteen realms in sequence. Story evidence, tool competence, encounter readiness and competitive participation are separate axes; none silently substitutes for another.

Before entering an optional pocket, its board previews the local hazards, available ordinary tools, recovery point, likely commitment and whether combat is optional. No numeric level recommendation is claimed until combat testing supports it. A player who declines the pocket retains the public road, capital services and story-relevant alternative evidence path.

### Beginner routes: civic literacy and one local rule

A beginner loop teaches observation, ordinary travel, a reversible interaction and a return to a familiar service anchor. Examples:

- Cindervault (poi-01-capital) → Ashbridge (poi-01-settlement) → the publicly supervised edge of Paleclay Bench (p2-poi-01-resource) → Cindervault. The player reads a pressure warning, repairs a cold ceramic fitting with a supplied tool and returns a signed sample. Active vents and boss migration are outside this teaching loop.
- Rootmeet (poi-07-capital) → Gleaning Margin (poi-07-settlement) → the marked outer edge of Coppice Commons (p2-poi-07-resource) → Rootmeet. A simple stewardship choice shows why propagation stock stays protected; no fight, Oath binding or land surrender is required.
- Aureate Steps (poi-09-capital) → Lantern Ward (poi-09-settlement) → Common Waystation (poi-neutral-crossroads) → Aureate Steps. Compare a posted service notice with a witness account, identify a safe relief route and find a staffed fallback when a counter closes. The locked plaza narrative is not rewritten as a tutorial reward.

These are candidate onboarding loops in different regions, not privileged starting factions. Other regions use the same small-scope learning objective with their local warning and service systems. If a regional event threatens a loop, staff reroute the exercise to the capital or pause its optional objective; they do not trap beginners behind a live encounter.

### Midgame exploration: compare systems across a border

Midgame routes combine two already introduced rules and ask the player to understand a consequence across institutions. They unlock through demonstrated knowledge or supplied explanations rather than a fixed continent order.

- Ash orchard descent, route-01-07 / p2-route-01-01-07: compare Kiln's ash management with Graft's food and habitat margins. The optional investigation can connect Stapled Cistern (p2-poi-01-secret) with ordinary stewardship records; it does not presume guilt or require entry into every dungeon.
- Treatment channel road, route-04-08 / p2-route-04-04-08: compare an upstream sample history at Vitrial with salinity and cargo restrictions at Confluence Quay. Landward public access and clean-water service remain protected during treatment closures.
- Common valley road, route-09-13 / p2-route-09-09-13: compare public testimony at Daybreak with disclosed scenic representations at Manydoor. A plain-surface route and evidence recap are always available; uncertainty is part of the mystery rather than an instruction to distrust the interface.

Each route supports a short local branch, a longer evidence circuit and a return without commitment. A player can complete any one of these before the others. Interregional travel times remain the transport registry's unmeasured blockout targets.

### Dangerous frontiers: optional exposure beside ordinary travel

Frontiers are bounded terrain or hazard pockets, not lethal faction borders. Mark the difference between a protected road and a discretionary expedition at the last staffed refuge.

- Moraine stair road, route-01-02 / p2-route-01-01-02: optional moraine and ice-dam observation margins near Rime test warning comprehension and retreat planning. The inspected ordinary route or the registered Fault detour remains the travel alternative.
- Shared Saddle road, route-03-06 / p2-route-03-03-06: optional exposed maintenance and nesting margins near The Shared Saddle (poi-contested-pass) combine gust and charge warnings. Weather exposure does not automatically enter PvP; protected relief travel remains separate from contested objectives.
- Headland ridge road, route-06-10 / p2-route-06-06-10: optional cliff instruments and Headland Transit (p2-poi-10-habitat) require a retreat plan and awareness of field boundaries. Mainland access does not require gliding, altered gravity or a research pledge.
- Karst terrace road, route-11-14 / p2-route-11-11-14: optional dry-adit inspection can close under rain, while the surface route remains legible. Concealment is bounded to marked spaces and never makes the whole region's navigation unreliable.

Advance warning, a visible refuge and a reliable turn-back are frontier acceptance conditions. Weather changes modify a local branch's availability; they cannot create an unavoidable continent-wide equipment check.

### High-level dungeons: complexity, not mandatory geography

Candidate high-level versions of existing dungeons add coordination, simultaneous responsibilities or competing evidence after their simpler local rules are understood. They do not create new baseline POIs or silently change a living encounter into a compulsory kill:

- The Counterweight Halls (poi-05-dungeon): combine support inspection, a bounded load transfer and worker rescue. The advanced challenge is maintaining an exit while redistributing weight, with a supplied tool-only route.
- The Falling Meridian (poi-10-dungeon): stabilize separate anchor systems while a conventional evacuation route remains active. An ordinary-gravity role and camera-stable presentation carry meaningful contribution.

- The Idle Assembly (poi-15-dungeon): coordinate lockout, worker protection and evidence custody without surrendering public stop authority. Technical competence cannot authorize destruction of the occupied factory.

Difficulty versions, party size, numerical tuning and reward parity require combat and accessibility review. These examples demonstrate an authoring band; they do not reserve all difficult content for the final four numbered regions. Any region's existing dungeon may support an advanced variant when its mechanics justify one.

## Rare discoveries, mysteries, achievements and treasure

### Rare discoveries without missed-life penalties

Rare means an unusual authored configuration or uncommon clue combination, not compulsory real-time attendance. For example, a clear observation window at Second Baseline Niche (p2-poi-10-secret), a dry inspection opportunity at Dry Ledger Niche (p2-poi-04-secret), or a disclosed backstage configuration at The Unpainted Door (p2-poi-13-secret) may reveal an additional optional perspective. If weather or a schedule hides it, an archived observation, resettable local scenario or later announced window preserves discoverability. No permanently missable main-story fact depends on a random spawn. Exact spawn cadence and loot probability remain unresolved.

### Mysteries as evidence graphs

Each mystery distinguishes observed object, authored record, witness interpretation and open uncertainty. A minimum clue path gives a reasonable conclusion; optional cross-region clues can challenge it without retroactively declaring the original player foolish. Suggested circuits are Stapled Cistern's safety accounts, Bypassed Sample Box's treatment history (p2-poi-08-secret), and Manual Override Cell's worker-authority evidence (p2-poi-15-secret). These are separate regional cases, not evidence of one newly invented universal conspiracy. The Unclaimed Stair (p2-poi-11-secret) supplies procedural evidence without demanding disclosure of protected identities. Journal recaps preserve clues across sessions and never require remembering information the game did not show.

### Optional achievement proposals

Achievement names below are review labels, not new implemented achievement IDs or promises of platform trophies:

- “A Road Kept Open”: contribute to a complete warning-and-recovery cycle at any one baseline public event, with credit for rescue, logistics, technical repair or defense. Remaining on the ordinary route is not failure.
- “Three Ways Home”: identify a capital's public arrival, service-safe route and relief exit using signs or an accessible guided inspection. It rewards spatial understanding without speedrunning or a particular movement ability.
- “A Claim Reconsidered”: resolve one regional mystery by comparing differing evidence and recording uncertainty. No achievement requires accusing a named character or publishing a protected identity.
- “Room for Passage”: support one habitat corridor's recoverable reopening without requiring a kill. Assistance through engineering, handling, supply or accessible observation is valid.

Achievements are cosmetic/recognition-oriented proposals with no exclusive combat-stat gate, required daily streak or mandatory perfect attendance. Credit is once per eligible authored outcome, recorded independently of external territorial control and protected against duplicate event receipts.

### Treasure and resource behavior

Treasure is placed where its ownership and history make sense: authorized workshop salvage, a clearly relinquished maintenance cache, a public discovery stipend or an explicitly awarded civic object. A chest inside someone's occupied home is not assumed free loot. The baseline civilian refuge, care stock, seed reserve and emergency food floor are never treasure containers.

At Paleclay Bench (p2-poi-01-resource), resource reward follows safe cell inspection; at Return Steel Yard (p2-poi-15-resource), salvage follows lockout and ownership review; at Coppice Commons (p2-poi-07-resource), useful material comes from marked rotational harvest. Optional secret rewards may be a local furnishing pattern, a journal perspective, a repair recipe proposal or a documented collectible, subject to economy/art review. Nothing here approves a new cash economy, real-money value or production drop table.

A discovered cache records eligibility and claimed state separately from the world prop. Reconnect cannot duplicate contents; recovery cannot delete a granted item; another player's discovery cannot erase a story clue. Repeat exploration can offer changed testimony, habitat recovery, material weathering or a new route choice instead of endlessly refilling the same rare chest. Scarcity, replacement cadence and inventory limits require the economy and simulation contracts before implementation.

### Exploration acceptance gate

Test a beginner, midgame and advanced route from at least three different starting Oaths without changing their public access. Verify a frontier retreat, a weather-delayed rare clue's alternate discovery, achievement credit after reconnect, an unclaimed cache after another party's visit, and a non-PvP journey through an endgame contested region. Reject any design that turns the fifteen realms into fifteen required combat levels, puts a mystery behind the sole safe exit, or makes protected services depend on treasure collection.

## Build order and gates

1. Build a plain-geometry slice of one dry, ordinary-access civic entrance, evidence branch and rescue exit. Validate the interaction language before multiplying content.
2. Contrast that slice with Tidal's non-swimming refuge route and Observatory's ordinary-gravity route. This tests whether accessibility is spatial rather than only UI.
3. Prove consent/identity handling with Rime, Sanguine and Theatre living-person encounters. Do not rely on boss defeat as blanket permission to kill, expose or rewrite characters.
4. Validate event/instance boundary ownership, reconnect, safe return, housing preservation and recoverable reward state.
5. Only then expand regionally, profile the actual scenes and seek narrative/art/engineering approval. Dossier completeness is not production completeness.

## Acceptance and known gaps

Every ID must resolve; every dungeon must offer a no-local-Oath completion path and independent exit; every secret must have optional clues and a safe return; no event may monopolize ordinary regional access. Exact terrain meshes, instance transitions, room dimensions, puzzles in engine, encounter mechanics, actor budgets, combat validation, localization and performance remain open. Architecture kits and reference observations guide production, while new chamber art and creature encounter states require explicit review.

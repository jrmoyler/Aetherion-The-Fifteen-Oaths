## Delta and physical basis

This expands existing realm ecology into land-use and season loops, without replacing the foundation realm registry or creature registry. Four basins, fifteen Oaths, thirty adjacencies and all baseline POIs stay unchanged. Habitat and resource POIs are proposed planning locations owned by the Phase II region registry. A habitat sequence is a relative cross-section, not georeferenced terrain. Ordinary plants and wildlife exist beside Oath phenomena; a realm is not uniformly its element.

## region-01: KILN OATH major realm

- Physical basis: western volcanic upland; upland caldera rims above the western river valleys; wet windward slopes with rain shadows inside calderas. Basin membership: basin-west.
- Substrate: Porous basalt and ash loam on old caldera terraces.
- Habitat transect: Vent exclusions → scrub apron → managed ash orchard → cool riparian forest.
- Seasonal loop: Wet ash compacts in renewal; dry growth season raises dust; harvest pruning opens firebreaks; cold rain increases steam.
- Wildlife foundation: Heat-tolerant scrub occupies cooled terraces; burrowers disperse mineral salts and nest beyond active vents. Slag-backed burrowers become hostile when dumping blocks nesting channels, creating a sanitation conflict rather than an evil species.
- Food and work: Ash orchard compost and refractory-clay extraction share settling ponds.
- Pressure and feedback: Dumping seals burrow outlets; nesting loss reduces soil mixing.
- Migration: Slag-backed burrowers move laterally between cooled terraces, never through active vents.
- Recovery: Clear drainage and cap dumping before reseeding; no instant forest growth.
- Human response: Heavy buttressed shells enclose ventilated work courts, with replaceable ceramic roofs and external cooling galleries. Public buildings display repair seams as civic records rather than hiding all wear behind black spikes.
- Oath boundary: Wet western winds meet volcanic heat, producing steam fog and ash rain. Eruption warnings combine ground vibration, flag patterns and audible bells; fire immunity never substitutes for oxygen or clear evacuation routes. No regional magic overrides the ordinary moisture, food or freight accounting model.
- Readability: terrain silhouette and maintenance evidence use fired ceramic, oxidized iron, firecloth as the recovered visual family; new flora/fauna/material assets are unmade proposals. Ground cover must preserve contrasting safe-path edges.

## region-02: RIME ARCHIVE major realm

- Substrate: Rock-founded alpine valleys, moraine gravel, peat-free thin soil.
- Habitat transect: Permanent ice → scree/lichen → dwarf shrub → sheltered conifer margin.
- Seasonal loop: Melt releases water in renewal; short growth is forage-rich; harvest stores fungi; cold season closes optional high trails.
- Wildlife foundation: Lichen and sheltered fungi feed rime-antler grazers that browse thaw margins. Seasonal migration signals changing snowpack; predators approach towns when sealed roads interrupt that movement, not because frost creatures are inherently cruel.
- Food and work: Warm-core fungus rooms use imported substrate and measured ventilation.
- Pressure and feedback: Heavy traffic narrows grazer thaw-margin passage.
- Migration: Rime-antler grazers follow receding snowline; predators follow at a separate lower-density band.
- Recovery: Reopen a marked corridor and wait a season step; frost casting cannot mint preserved food.
- Human response: Insulated stone shells surround blue-white ice vaults, with narrow vestibules and warm public rooms. Outer ice is replaceable environmental architecture; manuscript rooms and occupied structures need credible humidity and thermal separation.
- Oath boundary: Persistent cold gives way to brief melt pulses, whiteouts and crevasse shifts. Slowing zones are covenant anomalies with marked boundaries, separate from ordinary hypothermia or the Time Oath's causality questions.

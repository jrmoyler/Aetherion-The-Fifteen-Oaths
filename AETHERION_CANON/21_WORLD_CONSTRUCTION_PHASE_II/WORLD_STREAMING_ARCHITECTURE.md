## 2. Invariants and responsibilities

- One connected continent, fifteen existing regions and Oaths, fifteen preserved capitals, 63 existing POIs, 30 existing land adjacencies, four existing drainage basins. Cell boundaries are technical partitions, never new territories.
- Region membership is semantic and can overlap a cell. A river or mixed-border settlement can reference several regions without duplicating entity ownership. One authoritative owner per mutable entity at any instant.
- Essential routes and service anchors remain reachable without binding an Oath. Weather, sieges, phasing and streaming failures cannot remove protected civic access.
- Physical terrain, hydrology and ordinary climate are primary. Bounded Oath exception volumes record owner, entry cue, effect, duration, recovery and exit; they do not rewrite the continent globally.
- Presentation residency, simulation interest and persistence ownership are distinct. Unloading a mesh cannot despawn a debt, questgiver, inventory or event.
- No creature, environmental asset, network server, generated terrain, working world or GPU pass is delivered by these documents.

Proposed responsibilities: content release owns immutable bundle versions and compatible schema sets; world authority owns logical position/collision/event eligibility; regional simulation owns coarse ecology/routines; client streaming owns residency and quality; persistence owns durable transactions and recovery. They may initially be modules in one process. This is not a requirement to deploy microservices.

## 3. Coordinates, terrain and hydrology

### Coordinate contract

Author measured assets in meters. Use right-handed planning coordinates with +Y up, +X east, -Z north, subject to explicit selected-engine import conversion. Store logical position as `(world_id, space_id, cell_x, cell_z, local_x_m, y_m, local_z_m)`. Cells are signed integers; local horizontal offsets normalize to `[0, cell_width_m)`, including negative positions. `space_id` distinguishes exterior and private/public interiors. A runtime origin is a transient render/physics offset, not identity or save data. Cell width and axis mapping live in a versioned world configuration, not hard-coded in entity IDs.

A **256 m cell** is a pilot hypothesis for comparing load granularity, not an approved continent grid. Test 128/256/512 m configurations against the same sample geometry before selecting one. Never convert `WORLD_REGION_GRAPH.json`'s 1120×960 editorial canvas into world meters: its north=-y display convention and arbitrary spacing are conceptual only. Elevation and channel-station targets likewise require future terrain survey validation. No cell assignment of existing capitals/POIs is approved by this plan.

Proposed render-origin rebase trigger: distance over 1,024 m from current origin; tune from precision measurements. Rebase at a synchronized simulation boundary; apply the same delta to cameras, physics proxies, active effects, trails, terrain, audio emitters and cached spatial queries. Keep logical server positions unchanged. Invalidate or transform pending query results by origin epoch. Imported Ability Matrix has no proven rebase contract: isolate its scene for initial tests; attach it to a rebased world only after validating every affected source coordinate/lifecycle path through an external adapter. Never patch the immutable source to conceal a failed test.

### Terrain authoring contract

A deterministic seed + generator version + approved landform masks produces a candidate height/material field, not canon. Geology, catchment divides and land connections constrain generation before biome dressing. Shared integer sample coordinates and common edge samples must generate identical seam heights/normals regardless of chunk load order. Bake authored roads, terrace cuts, capital pads and river beds as versioned overrides. Preserve old generated content revisions for save replay; regeneration is a migration, not an automatic update.

Heightfields suit broad ground; overhangs, caves, cliffs and built interiors require explicit meshes/volumes with their own collision and drainage relationships. Do not promise arbitrary voxel destruction. Maintain a coarse collision shell and landmark mesh independent of fine decoration. LOD transitions use shared edges/stitching or validated skirts with matching collision, not visible cracks hidden by fog.

Hydrology checks terrain-derived flow direction from each headwater to its intended outlet, no uphill channel segments without an authored mechanism, catchment balance, confluence continuity and floodplain refuges. Preserve Thawcourse, Choirwater, Dawnrun and Veilwater sequences from baseline `03_GEOGRAPHY/WORLD_GEOGRAPHY.md`. Karst loss must resolve through mapped groundwater/reemergence. Check ocean datum and tidal reach separately from river discharge. Runtime water graphics never define authoritative swim depth or flood damage.

## 4. Residency, readiness and failure-safe traversal

A proposed cell descriptor carries stable cell key, world/content revision, bounds, neighbor keys, dependency hashes, region references, coarse collision, nav revision, service anchors, spawn ownership and available quality levels. Strict planning records in `STREAMING_PROTOTYPE_CONTRACTS.json` specify test intent; they are not executable engine descriptors.

State sequence: UNREQUESTED → REQUESTED → IO_READY → DECODED → COLLISION_READY → GAMEPLAY_READY → PRESENTATION_READY → RETIRING → UNREQUESTED. FAILED and CANCELLED terminate a request generation. Gameplay-ready requires authority state, legal collision/nav and mandatory hazard/service cues, but may use coarse art. Presentation-ready additionally meets selected visual quality. A new content revision is prepared side by side and committed atomically; mixed revisions cannot enter gameplay.

Every request has cell key + content revision + monotonic request generation. Late completions cannot revive a retired cell. Shared assets use explicit leases/refcounts; eviction releases cell ownership once and disposes only at zero users. Keep pinned resources for local collision, active encounter, party handover, interior exit and protected service anchors. Under memory pressure evict optional distant art first; cancel speculative work, lower cosmetic density and fall back before admitting unsafe entry.

Prefetch from velocity, acceleration bound, possible turns and route graph, not camera heading alone. A pilot lower bound is `ready_distance >= maximum_travel_speed × worst_case_ready_latency + stopping_distance + safety_margin`; each term must be measured with collision/nav and required cues included. For illustration only, 25 m/s × 4 s + 30 m braking + 20 m margin = 150 m. This is not an approved mount speed or production latency. Teleports, rapid traversal, summons and fast mounts request destination readiness explicitly. If the destination misses readiness, hold at a legal staging anchor, maintain current collision and offer an accessible loading/retry indication; do not pretend seamlessness by allowing falls or invisible blockers in danger zones.

Use different enter/exit thresholds and minimum residency to prevent border oscillation. A pilot 20% outer-ring hysteresis is to be tuned, not enforced on editorial geography. Server migration/handover requires lease epoch and acknowledgement; if unavailable, keep one authority and separate client cells from server boundaries.

## 5. Rendering, visibility, lighting and environmental presentation

| Concern | Proposed construction rule | Required failure probe and acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| LOD and distant landmarks | Importance + screen-space error choose geometry/material/animation levels with hysteresis; preserve civic and regional silhouettes. Maintain low-cost distant skyline independent of close cells. | Repeated approach/reversal at day/night and low visibility: no crack, rapidly alternating LOD, lost landmark or unreadable gameplay cue. Capture transitions on nominated devices. |
| Instancing | Share compatible static geometry/material; retain separate entity/interaction IDs. Separate harvestable/destructible state from instance slot. Use bounds per cluster. | Delete/reorder/reuse instance slot and reload cell: interactions still resolve same entity; no inventory or collision identity follows the visual slot. |
| Occlusion | Use conservative bounds, portals for interiors and measured optional occlusion; expand bounds for animation/VFX. Occlusion affects rendering only, never authority. | Fast camera turns, open doors, thin walls and large mounts: no false invisibility of threat/exit; bypass stale occlusion result after rebase or layout change. |
| Lighting | Shared calibrated material stage; broad day/night key light, bounded local lights, explicit indoor exposure and probe strategy. Baked/dynamic mix decided per candidate engine. | Cross doorway and change time/weather while casting: cues retain contrast; no stale light leak or blinding exposure pulse. Record shader warm/cold costs. |
| Weather | Authority publishes category, seed, transition timing and permitted local modifiers; client synthesizes particles/clouds/wetness. Blend boundaries from shared forecast and hysteresis. | Different clients/quality tiers show same hazards and route state despite different rain counts; fog cannot hide required warning/exit. |
| Foliage | Habitat/slope/moisture masks before art scatter; seeded placement and exclusion masks for roads, sightlines, foundations and service anchors. Decorative blades are not persistent resources. | Repeated unload/reload/season change: no tree in bridge, no duplicate yields, no navigation blocked by decorative grass; canopy/alpha costs measured. |
| Water | Distinct ocean/river/flood/wetness layers, matched shore transitions and bounds; authority owns swim/flood volumes and safe banks. Cosmetic surface motion does not change hit logic. | Seam crossing, tide/flood phase, underwater camera, low quality and delayed water art: collision/depth and refuge remain coherent. No upstream salinity without stated cause. |
| Interior portals | Separate space IDs; preload door vestibule and pinned legal exit; external shell persists independently. Door state and authority use stable portal ID. | Open/close rapidly, disconnect inside, evict exterior, change owner permissions: return to safe public anchor without inventory/quest loss; no entry to unauthorized private housing. |

Source VFX fidelity is checked independently from environmental cost reduction. Never silently substitute a new effect for an imported castable and call it parity. Optional cosmetic degradation must preserve anticipation, boundary, timing, target identity and accessibility feedback. See baseline `17_TECHNICAL_ART/PROPOSED_BUDGETS.json`; its budgets are hypotheses, not a license to claim performance here.

## 6. Population, navigation, mounts and destruction

Near-field NPCs use full authored actors; mid-field actors can use reduced animation/decision frequency; far-field life is aggregate schedule/population state. Named quest/service NPCs retain stable identity and authoritative location even offscreen. Materialization consumes a population/spawn lease; retirement returns it once. Seeded cosmetic crowds may be nonpersistent, but must be clearly separate from tradable/reward-bearing actors. Time skip/catch-up is bounded and cannot mint repeated wages, encounters or harvest yields. A player returning before a caravan's travel window ends must not observe it at two towns.

Navigation derives from gameplay collision. Build separate clearance profiles for pedestrian, assisted access, pack animal, large mount/vehicle and approved aquatic/air travel. Store height, width, radius, slope, step, turn radius, braking and traversal permissions as profile data; do not assume humanoid nav works for a mount. Candidate numeric profiles require actual approved body plans. Connect cell nav via versioned boundary links with matching endpoints and clearance; routes are valid only when all required segments/revisions are resident or certified coarse corridors exist. Ordinary walking/pack routes exist on every baseline adjacency; special traversal is optional. Essential vertical sites get grounded/mechanical alternatives. Route planning verifies both primary and service detour, including all authored event states.

Path failure holds or replans at a legal safe node; never teleport through locked doors or user property. Agent avoidance must not deadlock a doorway permanently; reserve passage for evacuation/service traffic. Mounting/dismounting, morph expansion and rider ejection require clearance probes and legal fallback pose. Narrow capital passages may require legal dismount/storage before entry, with retrievable mount identity and no item destruction.

Destructibility is a whitelist of authored states: intact → damaged → restricted/rerouted → reconstruction → restored or approved visual variant. Arbitrary whole-continent deformation remains unapproved. A transition prepares mesh, collision, nav and service detour; authority commits one revision only after validating connectivity. Debris is capped cosmetic content unless explicitly interactive. If collision/nav build fails, keep the previous safe state and report a blocked transition. Rewards and material contributions commit exactly once through journal/ledger; reset cannot duplicate them. Public service and housing access have protected floors across all states. Visual collapse does not decide territory ownership or permanent loss.

## 7. Persistence, multiplayer and save compatibility

Durable records store stable entity/space IDs, content/schema version, authority revision, logical position, authored destruction/event stage, quest/service state, owner-scoped inventories and journal offset. Do not save pointers, GPU buffers, renderer origins or instance slots. Snapshot plus ordered journal tail reconstructs state; transactions carry idempotency key and expected revision. Write intent/receipt atomically with consequential state or use a tested recovery protocol. Crash before/after each boundary must result in one committed outcome or no commit, never partial goods/service changes.

Dedicated authority, host topology, offline support, sharding and maximum population are decisions, not implementations. Prototype multiplayer uses one authority owning resolution and accepted events, clients sending intents, and presentation derived from seeds. Interest sets include nearby cells, predicted destinations, encounter dependencies and party relevance; do not publish private housing or unrelated quest state. Quantize logical coordinates only with measured hit/clearance error bounds. A 30 Hz authority / 10 Hz snapshot experiment may reuse the baseline budget fixture, but shipping rates remain undecided. Test reorder, duplicate, loss, delay and reconnect; sequence/epoch/content revision reject stale outcomes. Client clock or visual cell readiness cannot authorize damage.

A future server handover uses source lease, transfer snapshot/journal fence, target acknowledgement and one activation epoch. Until it passes split-brain and crash tests, use one owner. Network interest/cell boundaries are not automatic sharding boundaries. Offline/cloud save conflict policy requires review; never infer silent last-write-wins for inventory, ownership or irreversible choices.

Schema changes require explicit old→new transform, alias map for renamed identifiers, dependency resolution, validation and recovery backup. Unknown/newer schema returns a readable refusal and preserves the original. Removed content resolves to safe placeholders retaining identity/value until reviewed migration. Restore player to nearest approved safe anchor if terrain changes invalidate placement, recording why. Cross-version multiplayer is rejected unless an explicit compatibility matrix passes. A readable JSON file is not a tested save system.

## 8. Cross-platform evidence and prototype exit

Select nominated device/OS/GPU/browser or native-build matrix before calling any tier supported. Record build/engine/content revisions, input mode, viewport, graphics API, driver, CPU/GPU timings, p50/p95/p99 frame/stream latency, resident/peak memory, bandwidth, thermal behavior, shader errors and accessibility captures. Cold cache, warm cache, constrained bandwidth, suspend/resume and storage-full cases are separate tests. Keyboard/mouse, controller and touch are obligations only where selected, not assumed supported. Low tiers retain collision, event semantics, service access and telegraph readability; optional rain, distant animation, shadows and vegetation density scale first.

Prototype exit requires ALL of: approved engine/device decision; verified terrain seams/hydrology; safe high-speed cell/interior traversal; origin-rebase parity; no duplicate entities/resources; readable quality fallbacks; no lost state under crash/reconnect; authority/interest correctness; sustained measured budgets; source-adapter regressions; human art/accessibility review. Until evidence exists, every test in `STREAMING_PROTOTYPE_CONTRACTS.json` remains SPECIFIED_NOT_EXECUTED.

## 9. Coordinated Phase II interfaces

`WORLD_ECOLOGY_SYSTEM.md`, `DYNAMIC_WEATHER_SYSTEM.md`, `NPC_CIVILIZATION_SYSTEM.md`, `REGIONAL_ECOLOGY_PROFILES.json` and `WORLD_EVENT_REGISTRY.json` own living-world detail. Their proposed integer monotonic `world_tick` advances once per authoritative server second; 1-second event phase checks and 60-second weather/ecology aggregate buckets are scheduling layers, not a replacement for the illustrative 30 Hz near-field simulation / 10 Hz replication fixture above. Near-interaction objective checks may cap at 10 Hz. No per-frame ecology update is required. The proposed maxima of 64 active ecological actors per cell, 8 habitats × 8 population bands per region, 24 full NPCs plus 96 cosmetic crowd per cell, 8 path requests/second/cell and 4 detailed caravans are subsystem budget experiments, not validated capacity. They never override tighter global streaming or simulation limits, and shared costs must be profiled together. `event_instance_id + phase_revision` joins journal idempotency; stale danger phases skip to safe reconstruction on recovery, and personal narrative remains separate. Implementation must explicitly convert between this one-second world tick and any faster fixed simulation step; a bucket must not use client wall-clock time.

`ARCHITECTURAL_KIT_REGISTRY.json`, `ENVIRONMENT_ASSET_REGISTRY.json`, `ENVIRONMENT_REFERENCE_EVIDENCE.json` and `ASSET_CONSTRUCTION_HANDBOOK.md` own construction/provenance detail. Their proposed bundle fields `{asset_id, revision, source_hash, mesh, materials, collision, nav, hazard_cues, states}` align with the critical-first residency contract. A reference image's `source_hash` proves source identity, not that it is a runtime mesh/texture or cleared production asset. Distinguish the content-bundle hash from every original-reference hash and retain both. Structural kit IDs and state variants remain registry-owned; streaming does not manufacture replacement assets.

The `p2-streaming-09-*` planning-record IDs use region-09 as their proposed pilot anchor only; every record explicitly applies to all fifteen regions. They refine baseline contracts and do not add new territories or claim new working services. Registry acceptance remains SPECIFIED_NOT_EXECUTED even if its JSON passes validation.

## Refinement boundaries

- Geography adds an irregular conceptual continental composition, physical processes, cross-border transitions, channel-height targets and ordinary-route detours. Canvas anchors and target heights are planning aids, not survey coordinates, measured gradients or final map approval.
- Region and capital blueprints refine the established dossiers into functional settlement, civic-space and traversal plans. New location IDs must not silently rename baseline POIs. Shared techniques do not excuse identical civilizations with different colors.
- Living-world proposals constrain weather, ecology, event state and civilian routines through bounded, recoverable authority-owned changes. Far-field approximations must conserve meaningful resources and identity. Cosmetic crowds and weather particles do not constitute economy or climate simulation.
- Environment registries specify production requirements and dependencies. Existing REF identifiers denote reference evidence; they do not denote export-ready meshes, collision, rigs, materials, commercial licenses or finished panorama-to-module conversion.
- Technical construction preserves the existing Ability Matrix harness as a comparison source. Its renderer does not select the full-world engine. New streaming, persistence and replication contracts require a future explicitly authorized implementation task.

## Outstanding decisions and evidence gates

1. **Founder review:** continental silhouette, exact settlement additions, capital organization, distinctive architecture, event content and production prioritization need review. This package makes proposals inspectable; it cannot lock canon by itself.
2. **Terrain and hydrology:** build an authored heightfield/mesh, test seams, flow direction, basin divides, river confluences, groundwater reemergence, tidal reach, flood refuge and catchment balance. Strictly descending target stations prove only their numerical ordering.
3. **Traversal and service access:** instantiate walking, assisted-access, pack, mount and vehicle clearance profiles; verify slopes, bridges, doors, detours and event-state navigation on actual collision. Graph connectivity alone does not prove playable traversal or acceptable travel time.
4. **Visual production:** convert reference-informed designs into reviewed orthographic plans, modules, interiors, material definitions and actual assets. Resolve every explicitly missing view or licensing/provenance gate before production use. Recovered images are not production assets.
5. **Systems implementation:** test weather/ecology approximation stability, caravan and NPC identity, market conservation, event arbitration, recovery and reward idempotency. Run outage/reconnect/reset cases with real authority and persistence.
6. **Engine and platform:** select engine candidates and nominated devices, then measure CPU/GPU, memory, streaming latency, shader compilation, bandwidth, thermals and readability. Numeric budgets in this package are targets, never observed performance.
7. **Persistence and multiplayer:** prove journal recovery, save migration, version compatibility, privacy scope and single-ownership handover. A JSON schema or readable save fixture cannot establish crash safety or anti-duplication.
8. **Licensing and integration authorization:** continue the baseline commercial provenance gate. No repository merge, push, deployment, dependency installation, engine selection, source rewrite or resource commitment is authorized by delivery of this planning package.

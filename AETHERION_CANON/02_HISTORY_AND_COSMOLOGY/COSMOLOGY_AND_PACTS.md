# Cosmology and pact mechanics

## Evidence boundary

[LOCKED; SRC-USER-DIRECTIVE] The Weave is the primordial structure holding reality together; the First Fracture damaged it; fifteen living Oaths arose and stabilize foundational forces while claiming allegiance. The original fragment permits one soul to hold multiple Oaths. No approved source establishes a creator deity, other plane, sixteenth force, exact age of the world or final cause of the Fracture.

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] The model below is a coherent design interpretation, not an extension of LOCKED status. Its purpose is to make narrative choices, civic infrastructure and bounded gameplay explain one another without pretending metaphors are implemented physics.

## A layered model

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] The Weave holds relationships among matter, perception, motion and living agency. The forces existed before formal Oaths; covenants are adaptive interfaces created after the damage, not proof that fire or water first appeared then. A covenant has a stable core duty and revisable interpretations. Its living quality means it responds to maintained relationships and accumulated practice; whether it is a conscious being remains unresolved.

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] An institution can maintain a covenant through distributed civic acts, while a personally bound practitioner enters a more direct relationship. This explains why a city relies on an Oath even though most residents are not combatants. Institutional collapse threatens service continuity, but killing one leader cannot instantly delete the force or every citizen's abilities.

## Cost is a relationship, not arbitrary punishment

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] A powerful act has an opportunity, maintenance demand, exposure, political obligation or environmental consequence appropriate to its context. Thermal work requires heat management; preservation postpones a decision and creates custody duties; concealment protects information but makes independent accountability harder. Costs should be legible before meaningful commitment and reparable when the fiction permits.

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Do not imply conservation equations the runtime does not simulate. A water effect may use an authored visual and bounded status change while the story describes local hydraulic stewardship. Technical contracts determine exactly which entities, volumes and durations exist; lore cannot silently enlarge a castable's range or persistence.

## Personal pact lifecycle

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Discovery teaches a practice; initiation establishes a consented relationship; mastery demonstrates reliable use and service; tension identifies incompatible obligations; reconciliation renegotiates specific terms. Breaking a pact changes trust and access according to disclosed terms, but cannot justify arbitrary deletion of unrelated player inventory or irreversible damage hidden behind a flavor choice.

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] An initiation has a civilian equivalent contribution and never requires an incompatible moral choice as the only path to basic play. Binding an Oath does not grant ownership of its realm. Leaving an institution need not erase learned knowledge; whether a particular technique remains available is a systems decision marked separately from narrative allegiance.

## The original fragment

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] The fragment preserves an interface pattern older than the current exclusive claims. It keeps covenant signatures distinguishable within one bearer, allowing negotiation and switching without immediate forced assimilation. Its limitation is mediation: it cannot supply everyone's consent, solve maintenance shortages or make contradictory obligations simultaneously fulfilled.

[UNRESOLVED; SRC-USER-DIRECTIVE; SRC-RECOVERED-LORE] The original source's plural Oath fragments and current directive's singular original Weave fragment need an explicit approved interpretation. Proposed resolution: derivative covenant tokens register pacts through one original interface. Alternatives are literal independent fragments or purely metaphysical marks; none is silently canonized here.

## Unbound terminology

[LOCKED; SRC-USER-DIRECTIVE] The player begins Unbound, while the Archivist is the last true Unbound and names the player successor.

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Unbound at character creation means unclaimed by a chosen personal Oath. True Unbound is a demonstrated rare capacity to maintain self-directed access at covenant boundaries. The Archivist is the last known verified bearer before death; the successor gains the means, but must still learn to use it. This preserves both statements without inventing a bloodline, secret prior initiation or predestined morality.

[UNRESOLVED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Approval must decide whether true Unbound is primarily an ontological state, learned mastery or institutional designation. Dialogue must preserve ambiguity until that decision is made; capitalization alone is not sufficient explanation to players.

## Two meanings of Tidal

[LOCKED; SRC-USER-DIRECTIVE] TIDAL COVENANT is Water; TIDAL OBSERVATORY is Cosmic & Gravity. They are distinct institutions with distinct realms and gameplay identities.

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Both inherited an old navigational word for periodic exchange. Coastal stewards retained its meaning in water's rise, fall and circulation; celestial observers retained its meaning in attraction across distance. A proposed historical scholarly exchange explains shared vocabulary, not shared ownership, merged powers or a hidden sixteenth predecessor Oath.

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Water uses open channels, shell-lime, rope, circulation and human work rhythms. Cosmic uses balanced masses, starstone, orbital instruments and measured metallic tones. Shared navigation trade gives them reason to cooperate and to dispute model versus local observation, while each remains culturally and mechanically independent.

## Time, Frost, illusion and absence

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Frost changes preservation and local rates within an authored effect; Time governs motion windows and local temporal anomalies. Neither automatically rewrites historical consent. Illusion changes perception within a disclosed scope; Shadow manages concealment and absence traces. Neither can rewrite authoritative game state merely because a character claims a new truth.

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Memory interventions require consent and preserve uncertainty. Evidence can be incomplete or manipulated, but the story must establish independent anchors before asking players to make irreversible judgments. A late reveal cannot invalidate every prior choice by announcing that all testimony, interfaces and consequences were imaginary.

## Death, identity and the ending boundary

[LOCKED; SRC-USER-DIRECTIVE] The Archivist dies in the opening. [PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] That death is not undone by a convenient Time or Blood technique. Recovered echoes, preserved testimony or simulations may provide partial information, explicitly identified as such, without restoring a living infallible guide.

[UNRESOLVED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Wider resurrection, afterlife, playable species and soul ontology are not established. Combat defeat and multiplayer respawn are game-system questions awaiting approved treatment; this package does not invent a complete theology to justify them.

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Endings change the governance of covenant access in the player's narrative timeline. Restoration revises relationships, Dominion concentrates authority and Liberation withdraws compulsory claims while replacing essential services. None deletes a fundamental force from existence or creates a magically costless society.

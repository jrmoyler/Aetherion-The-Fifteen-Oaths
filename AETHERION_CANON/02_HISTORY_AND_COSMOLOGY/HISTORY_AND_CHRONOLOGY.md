They are not recovered dates, not an approved calendar and not evidence of the Archivist's lifespan. A production decision may replace them consistently before dialogue or localization locks.

## Before the Fracture

[UNRESOLVED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Approved source does not describe pre-Fracture governments, species, gods, continents or technology. The world bible intentionally avoids naming an ancient empire to fill that gap. Ruins can show different construction phases without proving one unified lost civilization or a morally superior golden age.

## Survival becomes institution

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] The founding period overlaps across regions rather than presenting a numbered sequence of elemental discoveries. Communities stabilize services they urgently need: safe heat, stored knowledge, messages, water, foundations, warnings, food, treatment, shelter, navigation, refuge, mutual care, shared imagination, transport and tools. The same practices that save lives later become claims over access and conduct.

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Territorial recognition follows practical settlement, not ethnic separation. Migrants carry techniques, families cross borders and civic contracts overlap. Fifteen major realms do not imply fifteen pure populations or fifteen completely self-contained economies.

## Integration and dependency

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Pass charters, relief agreements and expanding services create a connected continent. Each improvement has a second effect: a relay increases access and creates a routing authority; a shared root system feeds people and makes exit difficult; an antidote protects communities and can become a monopoly. History should show decisions and material constraints, not a universal fall from innocent founders to evil descendants.

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Conflict includes strikes, legal disputes, migration, sanctions, floods, failed experiments and limited military campaigns. A world at perpetual total war could not sustain its elaborate civilian cultures. No additional named war, dynasty or disaster is canonized here without a specific narrative need and approval.

## The Archivist's investigation

[LOCKED; SRC-USER-DIRECTIVE] The Archivist belongs to the Rime Archive, is the last true Unbound and possesses the original fragment before the opening death. [PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Their investigation compared incompatible covenant claims and sought a successor able to choose rather than merely inherit a program. Why this player was selected, how the fragment was found and who caused the death remain open mysteries, not secretly settled facts.

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Regional evidence reveals the institutions' subsequent choices more reliably than the Fracture's ultimate cause. A preserved ledger can prove a relief debt was extended; it cannot prove every cosmological theory in the same vault. This distinction lets investigation produce meaningful certainty without prematurely closing the world's deepest mystery.

## Player era and branch history

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] The opening is fixed, regional arc order is not. Later scenes refer to actual completed settlements and evidence categories, avoiding dialogue that assumes Water was learned before Frost or that one leader always betrayed the player. Betrayal requires a documented motive, warning and triggering player commitment.

[PROPOSED; SRC-WORLD-PROPOSAL] Final choices create branch-specific historical records in a personal or declared party narrative scope. Shared-world event history remains separately recoverable. Post-ending accounts include dissent, implementation costs and people still doing maintenance; the credits do not imply that one decision removed politics from the world.

## Prioritized findings

### DV-01 — HIGH_ART_FIDELITY

The characteristic pointed fabric-and-bronze canopy/arcade silhouette is not matched. Near-planar fabric, zigzag wire-like ties and pale knob capitals replace layered ribs, sag/tension geometry, central pendant and decorated pier heads.

**Next:** Rework the primary canopy and pier silhouettes from source105 plus proposed colonnade/roof sheets before multiplying bays; render matched front, side and underside views.

### DV-02 — HIGH_ART_FIDELITY

Large plain rectangles and sparse trim make the wall/threshold family much less specific than the references. Door/window seat relief, crowned head motifs, pilaster bases/caps and bronze framed sills are missing.

**Next:** Author continuous trim profiles and corner termination pieces; demonstrate one L-corner, one seated window and one seated clinic frame at readable front/section angles.

### DV-03 — HIGH_INTERFACE_VISUAL_GAP

Both assembled landing fixtures terminate rails at the flight/run and leave side/rear landing edges visibly unguarded. The references show guarded landings with returns; side infill and substantial post bases/caps also differ.

**Next:** Build a guarded landing join fixture with intentional exit opening, consistent rail transitions and source-like side treatment; technical controller/collision/accessibility acceptance remains separate.

### DV-04 — MEDIUM_ART_FIDELITY

Reference sunburst inlays, layered bearing blocks, differentiated borders and structural/decorative material divisions are reduced to plain slabs, thin lines and a central polygon.

**Next:** Restore recognizable radial/diamond layout and stepped bronze/stone interfaces at low polygon cost; inspect paired floor bays and roof/pier seats in match-lit closeups.

### DV-05 — MEDIUM_MATERIAL_FIDELITY

Pale marble and bronze are consistent, but marble reads as sparse smooth wave lines, bronze as broad brown strips, and masonry as uniformly noisy edge bands. The material sheet shows branching mineral veins, brushed highlights, restrained localized wear and explicit facing/substrate separation. Current noisy shadow renders limit finer assessment.

**Next:** Refine texture scale/variation and material response with source-matched neutral closeups; improve sample quality where available without claiming an unavailable denoiser or GPU pass.

### DV-06 — UNRESOLVED_MOVING_INTERFACE

Three closed panels are evident, but only two are clearly exposed in the open hero. Independent technical checker reports three synchronized pivots; this review does not infer a missing blade. Decorative pivots/service cover/manual release are not modeled to the proposed sheet.

**Next:** Render orthographic side/front open states and inspect swept contacts against fixed rails; retain zero physical motion-clearance approval until measured.

## What the fixtures establish

The civic, residential and work arrangements demonstrate a shared module vocabulary, readable pale/bronze palette and candidate adjacency. The clinic frame in civic is free-standing, so it does not prove frame-to-wall seating. The residential wall makes the window opening visible but does not expose a fully detailed inside/outside corner joint. Stair/ramp landings look connected in perspective; their unguarded exposed edges remain a visible discrepancy. Technical clearance and fit measurements are the separate verifier’s responsibility.

The final baffle review retains a specific uncertainty: three panels are visible closed; only two are clear in the open perspective. The technical verifier independently reports three animated pivots. This is a visibility/clearance question requiring better diagnostic views, not evidence of an absent third panel.

No candidate or repository files were modified. This review is useful for repair prioritization, not approval of geometry, engineering, accessibility, gameplay or engine parity.

# Independent Fault V13 review

**Concrete local facet/depth improvement; overall result remains mixed and unaccepted.**

Compared with V12, the crystal has clearer angular faces/highlights and the stone has more articulated relief and overlaps. This improves the earlier flat-strip reading. However, it remains one long connected channel between large tile-like stone banks; profile still exposes long near-parallel substrate edges. The source fracture hierarchy and changing crystal exposure are not yet matched.

The bulky cuff guard, regular flanges, long prism pommel and repeating broad grip wrap remain generic compared with original053's fitted irregular bronze/stone enclosure, compact crystal cluster and layered leather. More small facets alone do not fix these major silhouettes.

Original053, actual final front/profile and V12/V13 comparison inspected individually. All 48 final manifest records match, totaling 19,632,456 bytes; all 63 predecessor V12 records also match. No frozen files changed or publication attempted. This is a two-view visual review, not GPU optics, gameplay, topology, collision or production approval.

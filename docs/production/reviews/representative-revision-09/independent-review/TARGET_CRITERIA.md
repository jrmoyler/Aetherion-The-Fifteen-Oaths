# V16 left ceramic bracer — independent target criteria

Actual original reference090 and V15 walk25 body pixels inspected. Character LEFT means viewer-right in the front view. This is a bounded bracer repair, not character acceptance.

- Replace the thin uniform ladder impression with one continuous thick terracotta plate: broad side margins, substantial closed top/bottom, rounded/beveled outer corners and mild forearm-conforming curvature/taper.
- Three central horizontal rounded-rectangle slots should have short dark recess walls/backing. Reference slots are shorter than plate width and do not read as open space directly to the sleeve.
- Distinguish the iron side spine/hinge from ceramic, with a short crosswise latch plate/pin and upper/lower dark straps wrapping the arm. Avoid oversized ornamental latch hardware or glossy metal unrelated to worn reference iron.
- Preserve forearm fit, wrist mobility/clearance and left/right equipment asymmetry. No acceptance of floating attachment, sleeve intersections or wrist clipping introduced by added thickness.
- Fired ceramic should read matte, porous and edge-worn; material noise alone cannot repair missing plate mass, perimeter or slot geometry.

Required review evidence: same-camera/light V15/V16 body or arm front/oblique plus side-latch closeup. Judge normal-distance silhouette first, then slot depth and hardware. Reference illustration is not a calibrated construction drawing; no exact dimensions or fit certification follow from it.

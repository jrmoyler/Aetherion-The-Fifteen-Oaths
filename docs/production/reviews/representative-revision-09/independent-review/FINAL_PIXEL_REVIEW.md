# Ninja V16 left bracer — independent pixel review

**Verdict: concrete scoped geometry and source-likeness improvement over V15; unapproved.** This is more than a material-only or near-neutral change. It does not establish exact art matching or whole-character acceptance.

I inspected original reference090 and all eight actual V15/V16 comparison pairs: front, back, side, hero, bracer front, bracer near, bracer far and cast. The 16 actual render hashes match `render-evidence.json`; the eight pairs have matching recorded camera, target, scale, floor, clip and frame parameters. Exact inspected image/receipt hashes are in `final-pixel-review.json`. These hashes pin this visual judgment; they are not an independent geometry/rig/animation-preservation test.

The former literal ladder is replaced by a coherent thick ceramic plate, with closed ends, broad side margins and three recessed rounded rectangular apertures. The change reads at normal body distance, including the front and hero views. The final endcaps no longer show the early broad diagonal folds. Final latch tabs sit beyond the aperture interiors; visible hinge knuckles and through-pin silhouettes make the attachment more recognizable than the earlier plain tabs. This is a worthwhile bounded construction improvement against the source plate/latch motif.

The far view exposes broad, coarse retention bands and a lower band edge standing proud of the sleeve, reading more as a stiff collar than a snug strap. That remains a fit/finish deficiency; the inspected pixels do not demonstrate an actual new harmful intersection. The cast view shows the plate following the forearm with visible wrist separation, and I find no obvious new wrist clipping or gross silhouette regression in these views. This is limited visual evidence, not all-frame collision or deformation certification.

Remaining source differences include simplified aperture spacing/proportions, generic latch/side-spine organization, fairly uniform ceramic pitting, weak worn-edge definition and simplified iron finish. True strap compression, attachment mechanics and wrist movement require further evidence. All pre-existing whole-character face/hair/mask/cape/chest/boot and equipment gaps remain. The narrow bracer improvement must not be represented as a complete likeness pass.

No repository or frozen candidate files were modified. No image generation, publication, approval or production promotion occurred. Independent technical preservation/import/grounding checks remain a separate gate owned by the technical reviewer.

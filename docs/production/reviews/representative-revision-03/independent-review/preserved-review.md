# Independent preserved-candidate review

**Result: neither candidate matches the reference sufficiently for final art acceptance.** Both remain review-only.

Viewed original 034 and 049 pixels and all 10 current candidate renders; compared Chronal v4 front, Graft pass05 front and pass06 planar diagnostic profile. Read-only source review; no repository edits. SHA256 verification is file identity evidence only.

## Chronal wrist V6

Brass hub distribution and hooked blade silhouettes improve on V4. The flat rectangular bridge, wide blade roots and bulky cuff still obscure the compact layered mechanism shown in original 034. Prioritize carrier and blade outlines, bearing recesses and glove/strap geometry; then refine aged brass and brushed steel under fixed lighting. The neutral wrist fixture does not establish actual character or moving-blade clearance.

## Graft Sapseed pass06

Long split husks, thinner sprigs and added profile depth improve on pass05 and the planar diagnostic. The crown remains too regular and open, the orange core too smooth and exposed, and bark/branching too uniform. Prioritize uneven overlapping husks, interrupted twisted wood masses and source branch junctions, then localized bark relief, moss and leaf clusters. Held and docked contact views remain absent.

Fault Choir — Amethyst Short Blade V9

Rebuilt overlapping fracture planes, widened the exposed crystal channel, narrowed guard supports and corrected the authored stone, bronze and leather values.

Remaining: Stone divisions and bevels remain too regular, the crystal channel too uniform and the guard/pommel too simplified. The suspected data-texture gamma defect was disproved by byte-level comparison.

Editable sources: source.blend, fault_weapons.review.py, review.glb

Impulse folding shield revision07

Rebuilt telescopic fork supports, travel slots, hinge connections and folding sequence. All 721 sampled quarter-frame states have no intergroup triangle-surface intersections.

Remaining: Flat casing and uniform surfaces remain below reference detail. Hidden mechanism is proposed; stowed depth is 0.227 m and maximum sampled movement depth is 0.554 m including straps. Wearer fit, continuous clearance and mechanical strength are unverified.

Editable sources: impulse-shield-lateral-review07.blend, impulse-shield-lateral-review07.glb, reproduce.py

Chronal wrist tool V6 — preserved revision

Compact brass bearing and hooked crescent outlines replace the broad dark hub and triangular blade forms. Includes a separate neutral wrist-fit witness.

Remaining: Broad flat bridge and blade roots, coarse bright brass and bulky cuff remain visibly unlike the reference. The fixture does not establish character fit or moving-blade clearance.

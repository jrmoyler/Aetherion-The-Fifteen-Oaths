## Approval and evidence

New design remains PROPOSED. Source code or asset presence alone does not establish production validation. Original reference images retain their identity and uncertainty; no generated replacement art is presented as recovered evidence. Authoritative action permissions, persistence, recovery, accessibility and localization are explicit engineering requirements.
